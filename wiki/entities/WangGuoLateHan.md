# 王国 / Wang Guo (late Han)

[[WangGuoLateHan|王国]] is the late-Han rebel leader whose failed siege of [[ChenCang|陈仓]] anchors the military half of [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-942-yue-youxiu-de-ren-huode-yue-lei-heli-ma-lkwlrcgnt-kxxc2qqz6z6wkg7e5z|Hanji 942]]. His army presses Chen Cang for more than eighty days, but [[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] judges that the fortified city can hold and waits for the besiegers to exhaust themselves.

When Wang Guo withdraws in spring 189, Huangfu Song pursues the tired and disordered army, defeating it and killing more than ten thousand men in the episode's account. The defeat triggers a leadership crisis on the rebel side: Wang Guo is removed, [[HanSui|韩遂]] coerces [[YanZhongLateHan|阎忠]] into taking nominal command, and the coalition weakens through internal conflict.
