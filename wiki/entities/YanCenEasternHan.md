## Overview
Yan Cen is the Shu commander entrusted with Gongsun Shu's army after the final Chengdu battle and the figure whose surrender of the city is followed by Wu Han's punitive clan destruction.

## Current Profile
In [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-713-liuxiu-nu-le-mingjiang-wuhan-jing-xuexi-chengdu-lv6555l99it33gw34uydsz4tkbje|Hanji 713]], Yan Cen first appears as Gongsun Shu's battlefield subordinate, sent against Zhang Gong while Gongsun Shu attacks Wu Han. His fight initially goes well, but Wu Han's reinforcement breaks the broader Shu position and Gongsun Shu is fatally wounded.

After Gongsun Shu entrusts the army to him, Yan Cen offers Chengdu in surrender the next day. The episode's judgment turns on what follows: Wu Han does not treat the surrender as a protection bargain but destroys Yan Cen's clan while killing Gongsun Shu's household and allowing a sack of Chengdu.

## Key Characteristics
- Final entrusted commander in Gongsun Shu's Chengdu regime.
- Battlefield officer who fights Zhang Gong during the last Chengdu engagement.
- Surrender broker who offers Chengdu after Gongsun Shu's mortal wound.
- Victim of post-surrender collective punishment through Wu Han's destruction of his clan.
- Evidence point for the fragility of surrender when the receiver disregards protection norms.
