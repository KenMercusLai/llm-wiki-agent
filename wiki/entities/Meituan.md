# Meituan

## Overview
Meituan is a Chinese local-services platform discussed across delivery, instant retail, foodservice formats, organizational design and possible agent interfaces. The registered episodes cover distinct businesses and outside comparisons, not a full audited corporate profile.

## Current Profile
The strongest common thread is reuse of traffic, merchant networks, transaction incentives and delivery/fulfillment infrastructure across local categories; later AI service-entry and OTA challenges are proposals or competitive possibilities.

## Key Characteristics
- Food delivery, medicine ordering and grocery formats extend local fulfillment into different categories.
- Coupons, membership and cross-selling can convert app traffic into repeat local transactions.
- Platform-led foodservice and organizational coordination are specific operational interpretations.
- OTA competition and AI-mediated ordering remain prospective rather than confirmed company rollouts.

## Evidence
- **Local fulfillment:** The healthcare history uses Meituan pharmacy delivery as a [[PharmaceuticalEcommerce]] and [[HealthcareO2O]] instant-retail extension, not proof it solved diagnosis or [[InternetHealthcare|hospital services]].
