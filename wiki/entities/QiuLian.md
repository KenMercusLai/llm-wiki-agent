# 邱连 / Qiu Lian

## Overview
Qiu Lian is a Sanlian Life Weekly journalist and Talk三联 speaker appearing in episodes on manufacturing, fragrance, new specialty foods and regional dining.

## Current Profile
Her attributable role is reporting and discussion across distinct domains, not an overarching personal theory of factory management, scent culture, aquaculture and restaurants.

## Key Characteristics
- Her role spans field reporting on manufacturing.
- She covers consumption rituals and scent products.
- She discusses ingredients through production, not only taste.
- She brings regional flavor reporting to restaurant formats.

## Evidence
- **Factory reporting:** In a multi-speaker [[DeliGroup|得力]] visit, [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] reporter 邱连 explains [[FlexibleManufacturing|柔性制造]] through C-side demand signals, IP collaborations, scheduling, line-side materials, pen-tip precision and [[AIVisualQualityInspection|visual quality checks]], not a claim that she personally designed these systems. [[ep271-tanfang-rouxing-zhizao-gongchang-shi-shui-rang-ni-xiangyao-jiu-neng-maidao-lqyrioptggnbb8hl-llej0-5ow0v]]
- **Incense consumption:** She contrasts everyday [[LineIncenseDailyLife|线香]] with high-threshold historical 香席 and describes [[IncenseFormulaReconstruction|古方香复刻]], including “鹅梨帐中香,” as ways consumers imagine [[ChineseFragrance|中式香气]]. [[ep263-xianxiang-jueqi-faxian-zhongshi-xiangqi-de-meili-lq1lsqcprqyr7auo5gslwyymuiv]]
- **Food supply chains:** Her discussion of [[ChineseNewSpecialtyAquaculture|farmed high-value ingredients]] emphasizes production, species labeling, processing and consumer formats rather than merely a fashionable new dish. [[ep260-yikou-feimei-zhifang-cangzhe-zhongguo-yangzhi-de-xin-gushi-linjq2fkzhqvzjcgrmhudq12vs4j]]
- **Guizhou dining:** In [[Guizhou|贵州]] reporting she describes [[GuizhouCuisineFlavorTransfer|酸辣风味]]—chili, fermentation, 木姜子 and red sour soup—through [[OhaGuizhouBistro|欧哈]] and other restaurant cases; local rice-wine tasting sets and dishes built around grain, microbes and seasonings illustrate [[LocalAlcoholPairing|餐配]] within [[ChineseBistroLocalization|中式小酒馆]]. These are four reported domains, not a unified personal theory. [[ep245-yinian-yidu-xunwei-zhilv-jinnian-qu-chi-chuangyi-cai-li9wtavemokcckialbbj7unpmqsz]]

## Qualifications
These are episodes with other speakers; their domain conclusions are not all Qiu Lian’s personal views or experiences. The incense note cautions that health and market-size estimates are not systematically evidenced; the food note leaves foie-gras welfare unresolved.

## What Changed
- Her factory, fragrance, aquaculture and regional-food appearances are described as reporting roles rather than one personal doctrine.
