# 杨端和 / Yang Duanhe

杨端和 / Yang Duanhe enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-108-1-lishi-gan-dangmian-dingzhuang-qinshihuang-diyi-ren-lg0dssyicp387foiraohkv1x5aij]] as the Qin commander who continues [[MengAo|蒙骜]]'s pressure on [[WeiState|魏国]] in Qin Shi Huang's ninth year. The source frames his campaign less as a standalone biography than as the next step in [[QinEastwardPressure|秦国东进压力]].

The episode's strategic point is that Qin's advance through Wei pushes its eastern boundary to meet [[QiState|齐国]]. The host reads that geography as ending the remaining practical space for effective [[HezongAlliance|合纵]], because the eastern states can no longer assume a contiguous anti-Qin arrangement beyond Qin's reach.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-111-1-ruoda-de-zhaoguo-jing-wang-yu-yige-jinv-zhi-shou-lhrqb8-swlkuyoydgjyn8ldnlkzc]] keeps Yang Duanhe in the Qin expansion sequence. In 236 BCE, he is named with [[WangJianQin|王翦]] and [[HuanYiQin|桓齮]] as Qin attacks [[ZhaoState|赵国]] while Zhao is campaigning against [[YanState|燕国]]. The source makes him one part of Qin's ability to convert rival-state conflict into territorial pressure.
