# 耿鄙 / Geng Bi (late Han)

耿鄙 / Geng Bi (late Han) enters through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] as the Liangzhou inspector whose command collapses before the 187 CE western rebellion expands. The transcript renders him as "耿彼"; this page keeps the late-Han inspector identity but records the correction as source-scoped.

The episode uses Geng Bi less as a full biography than as an institutional failure marker. His subordinate [[MaTeng|马腾]] rises from low resources through military service, but after Geng Bi is betrayed and killed, Ma Teng continues into rebellion with [[HanSui|韩遂]] and [[WangGuoLateHan|王国]]. The source ties that move to frontier governance failure: the local official order has become intolerable for both Qiang-Hu groups and ordinary people.
