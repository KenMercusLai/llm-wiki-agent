# 颜聚 / Yan Ju (Zhao)

颜聚 / Yan Ju enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-113-limu-ruhe-yong-yiji-zhili-zu-qinguo-tongyi-luymqpcolsvyz5miodit3wdlx0ob]] as the second replacement commander appointed with [[ZhaoCong|赵葱]] after [[ZhaoState|赵国]] removes [[LiMu|李牧]] and [[SimaShang|司马尚]]. The filename uses YanJuZhao to avoid collision with unrelated modern entities named Yan.

The source treats Yan Ju as part of a command-trust failure rather than as a fully developed general. Once [[GuoKai|郭开]]'s accusation convinces [[ZhaoYoumiuWang|赵迁 / 赵幽缪王]], Zhao trades the field commanders Qin fears for replacements who inherit a shaken front.
