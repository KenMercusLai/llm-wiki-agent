# 郭开 / Guo Kai

郭开 / Guo Kai enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-104-yinmou-shisuan-rang-qinguo-bantu-you-you-you-pengzhang-le-lgnyhbduqutegtksrjwcvhmn8-t6]] as [[LianPo|廉颇]]'s enemy in [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. When [[ZhaoDaoxiangwang|赵道襄王]] considers recalling Lian Po from exile, Guo Kai bribes the investigating envoy.

The bribe changes the political meaning of Lian Po's display. Lian Po eats heavily, wears armor, and mounts a horse to prove he can still command, but the envoy reports bodily weakness instead. In the episode, Guo Kai therefore becomes the court actor who turns Zhao's possible recovery of a veteran general into another case of [[RumorErosionOfTrust|trust erosion]] and self-inflicted military loss.
