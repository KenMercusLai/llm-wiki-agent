# 朱亥 / Zhu Hai

朱亥 / Zhu Hai enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-5-lp6yfcxoysxgcnmg8v1uljfyptof]] as the butcher whom [[HouYing|侯嬴]] deliberately visits after [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] has personally driven to [[Daliang|大梁]]'s Yi Gate to receive him.

In this episode, Zhu Hai is less an independent actor than a pressure point in Hou Ying's test. Hou Ying asks Xinlingjun to detour to the market, talks with Zhu Hai for a long time, and quietly watches whether Xinlingjun becomes irritated while his high-status carriage waits in a low-status public setting.

The source stops before Zhu Hai's later famous role. For this page, his significance is source-scoped: he helps turn Xinlingjun's respect for Hou Ying into a public, observable act rather than a private invitation.
