## Overview
Coffee is a drink, globally traded crop and repeat-purchase category. These sources examine its social and commodity history, Chinese chains, physical experience, household spending, founder distribution, taste education and even a podcast's morning metaphor.

## Current Profile
Its uses depend on context: political public space and labor stimulant in a historical book reading; convenience, price and identity in contemporary retail; repeated ritual and sensory learning for individuals. An analogy about coffee cannot establish a market-wide commercial rule.

## Key Characteristics
- Coffee's historical diffusion made it a medium of wakefulness, public conversation and state concern.
- Commodity supply connects distant growers to monopoly attempts, speculation and price cycles.
- Chinese chain competition combines institutional capital, local operations and tension between premium status and daily affordability.
- Stores and consumer price ladders show coffee as both embodied experience and adjustable household expense.
- Founder cases show different ways to convert coffee-product discovery into repeated purchasing and channel proof.
- Tasting vocabulary and a morning-show metaphor show personal and symbolic uses distinct from production economics.

## Evidence
- **Public life:** A reading of [[QuanqiuShangyin|《全球上瘾》]] traces origin legends and Arab/Ottoman disputes through Vienna, Venice and Marseille to Paris adoption and [[CoffeehousePublicSphere|coffeehouses]] as places for talk, literature, criticism and surveillance anxiety. Its “black Apollo” contrast with alcohol associates wakefulness with rational sociability, later factories and military endurance—an interpretation of [[CaffeinatedModernity]], not proof that caffeine caused the Enlightenment. [[74-quanqiu-shangyin-a-kafei-wo-heise-de-apoluo-678615763]]
- **Commodity politics:** The same reading describes French licenses, port restrictions, fixed prices and premises searches, followed by 19th-century war-rumor speculation, [[Brazil|Brazilian]] harvest swings, overproduction, coffee burning and producer-protection measures: [[CoffeeCommodityPolitics]] creates [[CommodityPriceExposure]] across the chain. [[74-quanqiu-shangyin-a-kafei-wo-heise-de-apoluo-678615763]]
- **Retail configuration:** The 2026 investment podcast portrays [[LuckinCoffee]]'s density and supply chain alongside [[Starbucks]]' [[PremiumEverydayBrandTension|premium-versus-everyday tension]] and [[BlueBottleCoffee]]'s higher-end positioning; institution-led ownership and [[CoffeeChainLocalization|local product and pricing decisions]] raise entry barriers, while tea brands create [[BeverageCategoryConvergence]]. The hosts' deal and brand interpretations are source-dated, not independently established transactions. [[141-kafei-zhanzheng-2026-jigouhua-yu-bentuhua-feng-tou-quan-1-141-1]]
- **Experience and price:** The hosts' [[SouthKorea|Korean]] observations contrast a competitive café scene, [[MannerCoffee]]'s reported difficulties and [[Sandunban]]'s differentiated instant positioning within [[AIResistantExperientialConsumption|offline experiential consumption]]. An unrelated household-budget episode uses a source-local ladder—RMB 180 boutique, RMB 45–50 hand brew, RMB 30 Starbucks, RMB 9.9 chain, RMB 5 discounted espresso and under RMB 1 home or group-buy—to show [[LifestyleCostRationalization]] without requiring total austerity. [[137-cong-shunde-zhuroupo-dao-hanguo-shengshuidong-naxie-ai-wufa-qudai-de-tiyan-xiaofei-feng-tou-quan-1-137-1]] [[ep35-jiangxin-bujiangzhi-zhongchan-jieji-zuihou-de-juejiang-lkyik124v1nnb-4dglyorf7-oc43]]
- **Repeat use and distribution:** In a founder advice call, [[VashonIslandCoffeeDust]] is a coffee additive whose gifting must turn into daily use through packaging, bundles, counter visibility and easier preparation; a K-cup-style suggestion addresses friction, not a proven category-wide tactic. Another call reports [[CaneDogCoffee]] at about $2 million annual sales, roughly 80% from hotels, restaurants and cafés, and recommends Barbados-origin branding and regional U.S. proof over unfocused expansion. Separately [[ShengdongZaokafei|声动早咖啡]] borrows the espresso image for a fresh, concentrated workday news habit: its 2021 launch trailer promises episodes under 15 minutes on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, aspiring to daily delivery—not evidence of the show's subsequent cadence. ## Qualifications
The historical source is a podcast reading of a book first published in 1934; it explicitly underplays colonial plantation labor and slavery by contemporary standards, so its triumphant modernization narrative is incomplete. The Chinese chain outlook comes from investor-host interpretations, Korean café judgments from field observation, pricing from host anecdotes and founder growth advice from individual cases. Neither a short-form show's coffee branding nor a Barbados founder's channel recommendation describes coffee's general market laws.

## What Changed
- Separate historical commodity and public-space roles from modern chain ownership and household consumption.
- Distinguish physical tasting, founder distribution cases and media metaphor from coffee-market evidence.
