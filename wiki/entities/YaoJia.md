# 姚贾 / Yao Jia

姚贾 / Yao Jia enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-112-1-yaojia-shi-ruhe-ping-yizhangzui-po-siguo-hezong-lthw0fvvinmf6ghxpqut60agsueg]], a [[RuiqiJiangtouZizhiTongjian|芮淇讲透资治通鉴]] episode about Qin diplomacy before unification. The source presents him as a [[WeyState|卫国]] man of low origin: his father guarded a city gate, and [[HanFei|韩非]] later attacked him by citing a theft record in [[Daliang|大梁]].

The episode first places Yao Jia on the anti-Qin side. [[ZhaoState|赵国]] values his speech talent and sends him to [[HanState|韩国]], [[WeiState|魏国]], and [[ChuState|楚国]] to organize or strengthen [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] pressure against [[QinState|秦国]].

[[LiSi|李斯]] disrupts that position by spreading damaging claims in Zhao. Once Zhao recalls and expels Yao Jia, Yao Jia reads the attack differently: if Qin is willing to destroy his Zhao position, Qin must think he is useful. He goes to [[Xianyang|咸阳]], is recommended by Li Si, and enters [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]'s service.

The source's main claim is that Yao Jia does not break the four-state coalition by speech alone. Ying Zheng gives him carriages, horses, money, clothing, and a sword, making his Qin backing visible. Yao Jia then spends three years moving through Han, Wei, Chu, and Zhao, combining persuasion with bribes to powerful court figures. His mission becomes a named case of [[InterestBasedCoalitionSabotage|利益瓦解合纵]] and [[WarringStatesCareeristMobility|乱世职业经理人式流动]].
