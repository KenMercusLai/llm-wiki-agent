## Overview
Gongsun Shu is the Shu-based rival ruler whose final Chengdu defeat in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-713-liuxiu-nu-le-mingjiang-wuhan-jing-xuexi-chengdu-lv6555l99it33gw34uydsz4tkbje|Hanji 713]] ends with battlefield collapse, Yan Cen's surrender, and a post-surrender massacre by Wu Han.

## Current Profile
In the episode's bounded account, Gongsun Shu personally enters the final Chengdu fight, attacks Wu Han's position, and sends Yan Cen against Zhang Gong. The battle initially strains the Han attackers, but Wu Han's reinforcements under Gao Wu and Tang Han turn the fight and leave Gongsun Shu fatally wounded. Before dying, he entrusts the remaining army to Yan Cen, who offers Chengdu in surrender the next day.

Gongsun Shu's posthumous profile is also shaped by the episode's account of coercive scholar recruitment. His regime tries to force Li Nie, Qiao Xuan, Wang Hao, Wang Jia, and others into service through poison threats, hostage families, and office/title inducements, so the fall of Shu becomes both a military defeat and a legitimacy exposure. Wu Han's subsequent killing of Gongsun Shu's household and clan then turns the surrender aftermath into a test of Liu Xiu's conquest discipline.

## Key Characteristics
- Shu-based rival ruler whose Chengdu defeat closes the western campaign.
- Personal battlefield participant who risks himself in the final attack on Wu Han.
- Commander dependent on Yan Cen for the regime's final military continuity after his mortal wound.
- Coercive recruiter of local scholars whose office summons rely on poison threats and hostage pressure.
- Defeated ruler whose household and clan become targets of Wu Han's post-surrender violence.
- Political foil for Liu Xiu's later honors, compensation, and talent incorporation in Shu.

## Qualifications
This page is bounded to the Hanji 713 source note. It describes Gongsun Shu through the episode's final-war and post-conquest frame rather than offering a complete biography of his earlier rule in Shu.
