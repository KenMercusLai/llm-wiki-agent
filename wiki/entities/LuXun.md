# 鲁迅

## Overview
本页按主题区分有出处的事件、解释及其局限。 《故事新编》的《理水》《起死》重写神话与庄子典故，节目强调幽默、社会批评与对工具化信念的警惕。

## Current Profile
《故事新编》的《理水》《起死》重写神话与庄子典故，节目强调幽默、社会批评与对工具化信念的警惕。 节目借鲁迅评价《儒林外史》与《金瓶梅》，将讽刺和社会书写放入文学接受史。 汉字论争中的引语与矿业、地质训练分别提供语言史及实用科学语境。 1921年芥川《鼻子》《罗生门》译介是中日现代文学流通的具体例子。

## Evidence
- **Myth and satire:** In [[GushiXinbian|《故事新编》]], [[LiShui|《理水》]] recasts [[YuTheGreat|大禹]] to satirize insulated scholarship, bureaucratic performance and [[DisasterAestheticization|aestheticized disaster]], while [[QiSi|《起死》]] uses the [[Zhuangzi|庄子]] skull motif to challenge [[QiWuLun|齐物论]] as a status shield when [[YangDa|杨大]] asks for material compensation. The [[MihuanChishu|蜜獾吃书]] reading stresses humor, myth's literary and religious roots, Lu Xun's science background and [[InstrumentalBeliefCritique|criticism of “吃教”]]: he is not simply anti-science or anti-religion, but wary of opportunistic appeals to science, revolution, loyalty, filiality and celebrity. [[75-gushi-xinbian-kulou-bei-fuhuo-hou-hui-ganji-zhuangzi-ma-681010087]]
- **Literary criticism and reception:** The hosts invoke Lu Xun's praise of [[RulinWaishi|《儒林外史》]] to explain painful satire about failed scholars, status performance and social cruelty, and place 《孔乙己》 and 《阿Q正传》 in its later tradition. A separate [[Banlatte|半拿铁]] discussion cites him among modern critics who read [[JinPingMei|《金瓶梅》]] as serious social-historical literature rather than only scandal, an instance of [[GenreLiteraryLegitimacy|genre legitimacy]] and [[ClassicReadingComplexity|complex reading]], not proof he authored either earlier work. [[58-rulin-waishi-jiashe-kaogong-chengle-weiyi-chulu-645207859]] [[fanwai-15-banlatte-jiang-jinpingmei-la-erqie-haishi-luse-de-991508433]]
- **Language and science:** One episode cites his “汉字不灭，中国必亡” in the modern phonetic-writing debate, where [[HanziTransdialectalFunction|transdialectal writing]] and [[ChineseCharacterEvidenceDiscipline|careful evidence]] complicate simple modernization slogans. [[ZhangQuan|张泉]] separately recalls his mining-and-railway schooling, descent into mines, first book on Chinese mineral resources and later geology, archaeology and paleontology interests, linking practical science and industrial anxiety with his literary generation. [[57-shuojie-hanzi-shiniaopi-weisha-shi-shi-zitou-642970319]] [[19-huangye-shang-de-dashi-wo-bei-yu-luanshi-qiu-yanjiu-ben-wei-niliu-zhi-wangju-565412437]]
- **Translation:** An episode says Lu Xun translated [[AkutagawaRyunosuke|芥川龙之介]]'s [[TheNoseAkutagawa|《鼻子》]] and [[RashomonAkutagawa|《罗生门》]] in 1921 and published them in Beijing's Morning News; this is an early China-Japan literary reception example, not a claim of sole mediation. [[17-diyubian-zhongsheng-jie-ku-yi-nian-ru-mo-561780565]]

## Qualifications
Podcast readings of literary texts are interpretations rather than direct proof of Lu Xun’s intentions. The 1921 translation notice and character-reform slogan remain attributed to the respective programs.

## What Changed
- 《故事新编》的文学分析之外，翻译、矿业训练与汉字论争扩展了人物的有出处的活动范围。

## Relationships
- [[GuJiegang]] - 节目借顾颉刚疑古学说解读鲁迅《理水》讽刺古史研究的层次，并非两人共同创作。
- [[GuoMoruo]] - 节目以郭沫若对鲁迅生前批评、身后称赞的转向说明《起死》所讽刺的立场工具化；这是节目解释。
- [[GushiBian]] - 《理水》中的古史学者形象被节目放入古史辨质疑禹等上古叙事的背景，非鲁迅直接否认全部史实。
- [[WanErDuoFeng]] - 节目借鲁迅评价《儒林外史》的“婉而多讽”解释其含蓄又刺痛的讽刺风格，而非鲁迅作品标题。
