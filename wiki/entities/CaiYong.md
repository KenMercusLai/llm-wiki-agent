# 蔡邕 / Cai Yong

蔡邕 / Cai Yong enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-964-bie-jiaoao-bie-jiaoao-bie-jiaoao-lo2-edzffu0v9ixoabc06rx-oqoq|Hanji 964]] as the scholar killed by [[WangYunLateHan|王允]] after [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s death. The episode says Cai Yong had been a guest at Wang Yun's house and had enough literary prestige and historical-writing capacity to make Wang Yun anxious.

The host's interpretation is source-scoped. Cai Yong is not treated only as someone punished for sympathy toward Dong Zhuo; he becomes the person who might have recorded Wang Yun's compromised conduct under Dong Zhuo and thereby weakened Wang Yun's public image as a loyal anti-Dong-Zhuo hero. His plea to accept punishment and live in order to continue writing history is compared to [[SimaQian|司马迁]], while Wang Yun's refusal is tied to [[HistoricalRecordAuthority|史官记录话语权]].
