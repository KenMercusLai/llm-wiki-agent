# 东阿 / Dong'e (Late Han)

东阿 / Dong'e enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-979-zaoyu-tongliao-beici-ta-ruhe-wei-caocao-liwan-kuanglan-luu58a2ww11g-radh-5g4l7fcis|Hanji 979]] as one of the remaining [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] strongholds that prevent [[CaoCao|曹操]] from losing his whole base after the Zhang Miao-Lü Bu turn.

The source pairs Dong'e with [[ZaoZhi|枣祗]]'s local organization. Zao Zhi fortifies the city, gathers officials and commoners, and organizes adult men and women for defense, while [[ChengYu|程昱]] blocks [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] at [[CangtingFordLateHan|仓亭津]]. Dong'e therefore matters as a city where base survival is produced by administrative mobilization and river-route denial together.
