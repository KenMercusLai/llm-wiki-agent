# 燕王喜 / King Xi of Yan

燕王喜 / King Xi of Yan enters the wiki as the [[YanState|燕国]] ruler whose first developed decision is the failed attack on [[ZhaoState|赵国]] in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-2-liuguo-jingran-tongshi-wei-ta-diaosang-ln6-5v-b-ybqmf5ucepusdo3lf68]]. After [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] dies and the six states attend the funeral, Yan Wang Xi looks for opportunity in Zhao's post-[[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]] trauma.

He sends [[LiFuWarringStates|栗腹]] to Zhao under alliance language, accepts Li Fu's report that Zhao's adult males are depleted and its orphans not yet grown, and calls officials to prepare war. [[LeJian|乐间]] warns that Zhao is a fighting state and cannot be judged only by demographic loss, while [[JiangQu|将渠]] warns that attacking immediately after alliance and gift exchange will end badly. Yan Wang Xi ignores both warnings.

The resulting [[HaodaiBattle|鄗代之战]] turns his calculation into a reversal. [[LianPo|廉颇]] defeats Li Fu near Hao, defeats the Yan forces around Dai, pursues Yan five hundred li, and surrounds Yan's capital region. Yan Wang Xi must ask for peace and appoint Jiang Qu as chancellor and negotiator, making the ignored remonstrant the person needed for recovery.
