# 彭城之战 / Battle of Pengcheng

## Overview
The Battle of Pengcheng was a Chu-Han turning point in which Xiang Yu’s counterattack drove Liu Bang from the Chu capital and shattered his coalition.

## Current Profile
The sources connect political mobilization to surprise, severed retreat, mass rout, then recovery through Xiayi, Xingyang, cavalry and grain routes; battlefield figures remain source-attributed.

## Key Characteristics
- Moral and coalition framing preceded the fighting.
- The battle combined secrecy, cavalry shock, severed routes and river pursuit.
- The defeat was simultaneously tactical, familial and diplomatic.
- The response was distributed command and logistical rebuilding.

## Evidence
- **Coalition and overreach:** After [[XiangYu|项羽]] killed Yi Emperor [[MiXin|芈心 / 楚怀王]], [[DongGongXincheng|新城董公]] urged [[LiuBang|刘邦]] to mourn him publicly and summon lords against regicide, a [[MoralizedWarPretext|moralized war pretext]]. Liu Bang deceived [[ChenYu|陈馀]] with a substitute head to secure Zhao support without killing [[ZhangEr|张耳]]; [[SimaAng|司马卬]]'s capture and Han control of [[HeneiCommandery|河内郡]] weakened Xiang Yu's north. [[TianHeng|田横]] gathered [[TianRong|田荣]]'s remnants, installed [[TianGuangQiKing|田广]], and held [[JuWarringStates|莒城 / 城阳]], keeping Xiang Yu in Qi under [[CoalitionSettlementFailure|联军战后安排失败]]. Liu Bang occupied [[Xuzhou|彭城]], then his army celebrated with treasure, women and wine while even [[ZhangLiang|张良]] and [[ChenPing|陈平]] are described as silent: a narrated [[VictoryOverreachFailure|victory-overreach failure]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-151-huanyuan-pengcheng-zhizhan-xiangyu-zai-fengshen-1-lspx-bqe5kczq6bmn6thlerfbrmu]]
- **Surprise and rout:** Xiang Yu hid Pengcheng's fall, feigned continuing war in Qi, gathered roughly 30,000 elite riders/chariots, took [[XiaoCounty|萧县]] at night west of the city and cut the retreat and supply line. [[BattlefieldInformationControl|Secrecy]] and [[CavalryShockCommandStrike|mounted shock]] hit a wrong-facing, poorly coordinated lord coalition still afraid after [[JuluBattle|巨鹿之战]]. Pursuit around Gu water, [[SiRiver|泗水]] and [[SuiRiver|睢水]] produced the chronicle image “睢水为之不流”; its violent northwest wind/sandstorm lets Liu Bang escape toward Pei with a small escort. The episode's 30,000-versus-560,000 contrast is not an independently verified count. A later comparison with [[HanXin|韩信]] at Jingxing finds that Liu Bang's deep, newly victorious army beside water lacked the conditions for [[BackwaterBattle|背水一战]] resolve. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-151-huanyuan-pengcheng-zhizhan-xiangyu-zai-fengshen-2-lqy1uu29xbfipo9e0kq6obkl3oay]] [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-159-2-zhangliang-shuole-sha-jiuxia-hanchao-lvs9elvrjp-ypz63w4czpuhbsfb]]
- **Command, family and alliance costs:** The host argues that keeping Han Xin in [[GuanzhongRegion|关中]] against [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]] denied Liu Bang large-force coordination under ancient signaling, transport and road constraints ([[AncientLargeArmyCommandLimits|古代大兵团指挥极限]]); this is a counterfactual, not a proven sole cause. In flight Liu Bang repeatedly pushed [[LiuYing|刘盈]] and [[LuYuanPrincess|鲁元公主]] from his cart; [[XiahouYing|夏侯婴]] retrieved them, while [[ZengHe|曾赫]] held formation against Chu riders. Relatives became hostages, and lords shifted toward Xiang Yu under [[PowerBasedAllianceRepricing|实力重估式阵营转向]], leaving a diminished network at [[Xiayi|下邑]] and [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-152-xiangyu-3wanren-ruhe-wansheng-liubang-56wan-dajun-1-lrtpxlak0dlyej8bozmmhdtfczno]] [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-152-xiangyu-3wanren-ruhe-wansheng-liubang-56wan-dajun-2-lq9ecpjb4r-ij0-e-iu-ezfavowj]]
- **Distributed recovery:** At Xiayi Liu Bang offered land east of [[Wuguan|武关]] and Zhang Liang's [[XiayiPlan|下邑画策]] divided the response: hold Chu frontally, turn [[YingBu|英布]] in the south, support [[PengYue|彭越]] in Chu's rear and give Han Xin independent room. [[SuiHe|随何]] volunteered for the Ying Bu mission. At Xingyang [[XiaoHe|萧何]] levied emergency Guanzhong manpower, Han Xin checked pursuers, and [[GuanYing|灌婴]] formed cavalry with [[LiBiQinCavalry|李璧]] and [[LuoJiaQinCavalry|骆甲]]; road work to [[Aocang|敖仓]] supported the [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]]. ## Qualifications
The 30,000 versus 560,000 contrast, river-death imagery and wind escape are episode/chronicle narrative rather than independently verified statistics. Han Xin’s absence is a host counterfactual, not a proven sole cause.

## What Changed
- Political mobilization, cavalry surprise, hostage and alliance losses now lead into a distinct multi-front recovery account.
