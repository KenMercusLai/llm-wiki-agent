# 李肃 / Li Su (late Han)

李肃 / Li Su enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-965-bangguo-bieren-de-mang-weishenme-yao-shao-ti-ltjd-cv59hnm-n8ar9jxdogp9q0q|Hanji 965]] as the officer [[LyuBu|吕布]] sends to attack [[NiuFuLateHan|牛辅]] after [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s death. His mission fails: Niu Fu defeats him at Shan County, Li Su flees back toward Hongnong, and Lü Bu executes him.

The page is source-scoped because the episode uses Li Su primarily as a transition point. His failed attack shows that Dong Zhuo's remnant forces are still dangerous after the assassination, while his execution adds to the coercive and fearful atmosphere that makes later [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]] decisions legible.
