# 彭越 / Peng Yue

## Overview
Peng Yue’s reported rise from local armed leader to Liang king combines irregular supply-line warfare with negotiated coalition rewards; his execution became an early-Han legitimacy warning.

## Current Profile
The narratives describe a former fisher assembling a disciplined irregular force, then using mobile raids to disrupt Chu supplies after setbacks at Pengcheng. Liu Bang’s territorial promise secured participation in the final coalition and led to Liang kingship; the later execution and Luan Bu’s defense challenged the safety of founding merit-holders.

## Key Characteristics
- Local command discipline.
- Mobile rear-line pressure.
- Reward and Liang kingship.
- Postwar danger to meritorious kings.

## Evidence
- **从巨野地方武装到反楚压力：** 据秦末叙事，昌邑渔人彭越在巨野一带转为武装首领，[[JuluBattle|巨鹿之战]]后的反秦形势中约定黎明集合、未滥杀全部迟到者，只处决最后一名、筑坛起事以确立[[BanditToArmyDiscipline|军纪]]，随后吸纳散兵，[[LiuBang|刘邦]]攻昌邑时已有逾千人来附。[[XiangYu|项羽]]分封后，他率逾万人于巨野，[[TianRong|田荣]]授将印，彭越击败[[TianAn|田安]]并袭西楚；他对楚境持续骚扰，构成项羽分封后的[[RewardAllocationBacklash|奖赏分配反噬]]；与刘邦诉诸[[HuaiwangAgreement|怀王之约]]的西进政治说辞共同牵制项羽，但不等于同一正式编制。[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-135-mingyun-tai-shenqi-cong-yufu-zuodao-wang-lrh1lhuxxikx4m6prpi6qm0jcafb]] [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-144-xiangyu-jituan-fumie-de-yuanyin-2-lut3jiatchla3pfxpkznsrs7wkzu]] [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-148-2-peng-le-wangling-muqin-xiangyu-sharen-zhuxin-ljyv8bow4rj1l5ths3znx2evgvtm]]
- **败后机动袭粮：** [[PengchengBattle|彭城之战]]后彭越失地、在黄河及河南滑县一带流动，[[ZhangLiang|张良]]的[[XiayiPlan|下邑画策]]仍看中其袭楚后方能力。因[[HanXin|韩信]]兵员不断被刘邦抽调到[[XingyangStandoff|荥阳相持]]，刘邦转以[[LiuJia|刘贾]]、[[LuWan|卢绾]]支援彭越；[[YuanShengChuHan|袁生]]诱项羽南下时，彭越向[[SuiRiver|睢水]]切粮，并在黄河、[[Xiapi|下邳]]至[[Chenggao|成皋]]之间反复牵制楚军，取睢阳、外黄等城、焚粮运物资，迫项羽在战线间往返。节目对战略疲劳的判断不否认项羽多次局部取胜。[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-152-xiangyu-3wanren-ruhe-wansheng-liubang-56wan-dajun-2-lq9ecpjb4r-ij0-e-iu-ezfavowj]] [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-153-xiayi-huace-canbai-hou-liubang-ruhe-qisi-huisheng-1-lgvjuxuskyyfmyeeqm9y8aeln-hu]] [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-2-zhuan-gei-ni-piqi-da-de-pengyou-weiwang-panhan-jing-shi-liubang-ai-maren-ls5nvunmhakvefd2oxn4zdyoob4l]] [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-1-ta-ti-liubang-qu-si-rujin-chengle-zhengzhou-de-shouhushen-li3su2zlmbm1ift9nibujgtcrxwm]] [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-2-liubang-ruhe-shoushi-wei-gao-quan-zhong-de-xiashu-lqvye2zmqhu3otk0uwu7lakf2prz]] [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-3-zuipao-wang-lishiqi-budong-yibing-naxia-70-yu-cheng-lq88alghxqxxcddk4zzqodkezipx]]
- **战功与许地会师：** 鸿沟议和后的追击中，彭越未按约到场；张良认为不是单纯抗命，而是封赏未定，刘邦遂许睢阳至谷城之地促其参与[[GaixiaBattle|垓下之战]]。胜后刘邦收韩信兵权并改封楚王，同时封彭越为梁王，领故魏地、都[[Dingtao|定陶]]；战时自主盟军的土地预期由此转入[[BattlefieldVictoryToFoundingOrder|建国秩序]]。[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-167-jianzheng-xiangyu-zuizhong-zhizhan-1-lm6nwhjjuguhrmwzoz0sqawgxwuv]] [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-169-pandian-liubang-dengjiqian-buweirenzhi-de-gushi-1-liwkaygjtyafxxpdwzq9pdhuzroi]]
- **处死、辩护与诸王恐惧：** 彭越被诛并夷三族后，曾受其赎回、任梁大夫的[[LuanBu|栾布]]在首级下哭丧，冒死向刘邦主张彭越因病未响应征召、并未反叛，且彭城至荥阳成皋、垓下皆有功；刘邦赦栾布并任都尉。这是栾布的辩词，不是独立裁定无罪。节目称彭越被制为肉酱送各诸侯，[[YingBu|英布]]闻讯停猎、回[[HuainanKingdomEarlyHan|淮南]]暗中备兵；该恐惧有助解释[[DifferentSurnameKingSuspicion|异姓王猜忌]]的连锁效应，却非英布起兵的唯一原因。[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-184-lishi-kongbupian-liubang-de-pengshi-roujiang-2-liebzrdrvo1okonpdjvrjatuwx9i]] [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-186-yi-chang-yin-bajie-lingdao-laopo-yinfa-de-miemen-can-an-lqx14uzf60oi6wnnzdkottd6kx1n]]

## Qualifications
- These are podcast retellings of ancient narratives, not independently verified troop or motive measurements. Luan Bu’s defense that Peng Yue had not rebelled is advocacy. The corpse-paste account describes the source’s claimed shock to Ying Bu; it does not by itself establish every cause of his revolt.

## What Changed
- Local force discipline and supply-line raiding are distinguished from postwar title bargaining.
- Luan Bu’s no-rebellion defense and Ying Bu’s reported reaction qualify an uncomplicated founding-merit narrative.
