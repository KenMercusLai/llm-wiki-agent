## Overview

施丹 / Shi Dan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-626-waren-fenmu-wangmang-jing-ruci-sangxinbingkuang-lotwk6rbtl5pvbrnlfrysu5ol6rz|Hanji 626]] as a Western Han official retrospectively rewarded after [[WangMang|王莽]]'s posthumous prosecution of [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]] and [[DingJiWesternHan|丁姬]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-579-zoujin-kexue-guyao-shi-yao-ma-lof7pvrnlzv2lmbzjshbx7vi2c2n|Hanji 579]] backfills the immediate removal mechanism: [[ZhuBoWesternHan|朱博]] and [[ZhaoXuanWesternHan|赵璇]] accuse him after Aidi accepts the Dingtao title settlement. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-580-huigu-sizhou-shuishi-nide-kaoshan-liid3tymhojmajkyeqoq6vryprhz|Hanji 580]] preserves that removal as the immediate precedent before Zhu Bo and Zhao Xuan turn against Wang Mang. His role is narrow but useful: he shows how one court judgment can be reversed when later ritual-political memory changes.

## Current Profile

Hanji 579 says the removal comes after the Dingtao title settlement. Zhu Bo and Zhao Xuan argue that Dong Hong had originally proposed changing the titles, that Shi Dan's earlier impeachment suppressed the proposal and harmed Aidi's filial duty, and that Shi Dan should lose title and fief now that Dong Hong has been restored. Aidi approves.

Hanji 580 then treats Shi Dan's title-and-fief removal as the preceding case before Zhu Bo and Zhao Xuan turn the same punitive logic against Wang Mang. Hanji 626 later says Shi Dan had previously opposed Fu Taihou and had been removed from office and stripped of ducal standing. After Wang Mang's 5 CE campaign against Fu Taihou and Ding Ji, Shi Dan is summoned to Chang'an, made a guannei marquis, restored to his former income, later made Yiyang marquis, and then dies soon afterward.

Shi Dan therefore functions as a posthumous-merit-vindication case. The reward does not come from a new achievement in the episode; it comes from Wang Mang recoding an earlier stance as correct once the court officially condemns Fu Taihou's old status.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han official whose earlier opposition to Fu Taihou becomes retroactively rewarded.
- Target of Zhu Bo and Zhao Xuan's post-title-settlement cleanup after he had opposed Dong Hong's proposal.
- Earlier target whose title and fief are removed before the attempted attack on Wang Mang.
- Example of Wang Mang turning posthumous ritual correction into living political sorting.
- Restored and re-titled shortly before his death.
- Narrow source figure rather than a fully synthesized statesman profile.

## Qualifications

Hanji 579 gives the immediate Zhu Bo/Zhao Xuan accusation but not Dong Hong's full original memorial, Shi Dan's full impeachment text, or the complete legal formula for reducing him. Hanji 580 only names Shi Dan as the immediately preceding punishment case before the Wang Mang attack. Hanji 626 does not reconstruct the full earlier debate in which Shi Dan opposed Fu Taihou. The page therefore treats him as source-scoped evidence for removal and later retrospective reward, not as a complete biography or independent judgment on the earlier dispute.
