# 蒙敖 / Meng Ao

## Overview
蒙敖 / Meng Ao is a [[QinState|Qin]] commander in the cited Warring States chronicle episodes. His campaigns span Qi, Han, Zhao and Wei and include one internal-suppression notice; the 285 BCE commander identification is explicitly source-critical.

## Current Profile
The episodes portray military pressure and commandery-building as connected but not identical: captured territory could become Qin counties or a new commandery, while a five-state coalition also checked Meng Ao in battle.

## Key Characteristics
- A 285 BCE Qi-campaign notice is corrected from “Meng Wu” to Meng Ao on the host's reading of 《史记》.
- Under Qin Zhuangxiang Wang he campaigns against Han, Zhao and Wei with named territorial gains.
- During Ying Zheng's early rule he suppresses unrest and continues attacks despite famine and epidemic.
- Xinlingjun's coalition defeat of Meng Ao limits any uninterrupted-victory narrative.

## Evidence
- **Commander identification and Qi:** The host contrasts [[ZizhiTongjian]]'s “蒙武” with [[Shiji]]'s “蒙敖,” identifying Meng Ao as Meng Wu's father; after [[QiMinWang]]'s Qi destroyed [[SongState]] under [[QinZhaoxiangwang]], Qin attacked [[QiState]] in 285 BCE, reportedly taking nine cities and installing Qin county officials. The correction is the episode's claim, not a fresh check of the primary texts. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-69-bikan-gudai-diwang-qipa-sifa-da-pandian-lukvjsktg-pgfjhizvypwmdmw-13]]
- **Eastern gains and administration:** In Qin Zhuangxiang Wang's first year (249 BCE), Meng Ao takes Chenggao from [[HanState]] and [[SanchuanWarringStates]] is established. In the second year he attacks [[ZhaoState]], taking thirty-seven cities including Yuci and Langmeng; against [[WeiState]] he later takes Gaodu and Ji. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-1-lvbuwei-shi-jiangziya-houren-lveg-wsmggroaxjsnxopaevexqod]] [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-2-kongzi-zai-luguo-de-shenshi-zhimi-lphy12exg4u7f-30qkir96gv634r]] [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-103-1-simaqian-kanzhong-de-xinlingjun-suan-shi-xiaoren-ma-lvty1pdnibvqpk0m16kkxzw4t-ck]]
- **Coalition check:** Those Wei losses prompt [[WeiAnxiWang]] to recall [[Xinlingjun]] after ten years in Zhao. Xinlingjun's [[HezongAlliance|five-state force]] then defeats Meng Ao south of the Yellow River, limiting the otherwise continuous Qin-expansion story rather than undoing the earlier gains. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-103-1-simaqian-kanzhong-de-xinlingjun-suan-shi-xiaoren-ma-lvty1pdnibvqpk0m16kkxzw4t-ck]]
- **Later pressure and administration:** In 246 BCE he suppresses Jinyang unrest under young [[QinShiHuang]]. A 244 BCE famine does not stop the Han offensive, which the source counts as twelve cities. He attacks Wei in 243 BCE but stops in the third month; after locust and epidemic pressure in 242 BCE, a renewed attack takes twenty cities and Qin establishes [[DongCommandery]]. ## Qualifications
The [[LushiChunqiu]] compilation notice is adjacent Qinji 102-2 chronology, not Meng Ao’s authored project. The city totals and exact years follow podcast summaries of ancient chronicles, not a new independent count. The Meng Wu/Meng Ao discrepancy is one notice's textual correction, not two distinct simultaneous Qi campaigns. [[RankSaleDisasterFinance]] is adjacent to Qin’s 242 BCE locust/epidemic response, not evidence that Meng Ao designed relief finance. A victory over Meng Ao belongs in the profile beside Qin's longer expansion.
