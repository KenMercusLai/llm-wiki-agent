# 牛辅 / Niu Fu (late Han)

牛辅 / Niu Fu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-965-bangguo-bieren-de-mang-weishenme-yao-shao-ti-ltjd-cv59hnm-n8ar9jxdogp9q0q|Hanji 965]] as [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s son-in-law and one of the immediate remnant commanders after Dong Zhuo's death. [[LyuBu|吕布]] sends [[LiSuLateHan|李肃]] to attack him at Shan County, but Niu Fu defeats Li Su; Lü Bu later executes Li Su after the failed mission.

The episode makes Niu Fu important less as a victorious commander than as a fear case. Even after winning, he remains unsettled by the wider anti-Dong-Zhuo danger and by camp instability. A night-fright event in his army prompts him to abandon the troops and flee, after which his close followers kill him. His case grounds [[MilitaryCampPanic|军营夜惊群体恐慌]] and shows why the broader [[LiangzhouWarlordClique|凉州系军阀]] saw disbanding as existentially dangerous.
