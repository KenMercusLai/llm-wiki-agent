# 刘辩 / Liu Bian (Hongnong Wang)

刘辩 / Liu Bian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-950-budong-quanli-beihou-de-renxing-bi-bai-wuyi-lu8-u78yqvvwwarsz8dlevvzchxr|Hanji 950]] as the deposed emperor and Hongnong Wang killed under [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s control. The episode says Dong Zhuo sends [[LiRuLateHan|李儒]] with poisoned wine to kill the fifteen-year-old Liu Bian, turning the captured court into a site of dynastic murder rather than merely intimidation.

His role is narrow but important. Liu Bian's death gives the anti-Dong-Zhuo side a stronger moral charge while making [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s position more visibly exposed: Dong Zhuo can remove one Han imperial figure and govern through the other.
