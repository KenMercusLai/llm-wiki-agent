# 张楚政权 / Zhang Chu Regime

张楚政权 / Zhang Chu Regime enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-2-qilai-bei-yapo-de-renmen-lpx9-iicwog4nj-nudaj-i4ajb25|Qinji 128-2]] as the rebel regime implied by [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] and [[WuGuang|吴广]]'s uprising. The episode does not narrate the regime's full creation or institutional form; it uses Zhang Chu mainly as the destination of the [[DazexiangUprising|大泽乡起义]] and as the setting for later internal fragility.

The source's most concrete Zhang Chu claim is negative. It says [[GeYing|葛婴]]'s death matters because it removes a major general and worsens suspicion between Chen Sheng and his subordinates. For now, this page keeps Zhang Chu source-scoped as an emerging anti-Qin regime whose legitimacy begins with [[RebelMandateManufacture|起义天命制造]] but whose durability is threatened by mistrust.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-3-chensheng-wuguang-zudui-chenggong-locnaq-fo1fztyud5nghwkteams|Qinji 128-3]] directly narrates Zhang Chu's creation. After Chen Sheng takes Chen County, local elders ask him to become Chu king; [[ZhangEr|张耳]] and [[ChenYu|陈馀]] advise against it and propose [[SixStateRestorationAntiQinStrategy|六国复国式反秦策略]] instead. Chen Sheng rejects that advice, declares himself Chu king, and names the regime Zhang Chu. The host glosses the name as "expanding Chu," making Zhang Chu a claim to larger succession of the unified realm, not just a local rebel label.

The same episode gives Zhang Chu an initial command move: [[WuGuang|吴广]] is made acting Zhang Chu king and sent to supervise generals moving west against [[YingyangWarringStates|荥阳]]. That makes the regime's first recorded direction both territorial and legitimacy-driven.
