# 张楚政权 / Zhang Chu Regime

张楚政权 / Zhang Chu Regime enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-128-2-qilai-bei-yapo-de-renmen-lpx9-iicwog4nj-nudaj-i4ajb25|Qinji 128-2]] as the rebel regime implied by [[ChenSheng|陈胜]] and [[WuGuang|吴广]]'s uprising. The episode does not narrate the regime's full creation or institutional form; it uses Zhang Chu mainly as the destination of the [[DazexiangUprising|大泽乡起义]] and as the setting for later internal fragility.

The source's most concrete Zhang Chu claim is negative. It says [[GeYing|葛婴]]'s death matters because it removes a major general and worsens suspicion between Chen Sheng and his subordinates. For now, this page keeps Zhang Chu source-scoped as an emerging anti-Qin regime whose legitimacy begins with [[RebelMandateManufacture|起义天命制造]] but whose durability is threatened by mistrust.
