# Paul Buchheit

## Overview
Paul Buchheit’s builder, YC adviser and angel-investor roles connect prototype feedback with founder judgment; outside recollections remain attributed to the interviewees.

## Current Profile
At Google, Buchheit developed Gmail through reuse of internal infrastructure and direct user feedback. In YC and angel-investor settings, he emphasized founder quality and costly customer signals; founders’ stories about Scribd, Coinbase and early smartwatch funding illustrate advice or backing without proving sole causation.

## Key Characteristics
- Gmail prototyping and user feedback.
- Customer pull and founder advice.
- Seed backing and network influence.

## Evidence
- **Gmail的迭代方法：** 自述称[[PaulBuchheit]]为1999年入职的[[Google]]第23名员工，参与广告、产品搜索等工作，后由Larry Page和Wayne Rosing指派开发[[Gmail]]。他复用Google Groups/Usenet索引快速做出原型，给内部用户使用、以单题满意度调查并与不满意者交谈；一GB容量及泄露后赶在期限内有限容量上线，体现[[FastFeedbackLoops|反馈迭代]]与[[ProductLaunchUnderConstraint|约束下发布]]，而非凭空出现的个人副业。他之后创办[[FriendFeed]]，曾与Facebook/[[Meta]]及[[YCombinator]]关联。[[socialradarspod-paulb-final]]
- **需求与创始人判断：** Buchheit说早期支付、意向书等有成本的客户行为比创始人的热情更能检验[[CustomerPull|需求]]；天使投资又取决于少数超大结果与创始人质量，他自述[[DoorDash]]是其已披露的最佳财务回报，而非所有投资都成功。[[BrianArmstrong]]回忆在[[Coinbase]]成立和融资前，PB建议及早解决联合创始人不合；[[JaredFriedman]]则回忆PB指出[[Scribd]]不是[[YouTube]]式娱乐网站而是搜索目的地，帮助团队利用[[Google]]文档流量形成[[SearchDrivenContentGrowth|增长回路]]。均为受访者的建议与回忆，而非PB独力造就两家公司。[[socialradarspod-paulb-final]] [[socialradarspod-brianarmstrong-final]] [[tsr-s5-jared-v3final-tsr-s5-jared-v3final]]
- **YC及种子投资网络：** [[HarjTaggar]]说[[AutomaticAuctions]]在Demo Day后获得PB投资，并回忆同PB及[[GarryTan]]赴莫斯科、接触俄罗斯政府创业兴趣的行程；[[EricMigicovsky]]称其Winter 2011 YC之后的早期[[Alerta]]/[[ImpulseWatch|Impulse]]资金有PB参与，支持了一批后来风险颇高的库存订单，后续[[Pebble]]成败不能单因归于这笔钱。[[tsr-s4-harj-v3-tsr-s4-harj-v3]] [[tsr-s4-ericm-v2-tsr-s4-ericm-v2]]

## Qualifications
- The Gmail first-Like-button claim is contested by Facebook’s account. DoorDash is PB’s disclosed strongest financial angel outcome in his interview; Pebble inventory and Scribd growth effects are founder recollections rather than isolated causal tests.

## What Changed
- Gmail’s prototype-feedback method and PB’s angel-investor selection criteria are related but distinct judgments.
- Founder interviews about Scribd, Pebble and Coinbase remain attributed cases, not proof that PB alone caused their outcomes.
