# 蒙恬 / Meng Tian

蒙恬 / Meng Tian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-116-shishang-zuican-taizi-bei-fuwang-qinshou-zhansha-lthruqxtd4iqofdxzvim58ph8rlk]] as the Qin general paired with [[LiXinQin|李信]] for the campaign against [[ChuState|楚国]]. The source does not yet develop Meng Tian's independent biography; his role is to mark Ying Zheng's choice to back a younger command team after rejecting [[WangJianQin|王翦]]'s 600,000-troop requirement.

In this episode, Meng Tian therefore belongs to the command-selection branch rather than to a finished battlefield outcome. The episode leaves the result of the Li Xin-Meng Tian campaign for later, using the appointment mainly to set up the risk created by [[AnchoringEffect|锚定效应]] and by overreading Li Xin's previous pursuit success against [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]].
