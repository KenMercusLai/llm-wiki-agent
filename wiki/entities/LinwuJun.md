# 临武君 / Linwu Jun

临武君 / Linwu Jun enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-2-mingming-yilian-baixiang-de-caize-ping-shenme-baixiang-lhfyb6hzcqkpbzdrwsxajb7hhmhe]] as the figure named in the [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] notice that [[Xunzi|荀子]] once discussed military affairs before [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]]. The episode does not develop his biography; it uses him mainly to raise a chronology and setting question after describing Xunzi's appointment in [[LanlingWarringStates|兰陵]] under [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]].

For the wiki, Linwu Jun is therefore a source-scoped military-debate node. His page records the source's limited function: he helps mark Xunzi as not only a philosopher of ritual and human nature, but also a participant in late-Warring-States arguments over war and state strength.
