# 陇西郡 / Longxi Commandery

## Overview
本页按主题区分有出处的事件、解释及其局限。 秦灭义渠后，陇西与北地、上郡构成北部边地防御与郡县治理的叙述。

## Current Profile
秦灭义渠后，陇西与北地、上郡构成北部边地防御与郡县治理的叙述。 前220年秦始皇巡行经陇西、北地；祭高庙的解释是节目推断。 刘邦收复关中后占领并设置陇西等郡，属于楚汉之际不同于秦时的行政阶段。 东汉末韩遂攻陇西、太守李相如附叛，呈现边地官治失效。

## Key Characteristics
- 秦灭义渠后，陇西与北地、上郡构成北部边地防御与郡县治理的叙述。
- 前220年秦始皇巡行经陇西、北地；祭高庙的解释是节目推断。
- 刘邦收复关中后占领并设置陇西等郡，属于楚汉之际不同于秦时的行政阶段。
- 东汉末韩遂攻陇西、太守李相如附叛，呈现边地官治失效。

## Evidence
- **Qin frontier administration:** After [[QinState|秦国]] destroyed [[YiquState|义渠]], the source places both 陇西 and the later [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]] in former Yiqu territory; with [[Shangjun|上郡]], they formed an administered defensive belt against northern Hu groups. This is a case of [[QinFrontierBufferManagement|rear-frontier management]], not a claim that all three commanderies had identical boundaries or histories. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5]]
- **Imperial itinerary:** In 220 BCE [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] passed through Longxi and [[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]] on his first post-unification journey. The host interprets the western route toward old Qin lands and [[YongchengQin|雍城]] as possibly linked to [[HighTempleRite|高庙礼]] rather than simply routine inspection; that purpose is an inference. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-121-yingzheng-zao-shijie-zuizao-gaosu-gonglu-2200nian-huangcao-busheng-ls1u8dmuvs5bbrzadf2dtd00js-p]]
- **Early Han consolidation:** During the Chu-Han struggle, [[LiuBang|刘邦]] moved his capital to [[LiyangQinHan|栎阳]] and took Longxi, then Beidi after defeating [[ZhangPingQinGeneral|章平]], securing a western rear base. After [[Feiqiu|废丘]] and [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]]'s last holdout fell, Han pacified former Yong territory and set [[ZhongdiCommandery|中地郡]], Beidi and Longxi as commanderies: acquisition and administrative conversion are distinct stages. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-149-xichu-bawang-xiangyu-weihe-shijun-2-lpohbb3prsgvokpjipfd7i1ntiwn]] [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-155-1-lishi-jizai-shenme-qingkuang-hui-fasheng-ren-chi-ren-canju-lrwz-prnxd8hzchagyrbywkux7lh]]
- **Late-Han rebellion:** Much later, [[HanSui|韩遂]] absorbed other Liangzhou rebel forces and attacked Longxi; its administrator [[LiXiangruLateHan|李相如]] joined the rebellion. This illustrates the collapse of formal border administration under rebel pressure, not continuity with Qin or early Han policy. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-937-zhanshen-zhisi-haoren-zongshi-duozai-duonan-lp7przsmag9ookq7eoisvsmnm5aw]]

## Qualifications
Qin, early-Han and late-Han uses of the commandery name span changing administrative boundaries. The proposed purpose of the 220 BCE itinerary is an episode interpretation.

## What Changed
- 楚汉占郡与东汉韩遂进攻的记载，使陇西不再只限于秦灭义渠后的边防叙述。

## Relationships
- [[ConquestCommanderyTransition]] - 刘邦平定故雍地后将陇西列入郡制，是先占领、后行政设置的楚汉案例，不等同秦初建郡。
- [[FrontierCommandCollapse]] - 东汉末韩遂攻陇西、郡守李相如附叛，是边郡官治随军事叛乱失效的具体表现。
- [[GuanzhongRegion]] - 楚汉相争时陇西纳入刘邦关中西部后方，不能把陇西本身等同关中核心平原。
- [[LiuBang]] - 刘邦定都栎阳后取得陇西，废丘陷落后又将故雍地郡县化。
- [[QinEastwardPressure]] - 秦灭义渠后陇西与北地、上郡构成西北后防，使东向用兵得以减少后顾之忧；并非陇西郡向东进攻。
- [[QinShiHuang]] - 秦始皇前220年巡行经过陇西；赴雍城祭祖的目的仍是节目推断。
