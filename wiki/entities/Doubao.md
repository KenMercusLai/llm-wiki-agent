## Overview
Doubao is [[ByteDance]]'s consumer AI assistant and model family. The notes describe large Chinese consumer reach, praised voice interaction, proposed paid tiers, enterprise-office packaging, possible commerce transactions, creative use and a limited in-car integration. These surfaces do not establish one proven monetization model: every additional inference can incur cost, and ordering, retention and trust require more than a chat audience.

## Current Profile
A 2025 discussion puts Doubao above 100 million monthly active users, while another podcast asks whether a reported 200 million daily users are an asset or liability. These are different dates and metrics, not a comparable growth series or audited numbers. The important split is between inexpensive distribution of questions, higher-value completion of work or purchases, and the enterprise systems needed to turn a model into a governed agent. The strongest source-grounded examples are voice quality as a guest judgment, store-grounded visual limitations, a creator's prompt workflow, merchant-reported hotel fees and Tesla China's information-only cockpit app.

## Key Characteristics
- Traffic leadership in consumer chat gives ByteDance reach but exposes a cost-and-retention problem when users switch easily and every query consumes inference resources.
- Voice and video-oriented product fit may differentiate Doubao from harder text, image, API and coding tasks, though outside evaluations disagree by use case.
- Assistant-to-service entry tests buying and booking through the Douyin ecosystem; ranking neutrality, completed-order fees and merchant adoption are unresolved.
- Feishu and enterprise edition could convert assistant/model capability into work agents with organizational data, permissions and commercial budgets.
- ByteDance's paid-acquisition system cannot simply purchase lasting assistant retention; task value, model quality, CAC/LTV and compute cost must all clear the growth test.
- Narrow creation and device cases show utility under human review or restricted authority, not end-to-end autonomy.

## Qualifications
- MAU, DAU, CAC, revenue contribution, RMB 68 and token-cost figures are different source-reported or hypothetical metrics; a rumored subscription is not a confirmed launch. The 12% merchant fee is disputed by Doubao's statement about payment and promotion, and booking accuracy or neutral ranking is unproven.
- The math note only lists Doubao among peers; Yao's voice praise is a personal evaluation, not a benchmark. Generic image generation cannot be assumed to solve store inventory, while the flower-shop experiment is not all offline retail.
- The short-drama results include platform distribution and later failed approvals; the in-car app cannot control the car. The commerce examples, including milk tea, are aspirations or brief news mentions, not proof of widespread assistant-executed purchases.
- [[kafeidou-he-niu-ziyou-cheng-zizhu-canting-maidian-guijia-guanghuan-cong-he-er-lai-1004978054]] primarily covers wagyu; its Doubao and Douyin mentions should not be made into evidence of measured shopping conversion. The growth interviews are recollections, not verified company telemetry.

## What Changed
- Consumer reach is weighed against marginal cost and retention rather than taken as proof of a profitable assistant business.
- Commerce, enterprise workflow and limited creator/car applications are separated by actual authority and observed evidence.
