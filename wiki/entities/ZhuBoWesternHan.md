## Overview

朱博 / Zhu Bo is synthesized as a late [[WesternHanDynasty|Western Han]] official whose rise and fall both turn office procedure into power politics. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-578-quanli-jiaofeng-shi-renshi-biangdong-zui-mingan-lnnrrbggajjfulskbig4kmgnif5s|Hanji 578]] first shows him as Da Sikong proposing restoration of the Yushi Dafu office, giving [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] an institutional way to reset promotion sequence and place [[DingMingWesternHan|丁明]] in the Da Sima field. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-580-huigu-sizhou-shuishi-nide-kaoshan-liid3tymhojmajkyeqoq6vryprhz|Hanji 580]] adds two Jianping 2 follow-ups: Zhu Bo and [[ZhaoXuanWesternHan|赵璇]] try unsuccessfully to strip [[WangMang|王莽]] of title and fief, and Zhu Bo then persuades Aidi to abolish州牧 and restore刺史 because high provincial rank has weakened oversight incentives. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-582-nvren-de-chenhenxin-zui-duo-nanren-ming-ljiuneown6ah1yx5g_uchpb5escp|Hanji 582]] then shows him as chancellor caught between formal court responsibility and [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]]'s private anger at [[FuXiWesternHan|傅喜]], where tactical cleverness fails once an impeachment memorial is legible as patron-driven revenge.

## Current Profile

Hanji 578 gives Zhu Bo an earlier institutional-design role. As Da Sikong, he argues that abolishing the Yushi Dafu office has left officials uncertain about responsibility and rank. His solution is to restore Yushi Dafu as a promotion bridge: capable local and commandery officials can be tested, raised to two-thousand-stone status, considered for Yushi Dafu, and then become plausible chancellor candidates. Zhu Bo also says he is willing to be moved down into the restored post, which the episode reads as a tactical route toward the chancellor line rather than mere self-demotion.

The same source makes Zhu Bo part of a broader personnel reset. Aidi accepts the recommendation, makes Zhu Bo Yushi Dafu, and appoints Ding Ming as Da Sima Wei General with subordinate officials. Zhu Bo later joins Fu Taihou-aligned officials in attacking [[KongGuangWesternHan|孔光]], whose earlier succession advice and anti-Fu position make him vulnerable. This layer ties Zhu Bo to [[OfficeSequencePowerReallocation|官职序列重组式权力再分配]] before his later collapse.

Hanji 580 adds Zhu Bo's next institutional and factional moves. He and Zhao Xuan argue that Wang Mang's earlier Da Sima status makes his opposition to Aidi's filial-title settlement especially serious, but Aidi refuses to reduce Wang Mang to commoner status because Wang Zhengjun remains Wang Mang's protective patron. Zhu Bo then turns to provincial oversight, arguing that州牧 have become too high-ranking and self-protective to serve the sharper inspection function that lower-status刺史 once performed; Aidi accepts the reversal.

Hanji 582 says Zhu Bo is approached through Kongxiang Hou after Fu Taihou remains dissatisfied with Fu Xi's existing punishment. Zhu Bo consults Zhao Xuan, who warns that Aidi has already settled the matter and that reopening it is improper, but Zhu Bo says he has already promised Kongxiang Hou and will act for Fu Taihou even at risk.

His chosen tactic is not open defiance. Zhu Bo tries to reduce exposure by bundling Fu Xi with [[HeWuWesternHan|何武]], another dismissed senior official who still retains rank and fief. The design is to make the memorial look like a general rule about former officials rather than a targeted strike against Fu Xi.

The strategy fails because Aidi already knows Fu Taihou's resentment. Zhao Xuan confesses under inquiry, [[PengXuanWesternHan|彭宣]] and central officials review the case, and the court classifies Zhu Bo, Zhao Xuan, and Kongxiang Hou as guilty of grave disrespect. Zhao Xuan and Kongxiang Hou receive lesser outcomes, while Zhu Bo is summoned to Tingwei custody and commits suicide. His case therefore sits near [[CourtLegitimacyOverPatronLoyalty|朝廷合法性优先于恩主忠诚]]: serving the patron's will becomes indefensible once the ruler reads the hidden intent.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han senior official who moves from Da Sikong to restored Yushi Dafu and then chancellor under Han Aidi.
- Uses office-restoration argument to define a promotion route toward chancellorship.
- Participates in the personnel pressure against Kong Guang and later fails to strip Wang Mang's title and fief because Wang Zhengjun's patronage limits the purge.
- Reverses the州牧 reform by arguing that high provincial rank weakens inspection incentives.
- Agrees to pursue Fu Taihou's demand for harsher punishment of Fu Xi and uses He Wu as a bundled comparison target to disguise the memorial's real focus.
- Misjudges Aidi's ability to infer the backchannel from known court context.
- Becomes the most severe casualty of the Fu Xi case, committing suicide when summoned for trial.

## Qualifications

The page is bounded to Hanji 578, Hanji 580, and Hanji 582. Hanji 578 supports Zhu Bo's office-restoration proposal and participation in Kong Guang's removal, but not a full San Gong institutional history or Zhu Bo's private motive. Hanji 580 supports the failed Wang Mang attack and州牧-to-刺史 reversal, but not the full provincial-office history or Zhao Xuan's wider role. Hanji 582 supports Zhu Bo's role in the Fu Xi impeachment attempt, his bundling tactic, and his suicide, but not a full biography, Zhao Xuan's full confession text, or the precise legal formula behind the charges.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 580's failed anti-Wang-Mang memorial and州牧-to-刺史 reversal between Hanji 578 and Hanji 582.
- Reframed Zhu Bo as a figure whose tactical use of formal procedure advances him, lets him reshape oversight incentives, and later destroys him.
