# 吴立伟 / Wu Liwei

## Overview
吴立伟 / Wu Liwei: Wu Liwei reports apparel production as a supply-chain and flexible-manufacturing problem.

## Current Profile
Wu Liwei reports apparel production as a supply-chain and flexible-manufacturing problem. His fragrance reporting separates industrial scent construction from the objects scents evoke. His hosting brings journalists’ reporting labor and field practice into conversation. His food reporting uses questions about regional recognition and creative cuisine rather than treating novelty as sufficient.

## Key Characteristics
- Wu Liwei reports apparel production as a supply-chain and flexible-manufacturing problem.
- His fragrance reporting separates industrial scent construction from the objects scents evoke.
- His hosting brings journalists’ reporting labor and field practice into conversation.
- His food reporting uses questions about regional recognition and creative cuisine rather than treating novelty as sufficient.

## Evidence
- **Wu Liwei reports apparel production as a supply-chain and flexible-manufacturing problem:** In a factory-reporting discussion, [[WuLiwei|吴立伟]] describes [[YagorGroup|雅戈尔]]'s vertical control and custom-suit data: functional fabrics, order scheduling, computerized cutting and [[SmallOrderQuickResponse|small-order quick response]] tie demand to [[FlexibleManufacturing|flexible production]]. He also notes the capital limits of smart-factory upgrades, rather than promising every firm the same route. [[ep271-tanfang-rouxing-zhizao-gongchang-shi-shui-rang-ni-xiangyao-jiu-neng-maidao-lqyrioptggnbb8hl-llej0-5ow0v]]
- **His fragrance reporting separates industrial scent construction from the objects scents evoke:** He explains that a perfume can simulate a named smell without literally containing that material, then connects [[ChineseFragrance|中式香气]] and brands such as [[Guanxia|观夏]] to [[FragranceProductStorytelling|product storytelling]] and [[ScentAsBrandMemory|scent memory]]. These are observations about formulation and marketing, not proof of literal ingredients. [[ep263-xianxiang-jueqi-faxian-zhongshi-xiangqi-de-meili-lq1lsqcprqyr7auo5gslwyymuiv]]
- **His hosting brings journalists’ reporting labor and field practice into conversation:** Hosting a [[XianchangXianchang|现场！现场！]] special, he asks [[QinSi|秦思]], [[ChengJing|程静]] and [[WeiZhaoyang|魏昭阳]] about field access, international reporting, source trust, emotional pressure and audience feedback. Their accounts supply the experiences; his role is to frame and question them. [[xianchang-xianchang-2025-nianzhong-teji-sanlian-nvjizhe-jiemi-xinwen-beihou-de-ku-yu-guang-loz-sggelhx166n2me8v6d8k9a3c]]
- **His food reporting uses questions about regional recognition and creative cuisine rather than treating novelty as sufficient:** As [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] host for [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]]'s “new flavor” issue, he asks why [[GuangxiCuisineLegibility|Guangxi cuisine]] has less visibility than Guizhou, Yunnan or Sichuan and how [[CreativeChineseCuisine|creative Chinese cuisine]] and [[ChineseBistroLocalization|Chinese bistros]] can retain recognizable regional grammar while combining food and drink. He did not develop the dishes or assert that novelty alone succeeds. [[ep245-yinian-yidu-xunwei-zhilv-jinnian-qu-chi-chuangyi-cai-li9wtavemokcckialbbj7unpmqsz]]

## Qualifications
His questions and framing are reporting roles; guest opinions and industry outcomes are not automatically his own findings.

## What Changed
- His reporting across manufacturing, fragrance and food is presented as distinct editorial roles, without turning interviewees’ views into his personal findings.
