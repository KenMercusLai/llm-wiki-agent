# 北宫伯玉 / Beigong Boyu (late Han)

北宫伯玉 / Beigong Boyu (late Han) enters through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-937-zhanshen-zhisi-haoren-zongshi-duozai-duonan-lp7przsmag9ookq7eoisvsmnm5aw|Hanji 937]] as one of the Liang州 rebel figures whose force is absorbed by [[HanSui|韩遂]] before Han Sui attacks [[LongxiCommandery|陇西郡]].

The page is source-scoped to that consolidation layer. Beigong Boyu is important here because his removal from the rebel leadership field helps explain why Han Sui can become the more visible western power before the later [[WangGuoLateHan|王国]] and [[MaTeng|马腾]] sequence.
