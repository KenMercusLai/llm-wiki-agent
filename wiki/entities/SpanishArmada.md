# Spanish Armada

The Spanish Armada is already backgrounded in [[England]] through [[14-wushi-weilian-dahanghai-shidai-de-riben-he-xifang-555771285]], where the 1588 conflict helps form [[WilliamAdams|William Adams / 三浦按针]] as an English sailor before his later Japan voyage.

[[696-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-killing-the-queen-of-scots-part-6-glt2334035496]] adds the Elizabethan political-security prelude. The episode links [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]]' 1587 execution to the escalation that followed, especially Philip II's 1588 attempt to invade [[England]].

In the new source, the Armada is not presented as caused by Mary's execution alone. It is the wider [[ConfessionalWarEscalation]] endpoint after the [[BabingtonPlot]], English Protestant security fears, Catholic succession politics, and Anglo-Spanish conflict reinforce each other.

- [[England]] and [[Spain]] - opposing powers in the 1588 escalation.
