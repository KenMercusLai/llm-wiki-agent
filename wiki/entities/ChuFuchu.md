# 熊负刍 / 楚王负刍 / Xiong Fuchu

熊负刍 / 楚王负刍 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-1-jintian-liaoliao-yingzheng-dou-you-naxie-canren-shouduan-lmqky6g3qi9nklpdnye4lk5rrbz6]] as the older half-brother who replaces [[ChuAiwang|熊昊 / 熊犹 / 楚哀王]] after [[ChuYouwang|楚幽王]] dies. The episode says Fuchu's faction launches the coup before the newly installed ruler can stabilize power.

The source does not yet develop Fuchu's reign. His wiki role here is the coup endpoint of a late Chu succession chain: after the [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] and [[LiYuanSister|李源妹妹]] branch already compromised the throne's legitimacy, another royal relative can use factional violence to redirect the line.
