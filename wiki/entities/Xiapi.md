# 下邳 / Xiapi

## Overview
下邳 / Xiapi: In the Chu–Han era Xiapi was a rear-logistics target and then Han Xin’s Chu capital.

## Current Profile
In the Chu–Han era Xiapi was a rear-logistics target and then Han Xin’s Chu capital. Under Tao Qian, the Xiapi grain route furnished resources later diverted by Ze Rong. In late Han politics the city changed hands through betrayal, resisted revolt, and fell to Cao Cao after a 198 CE siege.

## Key Characteristics
- In the Chu–Han era Xiapi was a rear-logistics target and then Han Xin’s Chu capital.
- Under Tao Qian, the Xiapi grain route furnished resources later diverted by Ze Rong.
- In late Han politics the city changed hands through betrayal, resisted revolt, and fell to Cao Cao after a 198 CE siege.

## Evidence
- **In the Chu–Han era Xiapi was a rear-logistics target and then Han Xin’s Chu capital:** [[PengYue|彭越]] attacked the [[XiangYu|项羽]] rear around Xiapi near [[Xuzhou|彭城]] and its river connections; threatening that [[TransportCorridorSeverance|transport corridor]] drew Xiang Yu away from the [[XingyangStandoff|荥阳]] front, allowing [[LiuBang|刘邦]] to retake [[Chenggao|成皋]]. This was Chu–Han maneuvering, not the later flooding siege. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-162-2-liubang-ruhe-shoushi-wei-gao-quan-zhong-de-xiashu-lqvye2zmqhu3otk0uwu7lakf2prz]] In the founding settlement, Liu Bang moved [[HanXin|韩信]] from Qi kingship to Chu kingship with Xiapi as capital; the narrator interprets this as [[HostileTerritoryRoyalContainment|placing a powerful general in possibly hostile territory]], not evidence that Lü Bu held the city in that period. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-169-pandian-liubang-dengjiqian-buweirenzhi-de-gushi-2-lqq8qhramfzii5o5icesd-uagwkc]]
- **Under Tao Qian, the Xiapi grain route furnished resources later diverted by Ze Rong:** In a late-Han account, [[TaoQianLateHan|陶谦]] put [[ZeRong|笮融]] in charge of transport grain across [[GuanglingCommandery|广陵]], Xiapi and Pengcheng and appointed him Xiapi chancellor. Ze Rong diverted resources to Buddhist temples, followers and ritual feasts; his subsequent killings of benefactors are attributed to him, not to Buddhism itself ([[InstrumentalizedReligiousPower|instrumentalized religion]]). [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-995-ta-zuishang-cibei-weihe-duoci-cansha-enren-llicukoezccd5iycin4uq7qun51k]]
- **In late Han politics the city changed hands through betrayal, resisted revolt, and fell to Cao Cao after a 198 CE siege:** In 196 CE [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s attack drew [[LiuBei|刘备]] away; [[ZhangFei|张飞]] killed [[CaoBaoLateHan|曹豹]] while guarding Xiapi, [[XuDanLateHan|许耽]] opened its gates to [[LyuBu|吕布]], and Liu Bei's family was taken. Promised grain helped turn Lü Bu from ally to occupier ([[GrainLeverageAllianceBetrayal|grain leverage]]). [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-999-sanguo-luanshi-renxing-you-duome-cuiruo-li8tz30elca7bnipei6dfqdxmmeu]] That year [[HaoMeng|郝萌]] attacked Lü Bu's headquarters at night, forcing him to flee to [[GaoShun|高顺]] until the revolt was put down; [[CaoXingLateHan|曹性]]'s allegations and Lü Bu's treatment of [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] reveal [[LyuBuFactionalBalance|factional fragility]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1000-hao-meng-panluan-jingbi-lvbu-fanqiang-taopao-lluthd9hv-0nqbot4fuctjcuka68]] [[ChenDeng|陈登]] later assisted [[CaoCao|曹操]] in the campaign against Lü Bu; the city's fall cannot be reduced to his actions alone. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1017-sanguo-yincang-yingxiong-chendeng-de-chuanqi-wangshi-lhcvtm8fobuzjfyo9uirdcgx2plq]] Cao's blockade thwarted Lü Bu's attempt to send a daughter to [[YuanShu|袁术]] to revive a marriage/rescue bargain; [[XunYou|荀攸]] and [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] argued against withdrawing, and Cao diverted the Si and Yi rivers to flood Xiapi ([[HydraulicSiegeWarfare|hydraulic siege]]). [[HouChengLateHan|侯成]], [[SongXianLateHan|宋宪]] and [[WeiXuLateHan|魏续]] then seized Chen Gong and Gao Shun from within, turning suspicion into [[SuspicionTriggeredCommandMutiny|mutiny]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1025-bai-yu-caocao-lvbu-cuo-zai-jinjiuling-ljjt1t2mk77si6re-wew-hpl8qz]] After surrender, Lü Bu, Chen Gong and Gao Shun were executed while others, including [[ZhangLiao|张辽]], were incorporated; the host contrasts punishment with [[StrategicDefectorIncorporation|settlement]] after the siege. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1026-jiemi-sanguo-di-yi-mengjiang-lvbu-de-luomu-lhjf-agujuliglqchumtff3tftxg1]]
