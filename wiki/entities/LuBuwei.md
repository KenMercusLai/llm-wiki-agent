# 吕不韦 / Lu Buwei

## Overview
本页按主题区分有出处的事件、解释及其局限。 阳翟商人吕不韦以子楚为政治投资对象，借华阳夫人无子与玉符收养安排改变继承机会。

## Current Profile
阳翟商人吕不韦以子楚为政治投资对象，借华阳夫人无子与玉符收养安排改变继承机会。 子楚即位后任相、灭东周与受封；秦王政年少时以仲父执政，郑国渠又涉及其粮食与灌溉考量。 向赵姬引入嫪毐后，风险转移变成宫廷权力危机，罢相又改变客卿政治环境。 李斯通过吕不韦入仕，而退居洛阳仍有宾客交往，最终收到迁蜀令并于235 BCE饮鸩。 秦孝文王死因及嬴政生父传闻不能被投资叙事推导为事实。

## Evidence
- **Succession investment:** The host first foreshadows 吕不韦 when [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] appoints [[QinXiaowenwang|安国君柱]] heir despite his more than twenty sons. A merchant from 阳翟, Lu then meets the impoverished Qin hostage [[QinZhuangxiangwang|异人 / 子楚]] in [[Handan|邯郸]] and calls him “奇货”: [[PoliticalVentureInvestment|concentrated political investment]] in a low-status grandson whose prospects might change. Because the favored [[HuayangFuren|华阳夫人]] has no son, he exploits [[MaternalStatusSuccessionLeverage|maternal-status succession leverage]], funding Yiren's reputation and Qin access, persuading [[YangquanJun|阳泉君]] and Huayang Furen, and formalizing adoption through a carved jade [[WrittenSuccessionCredential|tally]]. He gives [[ZhaoJi|赵姬]] to Yiren; the claim that her son [[QinShiHuang|嬴政]] was biologically Lu's remains doubtful in the source, not established. The episode contrasts his calculation with [[LuZhonglian|鲁仲连]]'s aristocratic refusal of payment. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-91-chulong-ruhe-qiaokai-shougua-zhaotaihou-de-men-1-lmgd7kdl1nrfwcrtkqttumtlpqok]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-3-lvbuwei-lishishang-fengtou-di-yi-ren-lswa9b864oc-s8vaxtdu15arydu2]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-4-shishang-wei-shiye-song-laopo-diyi-ren-lo8omg01gkwlojkpiflaep99x-vz]]
- **Chancellorship and state capacity:** After Zichu's accession as [[QinZhuangxiangwang|秦庄襄王]], Lu became chancellor. One episode gives his lineage as 姜姓、吕氏、名不韦, from Puyang in Wey and, by its account only, a twenty-third-generation descendant of [[JiangTaigong|姜太公]]. He was sent to eliminate [[EasternZhouPolity|东周国]] after its anti-Qin plot, moved its ruler to Yangren, and received the Wenxin Hou title with ten myriads of households around [[Luoyi|洛邑 / 洛阳]]. After Zhuangxiang died, thirteen-year-old Ying Zheng succeeded; Lu as “仲父” directed affairs with an unusually large guest network despite being neither royal kin nor a queen-mother relative. In the [[ZhengGuoCanal|郑国渠]] episode, [[HanState|韩国]]'s attempt to exhaust Qin through [[ZhengGuoHydraulicEngineer|郑国]] aligned with Lu's existing Guanzhong irrigation and wartime grain-security agenda; the host presents an enemy diversion converted to [[QinState|秦国]] capacity. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-1-lvbuwei-shi-jiangziya-houren-lveg-wsmggroaxjsnxopaevexqod]] [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-103-2-simaqiande-idol-weihe-siyu-jiuse-lvmqg1ojmka9pqagfhgvo4albpjx]] [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-104-yinmou-shisuan-rang-qinguo-bantu-you-you-you-pengzhang-le-lgnyhbduqutegtksrjwcvhmn8-t6]]
- **Palace scandal and removal:** As Ying Zheng neared personal rule, Lu feared exposure of his earlier relationship with Zhao Ji. He let rumors stimulate her interest in [[LaoAi|嫪毐]] and arranged a sham-eunuch palace entry; this solved his immediate intimacy problem but gave Lao Ai title, retainers and influence before his rebellion. Afterward the investigation implicated Lu: the episode says Ying Zheng wanted to kill him, but his prior merit, intercession and the king's incomplete consolidation spared him. He lost the chancellorship and left the capital for his fief, an instance of [[PalaceScandalPowerConsolidation|scandal facilitating political removal]]. Qin royal-clan officials then advanced the [[GuestExpulsionOrder|逐客令]], casting outsiders and Lu's former retainer world as possible infiltrators; this is a political sequel, not proof all guests were disloyal. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-108-1-lishi-gan-dangmian-dingzhuang-qinshihuang-diyi-ren-lg0dssyicp387foiraohkv1x5aij]] [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-108-2-laoai-de-hougong-mishi-lof4xuuxfkehm9igpdvihv7exqdy]] [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-108-3-huangtaihou-he-qingfu-laoai-qingse-beihou-de-qishi-lthiqkpc0nbnlkxhcmivufvgl25b]] [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-110-1-lisi-micang-laoshu-he-xiashuidao-laoshu-ni-zenme-xuan-lmv-fw-kgsuokonssq8bkbfvdsb]]
- **Networks and final exile:** [[LiSi|李斯]] first used Lu as his path toward Ying Zheng; Lu later recommended him as a 郎官. Yet retired in Luoyang, Lu still attracted foreign envoys and could not appear politically absent. Ying Zheng's brief letter repudiated merit, kinship and “仲父” language and ordered his family to Shu. The episode interprets Lu's 235 BCE poisoned-wine suicide as an exit trap: return was impossible, flight endangered kin and compliance could defer rather than avert death. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-110-2-qinshihuang-de-cp-weihe-bei-quzhu-chuguo-lj0222vw8ijbvj25iwfzh-qhvzr9]] [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-111-2-wo-yu-lvbuwei-de-gekong-duihua-ltpbkg1echouxwjyhsfax2swfzch]]
- **Unresolved origin and death suspicions:** [[QinXiaowenwang|秦孝文王]]'s sudden death made a Lu Buwei murder theory tempting because a longer reign would delay Zichu's succession. The episode also weighs physical weakness, prolonged crown-prince pressure and mourning exhaustion, declining to establish murder under [[QinXiaowenwangDeathUncertainty|death-cause uncertainty]]; neither that suspicion nor the biological-paternity rumor follows from the investment narrative. ## Qualifications
Neither the Ying Zheng paternity rumor nor a murder theory concerning Qin Xiaowen Wang is established. The episode’s venture-capital framing is an analogy, not a historical institution.

## What Changed
- 从子楚继位安排到秦廷相权、嫪毐危机与退居洛阳，人物画像不再只是“奇货可居”；两项传闻仍未获确证。

## Relationships
- [[GenderedHistoriographicalDesireFraming]] - 吕不韦引嫪毐入赵姬宫廷的叙事受《史记》对女性欲望的写法影响，须区分政治安排与贬抑性修辞。
- [[HistoricalDetectiveReasoning]] - 对子楚收养玉符、嬴政生父传闻及孝文王猝死嫌疑须分别核对证据，不能由吕的获益反推其必然犯罪。
- [[MerchantAristocraticValueConflict]] - 节目以吕不韦投资子楚，对照鲁仲连拒平原君封赏，呈现功利交易与士人自持的价值张力。
- [[PowerExitTrap]] - 吕失相后仍有宾客交往，嬴政迁蜀令使他在服从、逃亡与家人安危间难以脱身；235 BCE饮鸩是其结局。
- [[SelfDefeatingResourceDrainStrategy]] - 韩国欲以郑国渠耗秦，吕不韦却把工程纳入关中灌溉及军粮考量，使疲敌之计反增秦力。
- [[WangYuSouthernDynasties]] - 节目以王玉的后世被迫死亡故事类比吕不韦的退场困境，并无两人同世或直接往来。
