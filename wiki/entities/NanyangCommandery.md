# 南阳郡 / Nanyang Commandery

## Overview
A recurring commandery name across Qin, the Qin collapse, and late Han; its administrative boundaries and military context must not be presumed identical.

## Current Profile
The Qin commandery is described around Wan after territorial gains near Chu; later episodes use the same name for Song Liu’s failed surrender, Liu Bang’s negotiated passage and several late-Han campaigns. These are separate administrative and wartime contexts, not one continuous jurisdiction.

## Key Characteristics
- Qin territorial conversion.
- Surrender policy and westward passage.
- Late-Han insurgency and command.
- Contested Jingzhou border.

## Evidence
- **秦的郡县化：** 战国末秦取得南阳后设郡，节目把它定位于此前魏让予秦、又牵涉楚北部新占土地的交界，治所为宛（今河南南阳附近）；这个带有“可能”限定的定位，说明秦如何把战果纳入郡制，可与[[NanCommandery|南郡]]、[[BeidiCommandery|北地郡]]比较，而不等于三郡边界相同。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-85-xuantaihou-weihe-sitong-30-nian-hou-ansha-le-laoqingren-lg7zy6vqtdjcufou4-mox5qkg2ct5]]
- **秦末招降与西进后方：** [[SongLiu|宋刘]]为[[ChenSheng|陈胜]]夺取南阳、进逼武关，陈胜死后在秦复得该郡时投降，却遭[[QinErshi|秦二世]]车裂；节目以此说明[[PunitiveSurrenderDeterrence|惩罚降者]]可能妨碍以后招降。相反，[[LiuBang|刘邦]]败郡守后本欲绕过[[WanCounty|宛县]]，[[ZhangLiang|张良]]警告后路受袭，遂接受[[ChenHuiNanyang|陈恢]]的建议、赏郡守和陈恢，保障西进后路，并为[[OccupationPacificationStrategy|怀柔治理]]与[[PoliticalSurrenderCascade|招降示范]]创造条件；这不是宋刘的同一次投降。[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-129-4-jiuquangui-zhangliang-ruhe-zai-liubang-shouxia-anshen-lp6qwg3ma5hdy15g4box7pe23jav]] [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-136-meinv-xijiao-liubang-ye-hao-zhe-kouer-lplgzwzahepwx9p7krfc4x0cnz4o]]
- **黄巾军与后续地方叛乱：** 184年南阳太守褚贡被[[ZhangManchengLateHan|张曼成]]所杀，张据地逾百日，六月为[[QinJieLateHan|秦颉]]所杀；残部续由[[ZhaoHongLateHan|赵弘]]、[[HanZhongLateHan|韩忠]]、[[SunXiaLateHan|孙夏]]率领，[[ZhuJunLateHan|朱儁]]调整围城和纳降策略，秦颉斩韩忠。其后185—186年的[[ZhaoCiLateHan|赵慈]]江夏之乱杀秦颉，荆州刺史王敏平乱：太守之死属于另一场叛乱。[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-927-zuo-shengyi-de-zuigao-jingjie-shi-shenme-lngfbjn6xui9k-9lp-ihdqkhpbpr]] [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-930-ruhe-pochu-tanxin-he-buganxin-lkyysh5jbgxwjq8ayrfar4gsjdcd]] [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-936-luanshi-xian-sha-shengmu-xin-zhehua-dui-ma-lml-kuyc7yys2ijh2kt46pwnugar]]
- **荆州北境争夺：** 196年[[ZhangJiLateHan|张济]]入[[Jingzhou|荆州]]攻南阳[[Rangcheng|穰城]]求立足而战死；[[LiuBiao|刘表]]以吊唁而非庆功安抚、收编余众，[[ZhangXiu|张绣]]继兵屯宛。节目认为刘表虽控制荆州北部许多地方，南阳仍是争夺地带。[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1010-sanguo-liubiao-jiujing-shibushi-piaoliang-de-caobao-lo58hzfo-qv0soauogtgfrkourrd]]

## Qualifications
- The Qin/Warring States commandery and later-Han Nanyang share a name, not necessarily fixed borders. The source’s Chu-region identification is explicitly tentative; Qin Jie’s death belongs to the later Zhao Ci revolt, not the Yellow Turban fight.

## What Changed
- The same place-name now holds distinct Qin administrative, Qin-Han surrender, and late-Han crisis profiles; no unbroken jurisdiction is inferred.
- Song Liu’s punitive execution and Chen Hui’s negotiated Wan settlement mark contrary surrender policies.
