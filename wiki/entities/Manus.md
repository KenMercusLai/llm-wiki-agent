# Manus

## Overview
Manus is an AI agent product discussed as a task-executing application, an [[AIAgentOverseasCommercialization|overseas commercialization]] case, and a benchmark for competing agent designs including [[HermesAgent]] and [[OpenClaw]]. The acquisition by [[Meta]] is **reported by a podcast**, not independently established here.

## Current Profile
Its attributed value is completing browser- and tool-mediated workflows rather than just generating slides or media. Interviews distinguish its project/session orientation from persistent personal and organizational context; observers disagree about how defensible that product lead is.

## Key Characteristics
- Task decomposition, browser/sandbox operation and workflow completion are the central product claims.
- Overseas access to web data, model capability and paying software customers shapes the commercialization argument.
- Manus serves as a comparison for memory, harness architecture, pricing and AI-native workspaces.
- Acquisition discussion and model-platform integration are source claims and conditional forecasts, not verified operating outcomes.

## Qualifications
The Keji Luandun account claims Meta/Facebook bought Manus and describes Singapore moves and reduced domestic availability, but it supplies no independently verified transaction terms or explanation of account changes. Its [[ChineseModelLiberalArtsConstraint]] thesis comes from practitioner impressions, not benchmarking; model-provider competition including [[OpenManus]] remains a forecast. [[QuKai]]'s [[AIApplicationMarketTrough]] assessment and comparisons with [[Genspark]], [[Dify]], [[Workmagic]] and [[Aestudio]] do not establish their finances or Manus's.
