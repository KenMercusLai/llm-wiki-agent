# 魏王假 / King Jia of Wei

魏王假 / King Jia of Wei enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-2-yanguo-cisha-qinshihuang-weihe-hui-xuan-jingke-lsewjrmgup1yanf8ipn-i0ykqwjx]] as the successor to [[WeiJingminWang|魏景湣王 / 魏增]]. The episode mentions his accession only briefly before turning to [[YanState|燕国]] and [[JingKe|荆轲]].

The source-scoped point is Wei's weakness, not Jia's personal policy. By this stage of the Qinji sequence, [[WeiState|魏国]] is described as unable to reverse its position under [[QinState|秦国]] pressure, making the old hezong repair option look too slow for [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]].
