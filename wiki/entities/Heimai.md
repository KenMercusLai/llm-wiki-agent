# 黑麦 / Heimai

## Overview
黑麦 is a [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] culture and lifestyle writer and a participant in [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] discussions. His reporting and conversation move between food, wine, sports, and consumer technology, using concrete bodily experience to test larger claims about taste and usefulness.

## Current Profile
His food writing connects sensory judgment to production, geography, technique, and the eater's life; in sports and AI hardware he likewise attends to whether a format actually works in a particular setting. The recorded conversations are not evidence that every position voiced by a multi-person panel is his own.

## Key Characteristics
- **Experiential food and wine reporting.** A liquor-factory childhood, Italian-restaurant kitchen work, private-kitchen practice, wine study, and winery interviews inform his treatment of [[Wine|wine]] as agricultural culture rather than a luxury credential.
- **Cuisine innovation within recognizable grammar.** His Sichuan examples show new soups, brines, yogurt marinades, low-temperature treatment, thick-cut tongue, and aroma extraction without erasing [[SichuanCuisine|川菜]] identity.
- **Production-side food curiosity.** In a discussion of trout, eel, and foie gras he helps shift attention from appetite to feed, water, processing, packaging, labeling, and animal welfare.
- **Participatory sports lens.** His first experience of [[Padel|板式网球]] in Bali and later China reporting use an easy-to-start yet spatially demanding sport to explain social access to niche urban activities.
- **Use-case test for AI hardware.** As host, he frames tennis robots, [[AWEChina|AWE]] appliances, smart beds, and food/drink recommendations around real utility versus merely attaching an AI label.

## Qualifications
These are examples from interviews and panels, not a comprehensive biography or independent product evaluation. The wine account resists prestige-based taste hierarchies without claiming every natural wine or local wine is good. The foie gras discussion does not resolve [[FoieGrasWelfareTradeoff|the welfare tradeoff]]; easier sourcing or lower mortality is not proof that force-feeding is harmless. The AI panel's privacy and child-companionship concerns are collective discussion points, not all individually attributable to Heimai.

## What Changed
- The food profile now connects pleasure and sensory writing to production transparency, technique, and welfare rather than treating them as separate episode additions.
- His sports and hardware reporting is understood through firsthand use and context-specific product fit, not a general endorsement of niche sports or AI devices.
