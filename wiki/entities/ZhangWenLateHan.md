# 张温 / Zhang Wen (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] backfills Zhang Wen before Dong Zhuo's later killing of him. In 187 CE he is removed as taiwei after failing to pacify [[MaTeng|马腾]], [[HanSui|韩遂]], and related Liangzhou rebels. The same source ties him to the [[Wuhuan|乌桓]] cavalry failure: he rejects [[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]]'s request for command and assigns the force to [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]], after which supply and pay failures help turn the mobilization into a rebellion environment.

张温 / Zhang Wen (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] as the senior official [[DongZhuo|董卓]] kills after a court astrologer predicts that an important minister will die. The source says Dong Zhuo accuses Zhang Wen of secret contact with [[YuanShao|袁绍]], has him beaten to death in the marketplace, and then presents the killing as the fulfillment of the omen.

The page is source-scoped. Hanji 957 also remembers Zhang Wen as an earlier superior of Dong Zhuo and as a commander linked to campaigns against Han Sui, Bian Zhang, and Beigong Boyu. The episode's main use of him, however, is not a full career biography. Zhang Wen is the victim through whom Dong Zhuo turns private resentment, formal accusation, public violence, and celestial language into [[OmenLaunderedPurge|天象包装式清洗]].
