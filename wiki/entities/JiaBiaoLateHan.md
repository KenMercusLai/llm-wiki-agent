## Overview

Jia Biao is a late-Han party-prohibition rescuer whose current wiki role is source-bounded to [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-876-zhichang-zong-pengbi-shuoming-ni-meiyou-zhaodui-guanxi-lrjob-a07t29hdr0iogrmcsjr7qp|Hanji 876]]. He appears when open rescue of [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]], [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]], and other imprisoned士人 has become dangerous after [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]]'s removal.

## Current Profile

Hanji 876 presents Jia Biao as an actor who reads a blocked political field and chooses a leverage path rather than symbolic protest. He judges that the party-prisoner case will not resolve unless someone goes to [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] and reaches people who can actually influence [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]]. Instead of submitting a direct memorial himself, mobilizing Taixue students, or kneeling at the palace gate, he visits [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]], the empress's father and a better-positioned intercessor.

The page therefore treats Jia Biao as the source case for [[PowerStructureTargeting|权力结构定向求援]]. His importance is not that he becomes the public face of the release, but that he identifies the person whose family status, office, and court access can convert concern into action. Dou Wu's memorial and the later softening around [[WangFuLateHan|王甫]]'s interrogation make Jia Biao's intervention a hidden hinge in the first Party Prohibition release path.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Han rescuer operating inside the first Party Prohibition prison crisis.
- Field reader who understands that ordinary public pleading has become risky after Chen Fan's removal.
- Targeted intercessor who goes to Luoyang instead of staying in distant moral anxiety.
- Strategic connector who chooses Dou Wu as the actor with better access to Emperor Huan.
- Low-visibility mover whose value lies in activating the right channel rather than becoming the main memorialist.

## Qualifications

The current page is limited to Hanji 876. It does not reconstruct Jia Biao's full biography, offices, or later career. The episode summary names Jia Biao's strategy clearly but leaves one additional key intercessor unnamed; this page does not infer that identity from adjacent sources.
