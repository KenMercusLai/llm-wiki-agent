# 楼缓 / Lou Huan

## Overview
本页按主题区分有出处的事件、解释及其局限。 楼缓属于战国游说者群体，最初仅是群像中的一名。

## Current Profile
楼缓属于战国游说者群体，最初仅是群像中的一名。 楼缓接孟尝君而任秦相、约295 BCE由魏冉替代的顺序是节目据史籍校订的说法，并非《通鉴》直接接樗里疾。 长平之后“卢缓”劝赵割地求和是节目所述的避险立场，不证明秦守约。

## Evidence
- **Persuader identity:** The early account names 楼缓 alongside [[SuDai|苏代]], [[SuLi|苏厉]] and [[ZhouZui|周最]] as part of a mobile [[ZonghengjiaDiplomacy|persuader profession]] made conspicuous by [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]], [[SuQin|苏秦]] and [[GongsunYan|公孙衍]]; it gives no independent exploit for Lou in this episode. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-56-qiaoqiao-liangqian-duo-nianqian-sha-shi-da-zhangfu-ltokbutqgsnrno-y29xmzwa3da41]]
- **Contested chancellorship sequence:** Rather than accept [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]]'s direct succession from [[Chulizi|樗里疾]] to Lou, the host follows [[Shiji|《史记》]]: [[WeiRan|魏冉]] follows Chulizi around 300 BCE, [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君]] serves in 298 BCE, Lou follows in 297 BCE, and Wei Ran returns in 295 BCE. In the account of the 297 change, [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] removes Mengchangjun after a warning about his loyalty to [[QiState|齐国]], appoints Lou and imprisons his predecessor; the 295 notice closes Lou's interval. This is the episode's source-critical reconstruction, not an undisputed chronology. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-61-shui-shi-lishishang-daner-zui-fei-de-diwang-lvpoaszanascnlrmu2atinlxzmk1]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-63-degao-wangzhong-mengchangjun-shi-weijunzi-lmd0owe3rq-faponbwzhkaigprin]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-66-zhongguo-lishishang-naxie-diwang-shi-bei-huohuo-e-si-de-1-lvl1uxjadiedqwad-skxrwf-usnq]]
- **Zhao peace counsel:** After [[ChangpingBattle|长平之战]], a transcript rendering “卢缓” is identified with Lou in the episode's context. Against [[YuQing|虞卿]], he urges [[ZhaoState|赵国]] to cede promised land to [[QinState|秦国]], hoping other states will see repaired Qin-Zhao relations and refrain from exploiting Zhao's weakness. The host presents a pro-Qin, risk-avoidance argument, not evidence Qin would honor peace; the name normalization is source-scoped. ## Qualifications
The transcript’s 卢缓 spelling in the Zhao debate is treated as a contextual name rendering, not an independent biography. The chancellor succession is a source-critical correction.

## What Changed
- 秦相更替的史籍校订及长平后赵国议和立场，使最初的游说者名单提及获得具体但有争议的场景。

## Relationships
- [[ChronicleChronologyDrift]] - 楼缓任秦相的先后，《通鉴》与节目采用的《史记》序列不同；297至295 BCE的排序是史料校订而非无争议事实。
- [[LandForPeaceExhaustionTrap]] - 长平后他以“卢缓”之名劝赵割地求和，与虞卿反对以土地换取可靠和平的判断相反；不能推定秦会守约。
- [[WarringStatesCareeristMobility]] - 他从游说者群体进入秦相与赵国议和场景，显示策士跨国活动，但早期名单不能证明具体任职细节。
- [[WarringStatesPeriod]] - 他在秦相任免与长平后赵秦谈判中活动，属于战国跨国游说与相权政治。
- [[ZhaoDan]] - 长平后他向赵孝成王所在赵廷主张割地，与虞卿的避险方案发生争论；赵王最终抉择不由其建议单独证明。
