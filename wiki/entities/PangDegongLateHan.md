## Overview

庞德公 / Pang Degong is a Jingzhou scholar whose appraisal helps make [[ZhugeLiang|诸葛亮]] and [[PangTong|庞统]] visible within a reputation-sensitive elite network.

## Current Profile

Hanji 1084 presents Pang Degong as a respected evaluator whom Zhuge Liang visits deferentially. His praise does not itself create strategic ability, but it reduces uncertainty around lesser-known talent and gives later recommendation by [[SimaHuiLateHan|司马徽]] a credible social foundation.

## Key Characteristics

- Occupies an evaluator role in the Jingzhou scholar network.
- Receives Zhuge Liang's repeated respectful visits in the episode's account.
- Applies elevated reputational labels to Zhuge Liang and Pang Tong.
- Converts private judgment into socially portable recognition.
- Represents endorsement as a complement to preparation rather than a substitute for it.

## Qualifications

The profile is bounded to one popular-history episode. Exact visits, wording, labels, chronology, and the modern KOL analogy remain source-scoped; endorsement is evidence of contemporary reputation, not independent proof of every later achievement.
