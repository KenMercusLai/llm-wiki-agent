# 半拿铁

## Overview
半拿铁 is the business-history podcast of [[XiaoLei|肖磊]] and [[LiuFei|刘飞]]. Its cited episodes connect product design, distribution, capital, policy, regional institutions and audience memory across China's internet, industries and merchant histories; a literary special tests the same explanatory form outside business history.

## Current Profile
The show's recurrent method is not a single thesis about every company. It uses long arcs and contrasting cases to ask why an opportunity became a system—or failed when execution, institutions or trust did not hold. A self-reflective interview explains its research-heavy, two-host production; the episode histories remain secondary syntheses, not primary archival adjudications.

## Key Characteristics
- Research-led conversational production turns structured business history into intimate, memory-rich listening.
- Its internet series tests commercial systems against monetization, channels, regulation and community trust, not merely user growth.
- Physical-product and infrastructure histories track technical standards, supply chains, state procurement and hidden labor or safety costs.
- Regional commercial history contrasts maritime diaspora, inland finance, state-connected capital and craft clusters without assuming one growth recipe.
- Founder and platform cases foreground practical control, financing and disputed narrative over heroic origin myths.
- A cleaned-up classical-literature special uses character networks and everyday social detail without presenting fiction as a company case.

## Qualifications
- The source notes are episodes' historical syntheses; company performance numbers, ancient episodes and causal explanations require source attribution, not elevation into independently verified findings. The Shantou-versus-Shenzhen comparison, private aviation allegations and Huawei partner-control account especially need their stated limits.
- Lan Shili's criminal acquittal does not resolve every civil dispute; railway integration achievements do not erase labor or safety costs; community usage is not identical to durable profit.
- A “clean” literary retelling is an editorial reading of fiction. It neither represents the novel's complete text nor documents actual Ming households without separate evidence.

## What Changed
- The show is profiled through recurring research and comparison methods rather than nineteen source-arrival summaries.
- Counterexamples—failed platforms, regional divergence, operational costs and contested founder evidence—remain visible within each thematic branch.
