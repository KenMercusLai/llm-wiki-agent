# 赵迁 / 赵幽缪王 / Zhao Youmiu Wang

赵迁 / 赵幽缪王 / Zhao Youmiu Wang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-111-1-ruoda-de-zhaoguo-jing-wang-yu-yige-jinv-zhi-shou-lhrqb8-swlkuyoydgjyn8ldnlkzc]] as the son who succeeds [[ZhaoDaoxiangwang|赵道襄王]] in 236 BCE while [[ZhaoState|赵国]] is being hit by [[QinState|秦国]].

The source presents his accession as an internal-disorder counterpart to the external campaign. Zhao Daoxiang Wang had displaced the original crown prince [[ZhaoJia|赵嘉]] and made Zhao Qian heir because of his attachment to Zhao Qian's mother, whom the episode identifies as a woman of sex-worker background. The host says Zhao Qian's bad conduct was already known in Handan, so his accession makes Zhao's survival problem more severe.

This page is source-scoped. The episode does not narrate the full later fall of Zhao; it marks Zhao Qian's succession as the moment when weak-state infighting, Qin opportunity, and a damaged heir structure begin to converge.
