# 于扶罗 / Yufuluo Chanyu

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-969-yuanshi-xiongdi-neidou-shui-neng-jianlou-lsgj6sewo35jpsgfm-koivijtr6r|Hanji 969]] adds an earlier active role before the later death notice. The episode says [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]] and Black Mountain remnants support [[YuanShu|袁术]] when Yuan Shu is forced by [[LiuBiao|刘表]] and then pursued by [[CaoCao|曹操]], making him part of the unstable force market around the 193 CE Yuan Shu retreat.

于扶罗 / Yufuluo Chanyu is later noted in [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] as the [[SouthernXiongnu|南匈奴]] chanyu whose death is recorded near the episode's end. His younger brother [[HuchuquanChanyu|呼厨泉]] succeeds him.

The source gives only a brief annalistic notice, so this page remains source-scoped and should be expanded only when later sources develop his role directly.
