# 郗俭 / Xi Jian (late Han)

郗俭 / Xi Jian enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-940-de-minxin-zhe-de-tianxia-ni-xin-bu-xin-loipf7b0p5a-yh0rj2njnsml8a1j|Hanji 940]] as the [[YizhouLateHan|益州]] inspector killed when [[MaXiangLateHan|马相]] and [[ZhaoZhiLateHan|赵祗]] rise at [[MianzhuLateHan|绵竹]]. The source says Xi Jian had alienated the population through harsh extraction, making him a local trigger for the revolt's rapid acceptance.

His page is therefore less a biography than a negative governance signal. In the episode's people-support frame, Xi Jian shows how an official can lose practical protection before formal office disappears: once crisis arrives, the office does not produce a defendable social base.
