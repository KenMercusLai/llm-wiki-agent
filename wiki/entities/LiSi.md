# 李斯 / Li Si

李斯 / Li Si enters the wiki through the closing hook of [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-109-2-chunshenjun-bei-huangdi-dajiuge-quanzu-miemen-locma2plu7v-vsssm5dr5frc3szw]]. After completing [[Chunshenjun|春申君]]'s death and the [[WarringStatesFourLords|战国四公子]] evaluation, the episode pivots to Li Si as a low-origin figure who works hard to win [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]'s appreciation.

The source only previews the next branch: Qin orders foreign guest scholars expelled, and Li Si writes [[JianZhuKeShu|《谏逐客书》]] while preparing to leave. This page therefore records Li Si as a forthcoming Qin court actor rather than expanding beyond what this episode provides.
