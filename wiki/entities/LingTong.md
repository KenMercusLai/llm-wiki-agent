# 凌统 / Ling Tong

## Overview
凌统 / Ling Tong is an Eastern Wu officer whose profile joins a private blood feud against [[GanNing|甘宁]] to the later rear-guard trust that helps the same command structure relieve Gan Ning at Yiling.

## Current Profile
Hanji 1086 says Gan Ning killed Ling Tong's father [[LingCaoLateHan|凌操]] while saving [[HuangZu|黄祖]]. Ling Tong repeatedly seeks revenge until [[SunQuan|孙权]] forbids it and moves Gan Ning away, making Ling Tong's private grievance a command-cohesion problem rather than a settled reconciliation.

Hanji 1109 gives Ling Tong a later, structurally important role. [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] estimates that he can hold the existing position for ten days, making Ling Tong's rear-guard capacity the condition that permits a temporary concentration of force to relieve the now-allied Gan Ning.

## Key Characteristics
- Carries a filial revenge claim against the officer who killed his father.
- Accepts ruler-imposed limits and physical separation instead of being allowed to prosecute the feud.
- Later holds a defensive assignment while senior commanders shift force to relieve Gan Ning.
- Appears as trusted capacity inside Lyu Meng's division-of-force plan despite the unresolved personal history.

- Ruler containment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1086-sunquan-beihao-zhuang-rentou-de-muhe-weihe-pianpian-fangguo-sufei-lt7xfjunv42n9532eqog-8k64odf|Hanji 1086]] says Sun Quan forbids retaliation and later stations Gan Ning elsewhere.
- Rear-guard assignment: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] says Lyu Meng proposes leaving Ling Tong behind for ten days.
- Operational dependency: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] makes that holding role the precondition for Zhou Yu and Lyu Meng's relief movement.
- Campaign result: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1109-xizaochi-nu-pi-caocao-duonian-jiye-hui-yu-yidan-lukll8jb60nx2brhwhsw7gfrd-kt|Hanji 1109]] reports that the relief force defeats Cao Ren's troops and returns successfully.

## Qualifications
The sources give no wider biography, no direct account of Ling Tong's response to Sun Quan's order, and no independent account of his conduct during the ten-day assignment. Hanji 1086's claim that he repeatedly tried to kill Gan Ning and the exact transfer arrangement remain source-scoped; later coordination does not by itself prove personal reconciliation.

## What Changed
- Added Ling Cao's death, Ling Tong's revenge motive, and Sun Quan's containment response.
- Qualified later operational cooperation so it is not mistaken for proven personal reconciliation.

## Relationships
- [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]] - planner who assigns Ling Tong the holding role.
- [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] - commander freed to join the relief operation.
- [[GanNing|甘宁]] - father's killer and later allied officer whose relief depends on Ling Tong holding the rear.
- [[LingCaoLateHan|凌操]] - father whose death creates the feud.
- [[SunQuan|孙权]] - ruler who contains the feud and retains both officers.
