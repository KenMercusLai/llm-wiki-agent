# 赵道襄王 / King Daoxiang of Zhao

赵道襄王 / King Daoxiang of Zhao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-104-yinmou-shisuan-rang-qinguo-bantu-you-you-you-pengzhang-le-lgnyhbduqutegtksrjwcvhmn8-t6]]. The source identifies him as Zhao Yan, successor to [[ZhaoDan|赵丹 / 赵孝成王]], and makes his first important act the removal of [[LianPo|廉颇]] from command.

His replacement choice, [[LeCheng|乐乘]], triggers Lian Po's angry attack and flight to [[WeiState|魏国]]. Later, after [[QinState|秦国]] repeatedly pressures [[ZhaoState|赵国]], Zhao Daoxiang Wang considers recalling Lian Po but relies on an envoy whose report is corrupted by [[GuoKai|郭开]]'s bribe. The episode therefore makes him a ruler whose distrust and bad information leave Zhao unable to recover a major veteran commander.
