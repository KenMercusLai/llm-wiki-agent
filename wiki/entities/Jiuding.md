# 九鼎 / Jiuding

九鼎 / Jiuding enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-76-shui-geile-chuqingxiangwang-de-zixin-lsut4cfic7xbbrmbmimrruf7n6y0]] as the ritual trophy that may tempt [[ChuState|楚国]] to attack the diminished [[ZhouRoyalHouse|周王室]]. In [[DongzhouWugong|东周武公]]'s argument, the ding are valuable less as usable assets than as dangerous symbols.

The source treats the Jiuding as a legitimacy object. If Chu only wants Zhou land or population, the gains are too small; if it wants the Jiuding, the act of moving them to Chu could advertise an assault on the Son of Heaven and invite wider opposition. The object therefore anchors [[ZhouRoyalSymbolicDeterrence|周王室象征威慑]]: a powerless-looking institution can still make its ritual property politically costly to seize.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-2-kongzi-zai-luguo-de-shenshi-zhimi-lphy12exg4u7f-30qkir96gv634r]] adds the terminal transfer as a source-scoped chronology note. The episode says Qin destroys [[EasternZhouPolity|东周国]] and moves the Jiuding to Qin in the same Qin Zhaoxiang Wang fifty-second-year frame used to explain [[LuState|鲁国]]'s fall. This conflicts with the [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-1-lvbuwei-shi-jiangziya-houren-lveg-wsmggroaxjsnxopaevexqod|Qinji 102-1]] framing of Eastern Zhou's destruction under [[QinZhuangxiangwang|秦庄襄王]], so the wiki treats it as a source tension rather than a resolved chronology.
