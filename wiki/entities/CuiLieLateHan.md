# 崔烈 / Cui Lie (late Han)

崔烈 / Cui Lie (late Han) enters through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-938-lishi-de-jingxing-ruanruo-zhihui-dailai-quru-lnlnrketgfbadhfjbzdjjtrmqfsh|Hanji 938]] as the situ who becomes taiwei after [[ZhangWenLateHan|张温]] is removed for ineffective pacification. The same episode later removes Cui Lie and appoints [[CaoSong|曹嵩]] as taiwei.

The source does not develop Cui Lie's biography. His function is to make late-Han high office look unstable during multi-front rebellion: the court can replace top ministers, but the replacements do not by themselves solve Liangzhou, Youzhou, Wuhuan, Changsha, or Xiongnu-linked disorder.
