# 周武王 / King Wu of Zhou

周武王 / King Wu of Zhou appears in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-71-xunzide-xingbenelun-lnyfxoxauexhwbrwkdevs8m4mr2j]] as one of the positive examples for [[Xunzi|荀子]]'s kingly-rule path. The host pairs him with [[ShangTang|商汤]] when explaining that rule grounded in ritual and righteousness can become secure and broadly ordering, not merely militarily successful.

This page is source-scoped. The episode does not retell Zhou conquest history; it uses Zhou Wu Wang as a classical exemplar inside [[XunzianWangbaThreePaths|荀子王霸三分法]], contrasted with failed rulers such as [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]] who possess state power without converting it into durable legitimacy or trust.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-1-lvbuwei-shi-jiangziya-houren-lveg-wsmggroaxjsnxopaevexqod]] adds the conquest-story branch that the earlier page only implied. After [[ZhouWenwang|周文王]] recognizes [[JiangTaigong|姜太公 / 姜子牙]], Zhou Wuwang honors Jiang Taigong as 师尚父, uses him in the campaign against Shang, and grants him [[QiState|齐国]] after Zhou is founded. The source therefore connects Wu Wang's kingly-rule exemplar status to a concrete adviser-and-enfeoffment story.
