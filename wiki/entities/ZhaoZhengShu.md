# 《赵正书》 / Zhao Zheng Shu

《赵正书》 / Zhao Zheng Shu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-126-qinshihuang-siyin-zhimi-2-ln15p5qb7lc20goni1ibcpmn3wca|Qinji 126 part 2]] as a Western Han bamboo manuscript in the Peking University collection. The episode says Peking University accepted the donated overseas-returned bamboo slips in 2009 and identifies Zhao Zheng Shu as one of the texts relevant to Qin Shi Huang's final tour.

The source uses Zhao Zheng Shu as an excavated-text supplement to [[Shiji|《史记》]] and [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]]. Its opening reportedly describes [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] falling seriously ill during his last tour and asking whether Heaven's mandate could be changed. It also says he already knew a "mountain ghost" prophecy that he would die that year, but not the month or day.

For the wiki, the text matters less as a standalone chronicle than as a source-critical support for [[OmenCountermeasureMobility|游徙避命]]. In the episode's reconstruction, Zhao Zheng Shu helps explain why Qin Shi Huang's last tour might be more than ordinary inspection: it can be read as an attempt to change qi, fate, or timing after "祖龙死" has made the death warning personal.
