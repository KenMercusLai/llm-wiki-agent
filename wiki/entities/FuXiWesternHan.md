## Overview

傅喜 / Fu Xi is synthesized as a Western Han Fu-family figure whose profile now has pre-collapse, attack, and post-collapse layers. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-582-nvren-de-chenhenxin-zui-duo-nanren-ming-ljiuneown6ah1yx5g_uchpb5escp|Hanji 582]] adds the immediate vulnerability layer: [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]] remains angry after Fu Xi has already been dismissed and returned to his fief, and [[ZhuBoWesternHan|朱博]] tries to strip his marquisate through a bundled impeachment. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-588-laoban-bu-chenzhi-xiashu-neng-you-duo-can-lovz7epf8qs0atyr3njy2kvblqpv|Hanji 588]] names him in [[BaoXuanWesternHan|鲍宣]]'s personnel remedy as the figure who should lead or discipline the outer-relative field under [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-604-xihan-wangmang-duoquan-hou-ruhe-qingxi-quanli-geju-ltwscyyiobc1ocvgti3fd-deajru|Hanji 604]] later presents him as the Fu-clan survivor after [[WangMang|王莽]]'s first cleanup of the Fu and Ding networks.

## Current Profile

Hanji 582 shows Fu Xi as already punished but not yet safe. His dismissal and return to his fief do not satisfy Fu Taihou. Zhu Bo and Zhao Xuan memorialize against Fu Xi and He Wu together, asking that both be reduced to commoner status. The tactic fails because Aidi suspects Fu Taihou's resentment behind the memorial, but the episode still shows how Fu Xi's nonalignment leaves him exposed to retaliation before it becomes a survival credential.

Hanji 588 gives Fu Xi's earlier positive reputation from an outsider-remonstrance angle. Bao Xuan argues that Aidi should summon Da Sima Fu Xi to supervise the outer relatives, implying that Fu Xi is a credible corrective choice in a court where untrained kin, glib speakers, and favorite networks are harming commoners.

Hanji 604 presents Fu Xi as the Fu-clan exception inside a general purge. [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] praises his gravity, caution, loyal thought, and refusal to change his conduct even when Fu Taihou's side punished him by driving him back to his fief. That earlier nonalignment becomes a survival asset once Wang Mang returns to power and the Fu and Ding families lose office, titles, and residence security.

The episode's second emphasis is Fu Xi's response to restored rank. He is recalled to Chang'an and made tejin, a position treated as equivalent to the Three Excellencies, but he responds with fear rather than delight. The host reads that fear as political intelligence: in a court where yesterday's favored houses can collapse overnight, high status is exposure as much as reward.

Fu Xi's later return to his fief and old-age death make him the episode's "善终" example. The profile therefore links him to [[HighStatusCautionSelfProtection|高位谨慎自保]] and [[GoodDeathPoliticalSurvival|乱局善终]] rather than to factional victory.

## Key Characteristics

- Fu-family figure distinguished from the Fu/Ding faction after Aidi's death.
- Target of Fu Taihou's continued anger before the later Wang Mang cleanup.
- Named by Bao Xuan before the succession crisis as a corrective figure for outer-relative management.
- Earlier nonalignment with Fu Taihou's political line makes him vulnerable first and survivable later.
- Recalled and honored by Wang Zhengjun after Wang Mang's return to power.
- Treats sudden rank recovery as dangerous rather than simply fortunate.
- Later returns to his fief and dies at old age, making him the episode's good-end countercase.

## Qualifications

This page is source-scoped to Hanji 582, Hanji 588, and Hanji 604. Hanji 582 supports Fu Xi's vulnerability to Fu Taihou's continued anger and Zhu Bo's bundled impeachment, but not Fu Xi's own words in the case. Hanji 588 supports Bao Xuan's recommendation of Fu Xi but not the exact office mechanics or Fu Xi's own response. Hanji 604 supports Fu Xi's nonalignment, recall, fear, and good-end role, but not a full biography, exact dates, complete offices, or his private motives beyond the episode's interpretation of his conduct.

## What Changed

- Added Hanji 582's earlier failed attack on Fu Xi, making his nonalignment both a source of danger before Aidi's death and a survival credential after it.
- Preserved Hanji 588's Bao Xuan recommendation layer and Hanji 604's account of Fu Xi as the Fu-family exception and good-end survival case.
