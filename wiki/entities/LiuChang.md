## Evidence
- **Ecology reporting:** In [[Xishuangbanna|西双版纳]], 刘畅 reports on Chinese honeybees, giant honeybees, stingless bees, traditional beekeeping and wild-honey collection. He distinguishes honey production from [[NativeBeeEcologicalPollination|native-bee pollination]] of rainforest plants and cash crops, and discusses overharvesting and chemical-intensive farming rather than claiming all managed bees are disappearing. [[ep261-mifeng-weiji-ruguo-shiqu-mifeng-shijie-jianghui-zenyang-lqp-cwcdxx1ziixb35ijmmvd6cv]]
- **Consumer-technology reporting:** His account of AI glasses treats translation, meeting recording and summaries, prompts, photos, music and visual recognition as assistant uses, not proof of a general computing platform. Weight, prescription-lens compatibility, displays, battery life and weak everyday scenarios limit adoption; the adjacent product questions include [[AIGlassesProductFit]], [[WearableAIAssistant]], [[QwenSmartGlasses]], [[AIHardwarePrivacyExchange]] and [[SmartGlassesBystanderPrivacy]]. [[ep253-baohuo-de-ai-haowu-daodi-shi-zhenxiang-haishi-zhishangshui-lgt0cdkotgnzjl0mu2tx41p9fw-4]]
- **Cultural interpretation:** As a [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]] speaker, he connects [[Homer]], [[TheIliad|《伊利亚特》]] and [[TheOdyssey|《奥德赛》]] to performed [[OralFormulaicEpic|oral formulas]], fixed epithets and [[OralEpicRepetition|repetition]]: [[Achilles|阿基里斯]] and [[Agamemnon|阿加门农]] dispute [[AncientGreekHonorCommunity|communal honor]], whereas [[Odysseus|奥德修斯]]' return tests identity and home. This is his reading of the texts, not a claim that later [[HomericAdaptationModernization|adaptations]] share a single motive. [[ep269-cong-manwei-yuzhou-dao-aodesai-weihe-zongyao-fanpai-yingxiong-shishi-ltklotheqpup6oamyflfzpxtipsw]]
- **Vocational-education reporting:** He describes [[LiAuto|理想汽车]]'s [[IndustrialCollege|产业学院]] at [[ChangzhouIndustrialVocationalTechnicalCollege|常州工业职业技术学院]] in [[Changzhou|常州]]'s new-energy-vehicle setting. [[ProjectBasedVocationalLearning|Project-based practice]] can build confidence where abstract classroom learning did not, but his case does not guarantee an employment “iron rice bowl” for all [[VocationalEducation|vocational students]]. - [[AIGlassesProductFit]] - 刘畅评估翻译、拍摄等用途能否克服重量、配镜及场景不足，而非断言眼镜已替代手机。
- [[AIHardwarePrivacyExchange]] - 其AI眼镜报道触及影像与语音功能的资料交换问题，不能把功能介绍当作认可所有采集方式。
- [[ForagingEthics]] - 其西双版纳野蜜报道警示掏巢与过度采蜜伤及本地传粉昆虫。
- [[GaoYiding]] - 两人在Talk三联蜜蜂及史诗节目中分别承担主持与讲述角色。
- [[OralEpicRepetition]] - 他用固定称谓与重复程式解释《伊利亚特》《奥德赛》的口传表演，而非将重复视为文本缺陷。
- [[PesticidePollinatorRisk]] - 他在蜜蜂专题中讨论农药密集使用对本地传粉昆虫的压力，与采蜜风险有别。
- [[QwenSmartGlasses]] - 他以千问眼镜的图像、位置识别为AI眼镜使用例，而非验证其长期产品适配。
- [[SmartGlassesBystanderPrivacy]] - 其眼镜摄影与录音场景涉及旁观者同意问题；节目未证明具体产品已解决该问题。
- [[VocationalEducation]] - 他以理想汽车与常州高职产业学院讨论项目制实践，未承诺所有职校生都能稳定就业。
- [[WearableAIAssistant]] - 他描述翻译、会议摘要和提示等眼镜助手功能，同时指出电池及佩戴限制。
- [[WildPollinatorCrisis]] - 其蜜蜂报道区分养殖蜜蜂产量与野生蜂授粉生态风险，反对将二者混为一谈。
