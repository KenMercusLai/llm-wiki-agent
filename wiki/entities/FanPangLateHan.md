# 范滂 / Fan Pang (late Han)

## Overview

Fan Pang is a late-Han party-prohibition figure whose current profile now runs from post-167 release, registry exclusion, and tacit gratitude to Huo Xu through later honor-list visibility, voluntary imprisonment, non-implication of helpers, and family farewell.

## Current Profile

Hanji 877 supplies the earlier aftermath of the first Party Prohibition case. After release, Fan Pang is still inside [[RegistryBasedPoliticalExclusion|登记造册式政治禁锢]]: the party figures are sent home, registered, and blocked from future office. In that setting he visits [[HuoXuLateHan|霍谞]], the man who saved his life, but does not stage verbal gratitude. The episode reads this through [[TacitGratitudeRestraint|心照不宣式感恩克制]] rather than ingratitude: the relationship is acknowledged without turning rescue into public performance. His later discomfort with a huge Runan reception adds a second caution layer around fame and crowd visibility.

Hanji 892 supplies Fan Pang's later pre-crackdown reputation setting by listing him among the士人 honor categories that make public praise visible. Hanji 893 then places Fan Pang among the party figures named in [[CaoJieLateHan|曹节]]'s accusation chain. When [[WuDaoLateHan|吴导]] receives the order to arrest him and grieves at the post station, Fan Pang recognizes the situation and goes to prison himself. [[GuoYiLateHan|郭揖]] tries to free him and even prepares to abandon office, but Fan Pang refuses to drag Guo Yi into the disaster.

The episode uses Fan Pang's death-facing choice as a high-intensity名节 case. His mother accepts that a person who shares fame with [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]] and Du Mi cannot also expect long life, while Fan Pang's words to his young son expose the court's moral collapse: neither doing evil nor doing good provides a coherent survival rule when the state has reversed praise and punishment.

## Key Characteristics

- Late-Han士人 whose honor-list visibility precedes his later party-prohibition arrest.
- Released party figure whose amnesty does not restore office eligibility or ordinary safety.
- Tacitly grateful actor who visits Huo Xu without turning rescue into public repayment.
- Party figure whose name appears in the arrest field created by Cao Jie's framing.
- Voluntary prisoner who recognizes the arrest order and goes to prison instead of hiding.
- Non-implicating actor who rejects Guo Yi's escape plan to avoid transferring danger to a helper.
- Death-facing son whose farewell with his mother turns reputation into accepted sacrifice.

## Qualifications

This page is grounded in Hanji 877, Hanji 892, and Hanji 893 and still does not reconstruct Fan Pang's full career outside those source windows. Du Mi is preserved through the existing disambiguated page where later sources support it. Transcript variants such as 范胖 are normalized to 范滂, and Hanji 877's Spring-Autumn allusion names remain source-scoped.

## What Changed

- Hanji 877 adds the earlier post-release layer: Fan Pang is free from immediate imprisonment but still politically registered and excluded.
- The profile now includes Fan Pang's tacit gratitude toward Huo Xu and his avoidance of conspicuous Runan reception.
- Hanji 892 adds the pre-crackdown honor-list visibility layer before Hanji 893's arrest and farewell account.
- The current profile treats Fan Pang's名节 as both relational restraint and later public reputation exposure.
