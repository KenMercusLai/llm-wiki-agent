# 董卓 / Dong Zhuo

董卓 / Dong Zhuo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] as the comparison case for [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]]'s late fortress strategy. The episode compares Gongsun Zan's [[YijingFortress|易京]] to Dong Zhuo's [[MeiwuFortress|郿坞]], arguing that both men tried to convert hoarded food and fortified enclosure into security.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-967-ta-canyu-mousha-dongzhuo-jingneng-taoguo-sishen-weilie-lv24biyswj9ljdld5htynhauhph3|Hanji 967]] adds the immediate post-assassination aftermath. [[WangYunLateHan|王允]], [[LyuBu|吕布]], and officials including [[ShiSunRuiLateHan|士孙瑞]] are tied to the anti-Dong-Zhuo action, but Dong Zhuo's death does not restore stable court order. His remnant commanders, especially [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]] and [[GuoSiLateHan|郭汜]], remain armed enough to return against [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] after [[JiaXu|贾诩]]'s survival advice.

This page remains source-scoped because the wiki has not yet ingested a full Dong Zhuo career sequence. The new branch makes his death a transition from personal dictatorship to remnant-military revenge rather than a clean liberation of the Han court.
