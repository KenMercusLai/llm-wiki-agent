# 阿房宫 / Afang Palace

阿房宫 / Afang Palace enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-125-1-qinshihuang-xiujian-shishang-zuida-lanweilou-afanggong-lhqc6jw4jzoyrg7jzc2mbxdebj4|Qinji 125-1]] as the late [[QinShiHuang|秦始皇]] capital-expansion project south of the Wei River. The episode frames it as a response to the crowded, post-relocation [[Xianyang|咸阳]] capital, but also as symbolic architecture meant to make imperial space resemble celestial order.

The source emphasizes scale and design: a large front hall, a raised route extending toward the Qinling, a gate on the mountain, and a gallery crossing the Wei River back toward Xianyang. In that reading, Afang Palace continues [[QinCosmicCapitalPlanning|秦代宇宙化都城规划]] from [[QinJimiao|极庙]], [[Lishan|骊山]], and road networks into an even larger palace complex.

Afang Palace is also the episode's most concrete example of [[ImperialConstructionOverload|帝国工程过载]]. It competes with [[QinShiHuangMausoleum|秦始皇陵]] for labor, draws on punished workers and transported materials, and is left unfinished after Qin Shi Huang dies. [[QinErshi|胡亥 / 秦二世]] later restarts it, but rebellion news quickly interrupts the revived work.

## Key Claims
- The episode treats Afang Palace as both capital expansion and imperial symbol, not only private luxury.
- "Afang" is presented as a temporary name because Qin Shi Huang dies before formal naming.
- Its incompletion results from succession timing, labor reallocation to the mausoleum, and later rebellion pressure.
- The source uses the palace's literary afterlife through "阿房宫赋" as a warning about power corroding the people beneath it.
