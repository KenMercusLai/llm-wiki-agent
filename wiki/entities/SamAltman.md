# Sam Altman

## Overview
Sam Altman is profiled through YC founder and leadership accounts, hard-tech recruiting, investor intervention, and his OpenAI role. Other appearances are comparisons, predictions, fiction, or interviewee interpretations rather than independent biographical proof.

## Current Profile
The central interview traces Looped, YC and OpenAI, while Graham’s account qualifies the YC transition: he says Altman chose OpenAI when asked to focus on one organization. Replit, Boom, Helion, and Jared Friedman supply specific examples of YC recruiting or partner selection.

## Key Characteristics
- **YC founder and leadership path:** The first-batch Looped experience, fundraising and subsequent YC presidency precede his OpenAI leadership; Graham’s account says Altman chose OpenAI rather than being fired by YC.
- **Recruiting and network leverage:** Replit, Boom, Helion and Jared Friedman accounts show recruiting and program-access decisions; Reddit and Zenefits accounts describe investor and reputation support.
- **OpenAI-related narratives:** Scaling and governance recollections coexist with an unreleased-model anecdote, a speculative one-person-company forecast, and a fictional cameo; these have different evidentiary status.

## Evidence
- **YC founder and leadership path:** In [[PaulGraham]] and [[JessicaLivingston]]’s recollection, Altman’s first-batch company raising a [[SequoiaCapital]] Series A helped establish [[YCombinator]]’s credibility. Altman recalls starting [[Looped]] in a Stanford dorm, flying overnight to interview, canceling a [[GoldmanSachs]] internship after acceptance, then learning carrier deals, fundraising and the constraints of depending on powerful partners; he calls the apparently bold choice favorable [[FounderRiskCalibration|risk-adjusted judgment]]. Graham later saw him as a natural YC successor but says he should have required undivided attention: YC asked Altman to choose YC or [[OpenAI]], and he chose OpenAI, not a firing in Graham’s account. ## Qualifications

Parker Conrad’s account is his own account of advocacy.
