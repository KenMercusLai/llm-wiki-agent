# 韩王安 / Han An

韩王安 / Han An first appears in the wiki as the successor to [[HanHuanhuiwang|韩桓惠王]] in [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-107-zuihou-yici-hezong-faqin-fa-le-ge-jimo-lrzq1qks7z-oromd-5f-dgqjwjhw]], where the notice is part of the late-Warring-States sequence after the failed final [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] campaign.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-112-2-qinshihuang-yeshi-tade-shumi-llkoy-zvhjjr6af9tflcw1xvogwz]] makes him consequential through [[HanFei|韩非]]. The episode presents Han as the weakest surviving major state and says Han Fei repeatedly advises Han An to adopt Legalist reform and strengthen the state, but Han An does not accept the advice. When [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] prepares to attack Han first, Han An sends Han Fei as envoy to [[QinState|秦国]].

The page is source-scoped. Han An's wiki role here is not a full reign biography; it is the ruler position that makes Han Fei's failure inside Han visible. Han Fei's writing can reach Ying Zheng, but it cannot first make his own ruler act.
