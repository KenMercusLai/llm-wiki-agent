# 甘宁 / Gan Ning

## Overview
甘宁 / Gan Ning moves from irregular force and an Yizhou talent-integration failure into an Eastern Wu commander whose gratitude, local knowledge, small-force initiative, and visible composure all become politically usable under [[SunQuan|孙权]].

## Current Profile
Hanji 981 presents Gan Ning as a Ba-region elite who rebels with Shen Mi and Lou Fa under [[LiuZhang|刘璋]], loses to Dongzhou troops, and leaves east. The episode uses his later Wu career to argue that Liu Zhang could suppress but not integrate important local capacity. Hanji 993 invokes him as another capable figure underused in the [[LiuBiao|刘表]] field.

Hanji 1086 adds the social and moral liabilities carried into his Wu service. It describes the “锦帆贼” background as privately organized violence later converted into official command through [[ViolenceToInstitutionalLegitimacy|暴力资本的体制化转换]]. Gan Ning also asks Sun Quan to spare [[SuFeiLateHan|苏飞]], whose earlier aid enabled his survival, and offers his own life if Su Fei escapes. At the same time, his earlier killing of [[LingCaoLateHan|凌操]] leaves [[LingTong|凌统]] seeking revenge, requiring Sun Quan to prohibit retaliation and separate the two officers.

Hanji 1108 supplies his first direct offensive command. During the standoff around [[JianglingLateHan|江陵]], Gan Ning proposes taking [[YilingLateHan|夷陵]] with several hundred selected troops. The source attributes the seizure to familiarity with waterways, roads, and defenses, plus troops suited to mountain and river fighting. Hanji 1109 continues the operation after he is besieged: Gan Ning asks [[ZhouYuLateHan|周瑜]] for aid and is portrayed drinking calmly under missile fire until a [[LyuMeng|吕蒙]]-backed relief force arrives.

## Key Characteristics
- Begins as a local-capacity loss from Liu Zhang's fragmented Yizhou order.
- Carries irregular fighting, organization, reciprocal loyalty, and an old blood-feud liability into Wu service.
- Uses his standing to secure clemency for Su Fei through a life-backed personal guarantee.
- Identifies Yiling as a flank opportunity during the Jiangling stalemate.
- Matches selected troops and local knowledge to a fast seizure.
- Uses visible calm to keep siege pressure from becoming morale collapse.

## Qualifications
Gan Ning's Yizhou rebellion and “锦帆贼” background cannot be reduced to victimhood or sanitized as talent alone, and the “lost talent” reading is retrospective. Hanji 1086's dialogue, kowtow scene, prepared boxes, private motives, and “violence as capital” frame remain source-scoped. The 1108-1109 sequence also comes from short popular-history retellings; exact numbers, “top special forces,” 解烦兵 genealogy, drinking details, and the horse capture remain source-scoped.

## What Changed
- Added Su Fei's earlier aid and Gan Ning's life-backed plea as a durable gratitude claim.
- Added the episode's institutional-conversion interpretation without treating it as Gan Ning's proven motive.
- Added Ling Cao's death and Ling Tong's revenge as liabilities that survive patron change.
