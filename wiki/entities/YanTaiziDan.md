# 燕太子丹 / Crown Prince Dan of Yan

燕太子丹 / Crown Prince Dan of Yan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-1-huangdi-zhong-de-chaochang-daiji-wang-guoshi-le-lt75scavlm8qjn6ulpd-b2kwzwjf]] as a young [[YanState|燕国]] hostage in [[Handan|邯郸]] in 252 BCE. The episode's notice is compact: he is placed in the same city and age-world as young [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]], who is then about eight.

The page keeps him source-scoped. His role in this episode is not yet the later Yan-Qin assassination branch, but the early human connection that lets the narrative foreshadow later hostility between Yan and Qin.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-113-limu-ruhe-yong-yiji-zhili-zu-qinguo-tongyi-luymqpcolsvyz5miodit3wdlx0ob]] returns to that foreshadowing. The source says Yan and Qin are still aligned enough to squeeze [[ZhaoState|赵国]] from east and west, but Dan's experience in Qin is humiliating rather than protective despite his childhood acquaintance with Ying Zheng. His escape back to Yan becomes an emotional and political setup for later anti-Qin escalation.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-2-yanguo-cisha-qinshihuang-weihe-hui-xuan-jingke-lsewjrmgup1yanf8ipn-i0ykqwjx]] turns that setup into the assassination-planning branch. Facing Qin pressure after Zhao's fall, Dan rejects [[JuWu|居武]]'s slower plan to rebuild [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] and contact [[Xiongnu|匈奴]], refuses to send away the Qin fugitive [[FanWuji|樊无忌]], and then follows Ju Wu's referral to [[TianGuang|田光]]. The episode presents his turn to [[JingKe|荆轲]] as a mix of personal revenge, weak-state desperation, guest-protection honor, and patronage binding.
