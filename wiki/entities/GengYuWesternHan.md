## Overview

耿育 / Geng Yu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-574-gudai-de-manfen-zuowen-shi-shenme-yangzi-lotqprwr3rk5lke0rjn94riljuvh|Hanji 574]] as the Western Han memorialist whose defense of [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s succession choice gives [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] a politically usable reason to stop further investigation of [[ZhaoFeiyanWesternHan|赵飞燕]]-linked palace accusations.

## Current Profile

The source presents Geng Yu less as a biographical subject than as a political writer. His memorial first acknowledges ordinary succession norms, then cites the Zhou precedent of Taibo yielding so that Jili and Ji Chang could continue the line. From there, he argues that Chengdi's choice of Aidi was not scandal or weakness but foresight: a young biological son might have left power with a mother, unstable ministers, or disorderly regency politics.

Geng Yu's practical achievement is narrative conversion. He turns continued investigation into a reputational danger for Chengdi and an unfilial burden for Aidi, while ending the case becomes clarification of rumor, completion of the deceased ruler's intention, and protection of dynastic reputation.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han memorialist known in this wiki from one Jianping 1 / 6 BCE source.
- Uses classical precedent to make an unusual succession decision politically and ritually defensible.
- Reframes palace scandal into Chengdi's long-range statecraft.
- Gives Aidi a public rationale for stopping a case he is already inclined to close.
- Shows how court writing can alter decision conditions by binding filial duty, reputation, and political stability.

## Qualifications

This page is source-bounded. Hanji 574 gives Geng Yu's memorial logic and effect, but not a full career, exact office, broader writings, or complete text-critical history of the memorial. The page therefore treats him as a court-rhetorical actor rather than a fully reconstructed official.
