# 乌桓 / Wuhuan

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-942-yue-youxiu-de-ren-huode-yue-lei-heli-ma-lkwlrcgnt-kxxc2qqz6z6wkg7e5z|Hanji 942]] backfills the Wuhuan before the later anti-[[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] coalition. [[QiuLijuLateHan|丘力居]] and Wuhuan forces help turn the [[ZhangChunLateHan|张纯]] and [[ZhangJuLateHan|张举]] disorder into a frontier crisis, but [[LiuYuLateHan|刘虞]]'s arrival induces submission through envoys and rewards.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] later shows Wuhuan as a northern frontier group participating in the coalition against Gongsun Zan. The episode says [[YanRou|阎柔]] is selected as Wuhuan sima because of his prestige and then gathers large mixed Hu and Han forces.

Wuhuan cavalry also joins [[XianyuFu|鲜于辅]] and [[Xianbei|鲜卑]] riders in welcoming [[LiuHeLateHan|刘和]] and linking with [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s side. In this source, Wuhuan are not background color; they are part of the force mix that makes the anti-Gongsun campaign large enough to break Youzhou's local alignment.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-972-sanguo-liuyu-zhisi-taisha-taitianzhen-lvawrcandbh628rckukosgarh7v2|Hanji 972]] adds a quieter frontier-order branch through [[TianChou|田筹]]. After Tian Chou gathers followers in [[XuWuMountain|徐无山]], Wuhuan and other frontier groups respect the settlement, send gifts, and receive reassurance that they should avoid raiding and disorder.
