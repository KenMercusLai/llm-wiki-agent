# 田丰 / Tian Feng (late Han)

## Overview
田丰 / Tian Feng (late Han) is a [[YuanShao|袁绍]] adviser whose wiki profile centers on high-quality warnings that Yuan Shao does not reliably convert into action.

## Current Profile
Tian Feng is a diagnostic adviser rather than a full biographical subject in the current bounded evidence. Hanji 975 shows him identifying [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]]'s grain request as practical support for [[LyuBu|吕布]] during the [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] rebellion. Hanji 976 places him among Yuan Shao's underused Hebei advisers with [[JuShou|沮授]] and [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]]. Hanji 1023 and Hanji 1041 now make the missed [[Xudu|许都]] attack opportunity a repeated pattern: Tian Feng sees that control of [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] and Cao Cao's absence can change the field, but [[YuanShao|袁绍]] does not convert the warning into action.

## Key Characteristics
- Reads political meaning behind resource, alignment, and court-control moves rather than treating them as isolated requests.
- Supplies urgent strategic warnings that require fast ruler-side action to matter.
- Functions as a mirror to [[XunYu|荀彧]] because both detect hidden danger, but only Cao Cao's side reliably converts such detection.
- Belongs to Yuan Shao's Hebei adviser group, making Yuan's failure a decision-conversion problem rather than a talent-supply problem.
- Highlights the lost court-legitimacy and attack-window opportunity because advice to seize Xudu while Cao Cao is exposed is ignored more than once.

## Evidence
- Grain-warning diagnosis: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-975-yanzhou-zhiluan-xunyu-ruhe-bang-caocao-shou-dabenying-ln0hh4jrokkwtqsmxmqhdjzcosdr|Hanji 975]] says Tian Feng identifies Zhang Miao's grain request to Yuan Shao as practical support for Lü Bu during Cao Cao's Yanzhou crisis.
- Underused adviser group: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] names Tian Feng with Ju Shou and Xu You to argue that Yuan Shao's problem is not lack of advisers but weak use of them.
- Xudu and emperor opportunity: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1023-jiemi-caocao-yu-zhangxiu-de-bingfa-boyi-lphbnmfdu0x5w7zjra7k0o8cyel4|Hanji 1023]] reports Tian Feng urging Yuan Shao to attack Xudu and receive Emperor Xian, and records Yuan Shao's failure to adopt it before Cao Cao withdraws after hearing the warning from a defector.
- Cao Cao absence window: [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1041-jiemi-lishishang-zhenshi-de-guanyu-jiangcao-luisp0ndk1lfrc87byygn6r8myhx|Hanji 1041]] shows Tian Feng urging Yuan Shao to attack Cao Cao's rear while Cao is fighting Liu Bei, but Yuan Shao does not move because his young son is ill.

## Qualifications
The sources do not provide a full biography of Tian Feng. They use him as an adviser-case inside Yuan Shao's decision system, so the page should not infer the full range of his later career from these episodes alone. Hanji 1023 and Hanji 1041 show that Tian Feng's advice could have pressured Cao Cao, but they do not prove that Yuan Shao would certainly have captured Xudu or the emperor if he had acted.

## What Changed
- Added Hanji 1041 as a second missed Xudu/rear-attack window while Cao Cao is away against Liu Bei.
- Reframed the Xudu point from a single missed opportunity into a repeated Yuan-side decision-conversion failure.
