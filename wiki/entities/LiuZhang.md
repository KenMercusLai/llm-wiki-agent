# 刘璋 / Liu Zhang

刘璋 / Liu Zhang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]] as [[LiuYanLateHan|刘焉]]'s surviving son and successor in [[YizhouLateHan|益州]]. After Liu Yan's other sons are dead or gone, senior provincial figures including [[ZhaoWeiLateHan|赵韪]] judge Liu Zhang mild and kind enough to accept, and the Han court eventually recognizes him because it cannot practically impose a different inspector on the province.

The source presents Liu Zhang's weakness less as a single personality defect than as an integration problem. Liu Yan's Dongzhou military bloc draws on Nanyang and Sanfu migrant soldiers, while Yi Province's older local elite families compete for resources and standing. Liu Zhang inherits that split without the capacity to make the groups cohere.

[[GanNing|甘宁]] becomes the episode's visible talent-loss case. As a Ba-region elite, he rebels with Shen Mi and Lou Fa, is defeated by the Dongzhou troops, and then leaves eastward. The host treats his later success under Wu as evidence that Liu Zhang's order could suppress a challenge but could not integrate capable local people into durable service.
