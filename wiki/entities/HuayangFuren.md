# 华阳夫人 / Lady Huayang

华阳夫人 / Lady Huayang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-3-lvbuwei-lishishang-fengtou-di-yi-ren-lswa9b864oc-s8vaxtdu15arydu2]] as the favored wife of [[QinXiaowenwang|安国君柱 / 秦孝文王]]. The episode's key detail is her structural position: Anguo Jun loves her and makes her夫人, but she has no son.

That absence creates the succession opening [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] sees. Anguo Jun has more than twenty sons, and [[QinZhuangxiangwang|异人 / 子楚]] has no obvious advantage among them. But if Huayang Furen adopts Yiren, then the principle summarized in the episode as "子以母贵" can convert a neglected hostage into a high-value heir candidate.

Huayang Furen is therefore not yet narrated as an active persuader in this episode. Her importance is positional: she is the high-status maternal channel through which [[MaternalStatusSuccessionLeverage|子以母贵式继承杠杆]] can operate. The source stops before the full adoption and lobbying sequence, so this page records only the opening created by her favored-but-childless status.
