# 高渐离 / Gao Jianli

高渐离 / Gao Jianli enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-2-yanguo-cisha-qinshihuang-weihe-hui-xuan-jingke-lsewjrmgup1yanf8ipn-i0ykqwjx]] as one of [[JingKe|荆轲]]'s close companions in [[YanState|燕国]]. The episode identifies him by his skill at striking the zhu, a musical instrument, and places him with Jing Ke and a dog butcher in the Yan marketplace.

The scene matters less as a biography of Gao Jianli than as part of Jing Ke's social profile. The three drink, Gao Jianli plays, Jing Ke sings, and they laugh and cry publicly "as if nobody else were there." The source uses that market performance to locate Jing Ke in a侠客 circle outside normal court office, while still leaving room for [[TianGuang|田光]] to recognize a deeper quality in him.
