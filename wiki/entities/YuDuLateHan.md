# 余毒 / Yu Du (Late Han)

余毒 / Yu Du (Late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-970-yuanshao-ansha-lvbu-weihe-hui-shibai-ln2pnvit-qgpnhn-pne7o-p1irrs|Hanji 970]] as the [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] figure who connects with the [[WeiCommanderyLateHan|魏郡]] soldier revolt and attacks [[YeLateHan|邺城]], then becomes [[YuanShao|袁绍]]'s first major retaliation target.

The source says Yuan Shao attacks Yu Du around Zhaoge's Luchang Mountain, fights for five days, breaks his position, and kills Yu Du along with many followers. Yu Du's page therefore marks the transition from Yuan Shao's exposed rear crisis to a punitive mountain campaign.
