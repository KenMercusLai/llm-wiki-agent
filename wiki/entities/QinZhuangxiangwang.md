# 异人 / 子楚 / 秦庄襄王 / King Zhuangxiang of Qin

## Overview
Yiren, later Zichu and Qin Zhuangxiang Wang, rose from a poorly supported Zhao hostage to Qin ruler through Lu Buwei’s backing and Huayang Furen’s adoption.

## Current Profile
The brief reign involved Lu Buwei’s chancellorship and Qin expansion before Ying Zheng succeeded as a child; paternity rumor and a conflict over Eastern Zhou chronology remain unresolved.

## Key Characteristics
- The same person appears as 异人, 子楚 and 秦庄襄王.
- Adoption and hostage diplomacy reshaped succession.
- A brief reign had ministerial and territorial consequences.
- Paternity rumor and Eastern Zhou dating are unresolved source questions.

## Evidence
- **Hostage with dynastic leverage:** In [[Handan|邯郸]], [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] finds 异人, an unfavored, poorly supplied Qin grandson and son of [[QinXiaowenwang|安国君柱 / 秦孝文王]], among many brothers, hostage in hostile [[ZhaoState|赵国]]. Their reciprocal agreement is an investment in succession, not an altruistic rescue: [[HuayangFuren|华阳夫人]]'s childless position creates [[MaternalStatusSuccessionLeverage|maternal-status leverage]] for [[PoliticalVentureInvestment|political venture investment]]. Lu Buwei later gives him [[ZhaoJi|赵姬]], who bears [[QinShiHuang|嬴政]] in the source's account; the biological-father rumor is not settled here. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-3-lvbuwei-lishishang-fengtou-di-yi-ren-lswa9b864oc-s8vaxtdu15arydu2]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-4-shishang-wei-shiye-song-laopo-diyi-ren-lo8omg01gkwlojkpiflaep99x-vz]]
- **Adoption and return:** Through [[YangquanJun|阳泉君]], Lu Buwei reaches Huayang Furen, whose husband makes 异人 his嫡子 with a jade [[WrittenSuccessionCredential|succession tally]]. During the Qin-Zhao crisis he bribes guards and escapes Handan with Lu Buwei, wears Chu dress before Huayang Furen and receives the name 子楚. After [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] dies, the new king makes Zichu heir; Zhao returns Zhao Ji and young Ying Zheng as political damage control. Qin Xiaowen Wang's formal reign lasts only three days, after which Zichu becomes Qin Zhuangxiang Wang and honors Huayang Furen and biological mother [[XiaTaihou|夏姬 / 夏太后]] with separate dowager titles. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-4-shishang-wei-shiye-song-laopo-diyi-ren-lo8omg01gkwlojkpiflaep99x-vz]] [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-1-huangdi-zhong-de-chaochang-daiji-wang-guoshi-le-lt75scavlm8qjn6ulpd-b2kwzwjf]] [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-101-1-zhe-wei-daomei-junwang-dengji-santian-ji-cusi-lll-bcc7basaf6-nzwqtmcb5qw6w]]
- **Short reign and campaigns:** In the source's 249 BCE first-year account, he appoints Lu Buwei chancellor, destroys [[EasternZhouPolity|东周国]] and rewards him as Wenxin Hou with a Luoyang-area fief. [[MengAo|蒙敖]] takes Chenggao and Qin organizes [[SanchuanWarringStates|三川]]; [[ChuState|楚国]]'s move against [[LuState|鲁国]] belongs to the wider annals. In a 248 BCE second-year account [[LushiChunqiu|《吕氏春秋》]] begins compilation and Meng Ao attacks Zhao, taking 37 cities, though another episode attaches Eastern Zhou's fall and [[Jiuding|九鼎]] transfer to Qin Zhaoxiang Wang's fifty-second year. The 247 BCE third-year account has continued action in [[Shangdang|上党]], creation of [[TaiyuanWarringStates|太原]] and attack on [[WeiState|魏国]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-1-lvbuwei-shi-jiangziya-houren-lveg-wsmggroaxjsnxopaevexqod]] [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-102-2-kongzi-zai-luguo-de-shenshi-zhimi-lphy12exg4u7f-30qkir96gv634r]] [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-103-1-simaqian-kanzhong-de-xinlingjun-suan-shi-xiaoren-ma-lvty1pdnibvqpk0m16kkxzw4t-ck]]
- **Death and retrospective title:** He dies in 247 BCE on the fifth-month bingwu day (glossed as the 26th); thirteen-year-old Ying Zheng succeeds while Lu Buwei handles affairs as Wenxin Hou and “仲父,” amid Taiyuan/Jinyang unrest. Only after unification does Ying Zheng honor his dead father as 太上皇 within [[ImperialSymbolicStandardization|imperial symbolic standardization]]; it is not Zichu's lifetime title. ## Qualifications
The claim that Lu Buwei was Ying Zheng’s biological father is a disputed rumor, not a settled genealogy. Qinji 102-1 and 102-2 conflict on Eastern Zhou destruction/九鼎 chronology. Qin Xiaowen’s three-day death cause is unresolved; the 太上皇 honor is after unification.

## What Changed
- Hostage adoption, the three-year reign, contradictory Eastern Zhou dating and posthumous honor are separated.
