# 异人 / 子楚 / 秦庄襄王 / King Zhuangxiang of Qin

异人 / 子楚 / 秦庄襄王 enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-3-lvbuwei-lishishang-fengtou-di-yi-ren-lswa9b864oc-s8vaxtdu15arydu2]] as the neglected Qin royal grandson whom [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] discovers in [[Handan|邯郸]]. The episode calls him 异人 and presents him before his later successful identity: a son of [[QinXiaowenwang|安国君柱 / 秦孝文王]], born to an unfavored mother, ranked mid-pack among many brothers, and sent to [[ZhaoState|赵国]] as a hostage while Qin-Zhao relations are hostile.

His value in the episode comes from the mismatch between present misery and latent dynastic leverage. In ordinary status terms he looks almost useless: he lacks favor at home, has little material support in Zhao, and is mocked as a poorly supplied hostage. In Lu Buwei's commercial-political reading, however, he remains a Qin king's grandson; if succession can be routed through [[HuayangFuren|华阳夫人]], that low-priced claim can become a future throne.

The first agreement between Yiren and Lu Buwei is therefore not a rescue gift. Lu Buwei tells him that his own门庭 can become great only through Yiren's, while Yiren accepts the logic and invites him into private discussion. The episode makes Yiren the object around which [[PoliticalVentureInvestment|political venture investment]] and [[MaternalStatusSuccessionLeverage|子以母贵式继承杠杆]] become visible.

The episode's ending adds the next dynastic consequence: Lu Buwei gives his beloved woman to Yiren, and she later gives birth to [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]]. That detail is recorded here as part of the source's setup for the Qin succession chain, while the later reign of Qin Zhuangxiang Wang remains outside this episode's narrated scope.

[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-4-shishang-wei-shiye-song-laopo-diyi-ren-lo8omg01gkwlojkpiflaep99x-vz]] turns that setup into court recognition. After Lu Buwei reaches [[HuayangFuren|华阳夫人]] through [[YangquanJun|阳泉君]], [[QinXiaowenwang|安国君柱 / 秦孝文王]] establishes Yiren as嫡子 and has a jade tally carved. The episode reads this as a [[WrittenSuccessionCredential|written succession credential]]: Yiren's route to succession now depends on the adopted maternal line he receives from Huayang Furen.

The source also adds Yiren's Zhao Ji and escape branches. Yiren asks Lu Buwei for [[ZhaoJi|赵姬]], later makes her夫人 after she gives birth to Ying Zheng, and eventually escapes [[Handan|邯郸]] with Lu Buwei by bribing guards during the Qin-Zhao crisis. Once in Qin, he wears Chu clothing before Huayang Furen, and she gives him the name 子楚, making his adopted identity emotionally and politically legible.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-1-huangdi-zhong-de-chaochang-daiji-wang-guoshi-le-lt75scavlm8qjn6ulpd-b2kwzwjf]] records the payoff after [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]] dies. [[QinXiaowenwang|安国君柱 / 秦孝文王]] makes Zichu crown prince, and [[ZhaoState|赵国]] reacts by sending [[ZhaoJi|赵姬]] and young [[QinShiHuang|嬴政]] back to Qin. The source frames Zhao's decision as damage control: once Zichu is heir, harming or withholding his family would create a dangerous future enemy.
