# OPEC

## Overview
OPEC coordinates producing states against company and market power, but quotas, national fiscal dependence and demand-side shocks limit collective control.

## Current Profile
Venezuela’s Perez Alfonso and other producing countries are described as seeking leverage against oil majors; later quotas depended on members’ willingness to limit output and on Saudi adjustment. A member’s fiscal exposure and China’s demand flexibility show two limits on producer coordination.

## Key Characteristics
- Producer bargaining origin.
- Quota discipline and oil dependence.
- Demand-side limit to producer power.

## Evidence
- **产油国议价形成：** [[JuanPabloPerezAlfonso]]推动委内瑞拉由与油公司双边[[OilConcessionBargaining|特许权议价]]转向同伊朗、伊拉克、沙特和科威特协作；面对[[SevenSistersOilMajors|七姐妹]]压低标价，1959年开罗场边秘密会面也联系了埃及代表，[[WandaJablonski]]的报道与人脉帮助官员理解公司结构。早期OPEC主要是磋商平台，1973年石油冲击显示减产威力，却不是一次OPEC全体共同政策。[[chevron-venezuela-and-the-paradox-of-plenty]] [[the-secret-meeting-that-launched-opec]]
- **配额与依赖：** 节目将1982年配额制、成员趁高价超产的诱因、[[SaudiArabia|沙特]]承担更多减产的[[SwingProducerRole|调节角色]]和1985年增产反制串成[[ProductionQuotaDiscipline|配额纪律]]的难题；其所称阿联酋退出及“趁长期需求下降前多产”的[[GreenParadox|绿色悖论]]仍是该期报道口径，霍尔木兹航运中断也未必因退出而缓解。另一成员[[Venezuela|委内瑞拉]]在2014年油价下跌后由[[NicolasMaduro]]求助OPEC，凸显石油财政、汇率管制及外汇短缺不能靠配额自动解决。[[the-secret-meeting-that-launched-opec]] [[venezuelas-recent-economic-history-update]]
- **需求侧不是成员资格：** 伊朗战争与[[StraitOfHormuz|霍尔木兹]]冲击的节目称每日约1400万桶原油滞留海湾，油价未达部分分析师预期的150美元；[[China|中国]]据报在2026年2月至4月将进口减半，并动用库存、限制成品油出口和节省燃油。[[MathieuFavasse]]据此称其价格敏感需求可能有稳价作用；“新OPEC”只是[[DemandSideOilMarketPower|需求力量]]类比，不是中国加入产油国组织，也不是独立归因检验。[[pump-and-circumstance-is-china-the-new-opec-6a7c37287cf3c58886773a66]]

## Qualifications
- The podcast’s account of a UAE exit and 2026 Hormuz disruption is source-dated and not independently verified here. The 1973 shock was not itself an OPEC-wide cut. China is a demand-side analogy, not an OPEC member.

## What Changed
- The origin as producer bargaining is separated from the later quota-and-swing-producer coordination challenge.
- China’s demand response limits the producer-only frame without making China part of OPEC.
