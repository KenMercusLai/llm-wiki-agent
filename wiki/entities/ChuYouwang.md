# 楚幽王 / King You of Chu

楚幽王 / King You of Chu enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-109-2-chunshenjun-bei-huangdi-dajiuge-quanzu-miemen-locma2plu7v-vsssm5dr5frc3szw]], where he succeeds [[MiWan|楚考烈王]] after [[LiYuanWarringStates|李源 / 李元]] has [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]] killed at the palace gate. In the episode's reading, he is the crown prince produced by the earlier [[PregnancySuccessionSubstitution|孕身继承替换]] plot and is treated as Chunshenjun's biological son while legally occupying the Chu royal succession.

His political role in this source is mostly structural. He is the young ruler whose accession converts a hidden paternity scheme into the governing reality of [[ChuState|楚国]], but actual court control is attributed to Li Yuan and [[LiYuanSister|李源妹妹]]. The page therefore keeps him as a succession node rather than a developed biography.
