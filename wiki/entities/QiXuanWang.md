# 齐宣王 / King Xuan of Qi

## Overview
Qi Xuan Wang is the Qi ruler in the Mencian dialogues, Yan intervention and Zhang Yi-era diplomatic accounts.

## Current Profile
The sources juxtapose compassionate capacity with the failure of Qi’s occupation of Yan, while conflicting annalistic dates require a bounded succession and death chronology.

## Key Characteristics
- Several early annalistic appearances are explicitly chronologically unstable.
- Rhetorical legitimacy did not ensure humane occupation.
- Qi’s alliances were repeatedly manipulated by persuaders.
- Classical dialogues are not evidence that the ruler implemented Mencian policy.

## Evidence
- **Disputed chronology:** [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] places [[QiWeiwang|齐威王]]'s death, Qi Xuan Wang's accession and recall of [[TianJi|田忌]] in 333 BCE, whereas later episodes put succession in 320 BCE and first regnal year in 319 BCE. A 324 BCE account of [[SuQin|苏秦]]'s appointment after fleeing [[YanState|燕国]], and his advice to enlarge palaces and gardens as [[StrategicLuxurySabotage|奢侈诱导式削弱]], remains an unreconciled [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|chronology-drift]] account. A death notice roughly twelve years too early is corrected to 301 BCE, when Tian Di succeeds as [[QiMinWang|齐湣王]] ([[QiChronologyDispute]]). [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-29-suqin-lizhi-shuangwen-zhishi-gaibian-mingyun-lrf5cqvoriyep2lkbozju-lyjaxv]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-37-suqin-liuguo-jituan-ceo-de-bukan-qingshi-fj2kdqlaxzyy2nqtcutktuwrq5fo]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-45-yi-shi-wei-jian-junwang-daodi-gai-bu-gai-sharen-chengxing-lo3cok7cfwzrxesp-i8miz1fc1db]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-61-shui-shi-lishishang-daner-zui-fei-de-diwang-lvpoaszanascnlrmu2atinlxzmk1]]
- **Yan and the test of 王道:** [[Mengzi|孟子]] is portrayed as hopeful about this ruler despite his ambition, candid weaknesses and the “以羊易牛” compassion story; the podcast distinguishes [[WangdaoVsBadao|王道 and 霸道]] from institutional results. Qi Xuan Wang claims restoration of ruler-son order for [[ShiBei|市被]] and Yan's crown prince, then sends [[ZhangZiQiGeneral|张子]] against [[ZiZhi|子之]] after their attack fails; killing [[YanWangKuai|燕王哙]] and occupying [[YanState|燕国]] complicate the [[LiAsPoliticalOrder|ritual-order]] rationale. Mencius asks whether Yan's people benefited rather than whether quick conquest signals Heaven's endorsement ([[MencianBenevolentGovernment]], [[MencianPeopleBasedLegitimacy]], [[MencianIdealismLimits]]). Rebellion leaves the king ashamed but without effective corrective action ([[CorrectableLeadershipError]]). [[30-mengzi-zhanguo-fenqing-de-chizi-zhixin-592494138]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-48-junwang-weisha-buneng-chuanwei-zaixiang-lnwhljnn888mzlj7let2ptn1pcdm]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-49-mengzi-yue-wo-jiao-ni-zhiguo-ni-que-ba-wo-dang-huaping-lhy14pbf9thcfdiaulcmf8dwcef8]]
- **Qi diplomacy after Yan:** In 313 BCE [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] of [[ChuState|Chu]] has Song Yi insult Qi Xuan Wang to demonstrate a Qi-Chu rupture after [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] delays Qin's promised land, helping turn [[QiState|齐国]] toward [[QinState|秦国]]. In 311 BCE Zhang Yi, rewarded by [[YingSi|秦惠文王]], argues that Qin-Chu, Han, Wei and Zhao alignments expose Qi and wins acceptance through [[EncirclementThreatPersuasion|encirclement fear]] against the old [[HezongAlliance|合纵]] assumptions. Another episode has Qi attack [[WeiState|魏国]] after Zhang Yi arrives, then withdraw when disclosure shows this was precisely Zhang Yi and [[QinWuwang|秦武王]]'s desired clash. These are source-bounded diplomatic scenes, not an unbroken chronology. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-50-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-ba-chuhuaiwang-huyou-que-de-loxg3ylhmskvdm-axtco5y-acege]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-54-wo-zun-ni-shi-wang-ni-dang-wo-shi-pi-lvhcz0ban9frlmwshd9vch-q7-ie]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-55-zhangyi-tongshi-guiren-qinhuiwang-lsclfbwn06fgzooipf5up9sdkedy]]
- **Text and later reuse:** In a Mencian garden comparison, Qi Xuan Wang's smaller park oppresses subjects because ordinary people are excluded and punished, unlike public enjoyment under the [[SharedPleasureGovernanceTest|shared-pleasure test]]. A later court's selective citation without that access condition illustrates [[ClassicCitationLegitimationCapture|citation capture]]. [[ZhongliChun|钟离春 / 钟无艳]] and [[JixiaAcademy|稷下学宫]] are brief cultural context in the episode, not independent evidence of his governing success. ## Qualifications
The 333 BCE death notice, 324 BCE Su Qin appointment, and premature Zhouji 49 death notice are not reconciled with the later 320/319 accession and 301 BCE death. Mencian dialogue, political intentions and garden anecdote are literary/episode interpretations, not proof of implemented benevolent rule.

## What Changed
- The 333/324 BCE notices remain disputed against the 320/319 BCE succession and 301 BCE death; Yan and diplomacy are separately attributed.
