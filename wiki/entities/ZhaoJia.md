# 赵嘉 / Zhao Jia

赵嘉 / Zhao Jia enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-111-1-ruoda-de-zhaoguo-jing-wang-yu-yige-jinv-zhi-shou-lhrqb8-swlkuyoydgjyn8ldnlkzc]] as the original crown prince of [[ZhaoState|赵国]] displaced by [[ZhaoDaoxiangwang|赵道襄王]]. The source says Zhao Daoxiang Wang changed the succession in favor of [[ZhaoYoumiuWang|赵迁 / 赵幽缪王]], the son of a favored woman.

The page is source-scoped and intentionally narrow. Zhao Jia matters here as the displaced legitimate line whose removal lets the host frame Zhao's 236 BCE crisis as more than a battlefield setback: succession order has already been weakened before Qin's attack lands.
