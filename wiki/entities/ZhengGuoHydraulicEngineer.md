# 郑国 / Zheng Guo (Hydraulic Engineer)

郑国 / Zheng Guo enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-104-yinmou-shisuan-rang-qinguo-bantu-you-you-you-pengzhang-le-lgnyhbduqutegtksrjwcvhmn8-t6]] as the [[HanState|韩国]] hydraulic specialist sent into [[QinState|秦国]] to promote a massive canal project. The source presents him as both an engineer and a strategic agent: Han hopes the work will consume Qin labor and treasury resources long enough to delay Qin's eastward attacks.

His defense after exposure is the crucial turn. Zheng Guo admits that the original purpose was to extend Han's life for a few years, but argues that the nearly completed [[ZhengGuoCanal|郑国渠]] will bring Qin lasting benefit. [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] accepts the argument, allows the work to finish, and the canal receives Zheng Guo's name.
