# Bitcoin

## Overview
Bitcoin is presented across these sources as a scarce, network-dependent digital asset: proposed money and “digital gold,” an imperfect payment instrument, a volatile tradable asset, and a subject of public-finance and compliance experiments. None of these roles follows automatically from fixed supply.

## Current Profile
The accounts disagree on whether a capped-supply internet consensus can become a monetary anchor or useful crisis refuge. Local adoption in [[Lugano]] demonstrates some transactions but not complete everyday coverage; near-term geopolitical flight to gold can coexist with Bitcoin's proposed longer-term cross-border utility. Future agent identity infrastructure remains [[ZhouLuohua]]'s hypothesis, not deployed functionality.

## Key Characteristics
- Scarcity, network consensus and mining motivate a monetary-anchor narrative, contested by payment-efficiency and demand questions.
- Retail and municipal payments have concrete but incomplete local demonstrations.
- Crisis portability and “digital gold” rhetoric differ from observed acute safe-haven performance.
- Investor confidence, trading structure, scarcity and conviction shape risk and potential upside without guaranteeing returns.
- State reserve initiatives create public-custody and taxpayer-risk questions not yet tested in a downturn.
- Onboarding, custody and regulated buying are practical infrastructure requirements for participation.
- Virtual-asset compliance and speculative future agent uses define separate risk and possibility boundaries.

## Evidence
- **Monetary design:** [[129-huobi-de-benzhi-yiji-huangjin-de-zhenzheng-jiazhi-chuantai-shifen-xiyin-lsjbfttqxf58uk-a4g8-srretkwb]] contrasts [[ShiLei]]'s network-belief/possible digital-anchor reading with [[DavidWeng]]'s doubts about rigid supply and everyday payment in a growing economy. [[jifa-dongwu-jingshen-chuangzao-gengduo-jihui-lndyfdmtgo-9l5cr8oy82t1xnlfe]] places mining beside gold-era [[ProofOfWorkMonetaryValidation]] in the revised [[BitStandardBook]], as a commentator's historical analogy rather than proof of identical monetary function. [[ZhouLuohua]] also argues that decentralization raises coordination costs but can let outsiders compete against entrenched interests; this [[OpportunityGeneratingDecentralization]] thesis is his social interpretation, not an observed Bitcoin outcome.
- **Local payment:** [[tech-20260114-0114-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260114-0114-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports [[LuganoPlanB]] launched in 2022; hundreds of shops/restaurants and some city services accept Bitcoin, with payment terminals distributed to merchants. [[MiaLiponi]]'s 11-day Bitcoin-only experiment still could not cover transit, fuel, bills, dentists and some medical services; a damaged [[SatoshiNakamoto]] statue and skeptical residents qualify public acceptance.
- **Crisis roles:** [[tech-20260312-0312-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260312-0312-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports gold rising after news of a US attack on [[Iran]] while Bitcoin did not draw comparable immediate safety demand. [[GilLuria]] of [[DADavidson]] distinguishes volatility/risky-use stigma from possible 24/7 liquid, divisible transfers, inflation/dollar-weakness exposure and [[CrossBorderCryptoCapitalFlight]] from Iran or [[China]] during longer disruption.
- **Market behavior:** [[tech-20260316-0316-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260316-0316-mp-tech-pod-128]] cites [[DaveReibstein]]'s [[WhartonSchool]] monthly confidence index and price–confidence feedback, with many consumers treating crypto as a risky stock and older savers as “gamble money.” [[ep88-chuanyue-lianghua-zhifu-ximengsi-ai-hui-rang-putongren-geng-rongyi-zhuanqian-haishi-geng-nan-lhvigzza2ugmayezkbrxufkmp4l1]]'s fictional [[JimSimons]]-style teaching contrasts 24-hour fragmented-market trading with cash-flow value investing. [[e43-zhang-xiaoyu-mengyan-duihua-xuzhe-meiyou-genghaode-shenghuo-lrsfby01kuournly5mlkkzi-ayls]] treats early holdings as an asymmetric payoff example, while [[vol-101-ji-anquan-shouyi-you-gao-liudongxing-hai-hao-de-touzi-daodi-cunzai-ma-lr7xt-2cgoru2gfdbatvbiv78ixg]] says scarcity can be offset by substitutes or changed belief, and [[vol-110-touzi-jiushi-dui-shijieguan-de-toupiao-maichu-zichan-peizhi-di-yi-bu-wanjiepian-lk5jkgnzvzerymcixylsdqdtn7f8]] says conviction or accidental lockup, not casual trend following, helped some long-term holders withstand volatility.
- **Public reserves:** [[tech-20260414-0414-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260414-0414-mp-tech-pod-128]] attributes to [[LizFarmer]] the contrast between [[Texas]]-style separate reserve funds and [[Arizona]]'s seized-asset custody (with [[NewHampshire]] in the wider state-law discussion); no state Bitcoin reserve had yet been drawn in a downturn in that report.
- **Market access:** [[socialradarspod-brianarmstrong-final]] recounts [[BrianArmstrong]] seeing the white paper on [[HackerNews]], buying on [[MtGox]], and learning through wallet-user calls that users needed to buy Bitcoin before storing it. The eventual [[Coinbase]] buy button, bank transfers, fraud controls and regulated trust address that access gap.
- **Compliance and possible next use:** [[ep44-momo-koudai-limian-de-qian-juran-shi-zang-de-li-4f9d60jmelqituybbioyyflxr]] explains traceable chains but identity-opacity and layered virtual-asset laundering risk, and the old comparison notes that volatile Bitcoin may be less attractive for some chains than [[Stablecoins]]. [[jifa-dongwu-jingshen-chuangzao-gengduo-jihui-lndyfdmtgo-9l5cr8oy82t1xnlfe]] separately imagines [[DecentralizedAgentIdentity]] and rewards for future robots/agents; this is a proposal, not Bitcoin's present capability.

## Qualifications
- Fixed supply is not a value floor, and the [[DigitalGold]] label cannot replace the adverse immediate-crisis observation. State diversification arguments have no cited stress-test of reserve drawdown; payment adoption in one city does not establish general monetary utility.
- The confidence-index discussion supplies neither sample design nor population weights here. Early upside is a retrospective, not a repeatable payoff profile; the Jim Simons dialogue is a fictional educational device, not a historical quote.
- [[ZhouLuohua]]'s account/reward and “final pre-communist currency” ideas are speculative positions, not independently established implementations. Money-laundering comparisons are source-scoped and do not absolve any asset or platform of AML obligations.

## What Changed
- The profile separates payment, safe haven, investment, custody and monetary-anchor propositions so evidence for one is not treated as proof of another.
- Acute gold/Bitcoin divergence is held against longer-horizon portability claims rather than flattened into a verdict that either always holds.
- Hypothetical agent identity is explicitly distinguished from existing Bitcoin infrastructure.
