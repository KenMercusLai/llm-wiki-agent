## Overview

赵璇 / Zhao Xuan enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-580-huigu-sizhou-shuishi-nide-kaoshan-liid3tymhojmajkyeqoq6vryprhz|Hanji 580]] as the Yushi Dafu who joins [[ZhuBoWesternHan|朱博]] in pursuing [[WangMang|王莽]] after the attack on [[ShiDanWesternHan|施丹]]. His role is narrow: he helps frame Wang Mang's past Da Sima status as evidence for harsher punishment, but [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]] refuses to strip Wang Mang's title and fief.

## Current Profile

Hanji 580 places Zhao Xuan inside the anti-Wang pressure sequence. After Zhu Bo and Zhao Xuan help persuade Aidi against Shi Dan's title and fief, they continue with a memorial against Wang Mang. They argue that Wang Mang once held the highest military-administrative rank but failed to support Aidi's filial-title order, so his offense deserves public execution if not for the pardon and should still cost him marquisate and fief.

The case fails at the final step. Aidi sends Wang Mang back to his fief but leaves his title and fief intact because Wang Zhengjun remains the protective senior dowager. Zhao Xuan therefore marks the limit of formal accusation when the target still has a stronger kinship-patron channel.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han Yushi Dafu active in Jianping 2 / 5 BCE.
- Works with Zhu Bo in the attack sequence against Shi Dan and Wang Mang.
- Frames Wang Mang's prior Da Sima status as aggravating evidence.
- Seeks removal of Wang Mang's title and fief after pardon removes execution from reach.
- Fails because Wang Zhengjun's senior authority protects Wang Mang from full reduction.
