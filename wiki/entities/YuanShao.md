# 袁绍 / Yuan Shao

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-961-neihuang-zhizhan-caocao-chulu-fengmang-ljdlt6kiu8tg0qws-jlyej-yhgxr|Hanji 961]] adds an earlier Yuan Shao-Gongsun Zan pressure scene before the later northern-war pages. Yuan Shao's household is staying with [[LiuDaiLateHan|刘岱]] in [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]], and [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] demands that Liu Dai hand them over after their alliance turns hostile.

The episode uses [[ChengYu|程昱]] to read Yuan Shao as the less immediate threat to Liu Dai. Cheng Yu says Yuan Shao has plans but is unlikely to harm Liu Dai if Liu Dai stays neutral, while distant Gongsun Zan cannot be relied on even if his cavalry commander [[FanFangLateHan|范方]] is nearby. The later [[NeihuangBattleLateHan|内黄之战]] then helps Yuan Shao indirectly because [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s strike relieves rear pressure from [[YuDuLateHan|于毒]], [[SuiGuLateHan|眭固]], and [[YufuluoChanyu|于扶罗]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-967-ta-canyu-mousha-dongzhuo-jingneng-taoguo-sishen-weilie-lv24biyswj9ljdld5htynhauhph3|Hanji 967]] ends by moving [[LyuBu|吕布]] toward Yuan Shao after Lü Bu leaves [[YuanShu|袁术]] and then [[ZhangYangLateHan|张杨]]. The source uses Yuan Shao mainly as the next host created by [[LiJueLateHan|李傕]]'s bounty pressure, setting up the Hanji 970 alliance and assassination-attempt branch.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-969-yuanshi-xiongdi-neidou-shui-neng-jianlou-lsgj6sewo35jpsgfm-koivijtr6r|Hanji 969]] backfills Yuan Shao immediately before the Hanji 970 northern crisis. After [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] is checked after Longcou, Yuan Shao turns toward the [[YangzhouLateHan|扬州]] office fight by sending [[YuanYiLateHan|袁遗]] to replace [[ChenWenLateHan|陈温]], only for [[YuanShu|袁术]] to defeat him. The same source makes Yuan Shao one side of Cao Cao's "two tigers" reading and shows his wider northern rivalry spilling into [[QingzhouLateHan|青州]] through [[YuanTan|袁谭]] against [[TianKaiLateHan|田楷]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-970-yuanshao-ansha-lvbu-weihe-hui-shibai-ln2pnvit-qgpnhn-pne7o-p1irrs|Hanji 970]] backfills Yuan Shao's northern base crisis before the later Yanzhou and adviser-judgment branches. While Yuan Shao is at Boluojin, [[WeiCommanderyLateHan|魏郡]] soldiers revolt with [[YuDuLateHan|余毒]]'s [[HeishanArmy|黑山军]] forces and attack [[YeLateHan|邺城]], leaving his household exposed until [[TaoShengLateHan|陶生]] protects and escorts the families to Chiqiu. Yuan Shao then rewards Tao Sheng and turns toward punitive campaigns against Yu Du, [[ZhangYanLateHan|张燕]], and other mountain leaders.

The same source adds Yuan Shao's failed attempt to remove [[LyuBu|吕布]]. After using Lü Bu against Zhang Yan, Yuan Shao becomes hostile to Lü Bu's violent retinue and wraps a killing attempt in appointment and escort language. The [[YuanShaoLyuBuAssassinationAttempt|袁绍刺吕布未遂]] therefore adds an earlier case of Yuan Shao as a strong but insecure patron: he can survive a rear crisis and defeat enemies, yet his handling of Lü Bu converts a temporary ally into a future destabilizing actor.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-974-caocao-xiang-ta-tuo-qi-xian-zi-weihe-zhuanshen-ai-daozi-lhfabab7xv2vfsgnhr9ozwqhmhd|Hanji 974]] adds an earlier Zhang Miao pressure layer before Yuan Shao's later Yanzhou grain-warning branch. After [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] criticizes Yuan Shao's arrogance during the anti-Dong Zhuo coalition context, Yuan Shao reportedly asks [[CaoCao|曹操]] to kill him; Cao Cao refuses because Zhang Miao is a close friend.

The same source makes Yuan Shao an indirect cause of the betrayal even when he is not commanding it. Once [[LyuBu|吕布]] forms a sworn tie with Zhang Miao, Yuan Shao's hostility makes Zhang Miao fear that Cao Cao might one day obey Yuan Shao. That fear becomes part of the [[PersonalTrustPoliticalFragility|私交信任的政治脆弱性]] that [[ChenGongLateHan|陈宫]] later exploits.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-975-yanzhou-zhiluan-xunyu-ruhe-bang-caocao-shou-dabenying-ln0hh4jrokkwtqsmxmqhdjzcosdr|Hanji 975]] adds a pre-Hanji-976 adviser contrast through [[TianFengLateHan|田丰]]. [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] asks Yuan Shao for a large grain loan during the [[YanzhouLateHan|兖州]] rebellion, and Tian Feng reads the request as concealed support for [[LyuBu|吕布]] rather than neutral aid.

The source uses the episode to sharpen Yuan Shao's recurring weakness before the broader adviser-roster summary. Tian Feng can identify the problem, but the host frames Yuan Shao as face-sensitive and hesitant, making the episode another setup for [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]] and the later claim that a strong adviser group only matters if the ruler converts advice into action.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-976-sanguo-moushi-dapandian-shui-shi-ni-xinzhong-de-shen-ljgpbosq-9xiowpgaeyossmgst6x|Hanji 976]] adds a compact "strong adviser roster, weak decision conversion" summary. The episode names [[JuShou|沮授]], [[TianFengLateHan|田丰]], and [[XuYouLateHan|许攸]] as a Hebei adviser group that should have given Yuan Shao overwhelming strategic capacity, then explains the failure as a leader-side decision black hole.

This branch bridges existing Yuan Shao pages: Ju Shou's missed welcome-the-emperor window, [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] leaving because Yuan Shao cannot use people, and [[TenVictoriesTenDefeats|十胜十败论]] all become cases of the same problem. The source's judgment is kept thematic, but it reinforces [[TalentAsStateTreasure|以贤臣为宝]] and [[CourtFeedbackCollapse|君臣反馈失灵]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-981-sanguo-zui-gang-moushi-chengyu-yi-ju-hua-rang-caocao-tanfu-lhlhlrmf2gvspwyvjkxg6kz44fth|Hanji 981]] adds Yuan Shao's earlier leverage over [[CaoCao|曹操]] during Cao Cao's Yanzhou crisis. Yuan Shao asks Cao Cao to send his family to Ye, offering a path that looks like protection while making Cao Cao's household collateral. [[ChengYu|程昱]] rejects the move by saying Yuan Shao has ambition without sufficient judgment, making Yuan Shao the stronger but strategically suspect patron inside [[HostageBackedDependencyTrap|家属质押式依附陷阱]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-992-luanshi-you-duo-kepa-jiemi-changan-de-xuese-mengyan-ltuxzrb-1x5y2ctwo4drggxn5stz|Hanji 992]] backfills Yuan Shao's missed emperor-welcome decision before the later Cao Cao title branch. [[JuShou|沮授]] argues that Yuan Shao should bring [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] to Ye and use the imperial name to command rival lords. [[GuoTu|郭图]] and [[ChunyuQiong|淳于琼]] oppose the plan because hosting the emperor would constrain Yuan Shao's own decisions.

The source treats Yuan Shao's refusal as a major [[LegitimacyWindowSeizure|合法性窗口抢占]] failure. He has troops, family prestige, and a northern base, but he does not turn the still-recognized Han title into public authority; the host therefore reads the choice as ceding the "挟天子以令诸侯" opportunity to [[CaoCao|曹操]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-996-luanshi-zanghong-jiangyiqi-de-ren-xiachang-zui-biequ-lgpa-m6h6bvhyintnuj6zztopui76|Hanji 996]] and [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-997-zanghong-zhisi-shi-yibaoyuntian-haishi-qingjie-nandu-lln2liblq-kyjrsvitnbh7cspbax|Hanji 997]] backfill Yuan Shao's earlier [[ZangHong|臧洪]] case. In Hanji 996, Zang Hong begs Yuan Shao to rescue [[ZhangChaoLateHan|张超]] at [[YongqiuLateHan|雍丘]], but Yuan Shao refuses because the demand would cut against his alignment with [[CaoCao|曹操]]. After Zang Hong breaks with him and holds [[DongCommandery|东郡]], Yuan Shao uses [[ChenLinLateHan|陈琳]] to persuade him, then escalates the siege.

Hanji 997 continues the branch after Yuan Shao captures Zang Hong and initially wants to spare him if he submits. Zang Hong refuses, so Yuan Shao executes him.

The source keeps Yuan Shao morally exposed without making the decision one-sided. Zang Hong condemns Yuan Shao's failure to honor the friendship tie through [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]], but the host also notes that Yuan Shao is Zang Hong's current superior and that Cao Cao is Yuan Shao's ally. Yuan Shao therefore becomes the powerholder in a [[DeathPartyLoyalty|死党之义与守职奉上]] conflict where old-master loyalty collides with current office and alliance structure.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1005-sunce-zhuding-zaoshi-shi-xingge-jueding-mingyun-lpqnere2cwcrngggyezd-bsqahx4|Hanji 1005]] backfills Yuan Shao's early status collision with [[CaoCao|曹操]] after [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]] reaches [[Xudu|许都]]. The court first reproaches Yuan Shao for having territory and troops but not sending rescue forces, then appoints him taiwei and Marquis of Ye. Yuan Shao resents that Cao Cao still holds the higher great-general title and says Cao Cao is using the emperor to command him.

Cao Cao's answer is [[StrategicTitleConcession|战略性名位让步]]. Rather than make the rank dispute a premature war, Cao Cao yields the great-general title to Yuan Shao and accepts sikong plus acting chariots-and-cavalry general. The source presents Yuan Shao here as a status-sensitive stronger rival whose emotional reaction changes Cao Cao's tactical use of titles.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1006-guojia-buman-yuanshao-weihe-que-neng-guishun-caocao-lup-p-inz1sdlhpnaggqjp0eavar|Hanji 1006]] introduces 袁绍 / Yuan Shao as the powerful northern host whom [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] rejects after a short stay. The source says Yuan Shao treats Guo Jia politely, but Guo Jia concludes that he does not understand how to use people, cannot distinguish urgent from secondary matters, and hesitates too much to achieve hegemony.

This makes Yuan Shao a negative talent-use case before the later leadership-comparison branch. Xin Ping and Guo Tu remain with him because they still trust the Yuan family's name and large force, while Guo Jia reads those visible assets as insufficient without executive judgment.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-998-baima-jiangjun-gongsunzan-cong-sanqian-baima-dao-kunshou-gucheng-lp-1l2gvbsbfnyznutdghxwwvqna|Hanji 998]] backfills the Gongsun Zan side of that northern war. Yuan Shao appears as the outside power whose forces connect with [[XianyuFu|鲜于辅]], [[YanRou|阎柔]], [[Wuhuan|乌桓]], [[Xianbei|鲜卑]], and [[LiuHeLateHan|刘和]] in the coalition that defeats Gongsun Zan and drives him toward [[YijingFortress|易京]].

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1015-sanguo-luanshi-weishenme-yuanshu-gan-chengdi-lim0jemkwyjcesj2nvsr3jndhxfk|Hanji 1015]] later adds Yuan Shao as the major northern power whose current war against [[GongsunZan|公孙瓒]] gives [[CaoCao|曹操]] time to act elsewhere. Guo Jia uses that northern commitment to argue that Cao Cao should first remove [[LyuBu|吕布]], because leaving Lü Bu available to help Yuan Shao would make the later confrontation much harder.

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-1016-sanguo-luanshi-li-chengui-jiazu-de-mingyun-jueze-ltuckdcxpyxk-xmzp8rt-2gjfzcs|Hanji 1016]] then adds Yuan Shao as the major northern power whom [[EmperorXianOfHan|汉献帝]]'s court formally recognizes while [[YuanShu|袁术]]'s imperial claim is being treated as usurpation. The source says the emperor sends [[KongRong|孔融]] with a tally to Ye and appoints Yuan Shao as great general, with oversight of Jizhou, Qingzhou, Youzhou, and Bingzhou.

Hanji 1014 supplies the immediate contrast frame before that sequencing. Yuan Shao's arrogant letter makes [[CaoCao|曹操]] angry but also forces an assessment of relative strength; [[XunYu|荀彧]] and [[GuoJia|郭嘉]] respond with [[TenVictoriesTenDefeats|十胜十败论]], where Yuan Shao is portrayed as ceremonious, suspicious, hesitant, faction-prone, and weaker at reward, punishment, and military substance than Cao Cao.

The page is source-scoped. Hanji 1016 does not narrate Yuan Shao's broader career; it uses the appointment to show that Han-court titles still matter in the late-Han competition. Yuan Shao receives an official place inside the imperial naming order, while Yuan Shu's self-claimed imperial name makes him a target.
