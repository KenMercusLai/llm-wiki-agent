# 韩馥 / Han Fu (late Han)

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] supplies Han Fu's post-[[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] collapse before the later Xun Yu camp-choice branch. After [[YuanShao|袁绍]] takes the province, [[ZhuHanLateHan|朱瀚]] attacks Han Fu's household and injures Han Fu's eldest son, showing how a former superior becomes exposed once office and coercive backing are gone.

The source then follows Han Fu to [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] in [[Chenliu|陈留]], where a whispered conversation between Yuan Shao's envoy and Zhang Miao makes him believe he is about to be killed. Han Fu's suicide becomes the episode's main case of [[PostPowerSafetyCollapse|失势后的安全崩塌]]: loss of land, team, and judgment turns ambiguous signals into unbearable threat.

韩馥 / Han Fu (late Han) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-960-caowei-dingji-zhinang-xunyu-weihe-qi-yuan-tou-cao-lmor1txqpm88itt4incalusconjr|Hanji 960]] as the Jizhou protector [[XunYu|荀彧]] expected to find after leaving [[YingchuanCommandery|颍川]]. The source says Xun Yu moved his kin north because Yingchuan was exposed to war, but arrived to find that [[YuanShao|袁绍]] had already taken Han Fu's position and territory.

Han Fu's role is therefore less a full biography than a route marker in [[CrisisMigrationJudgment|危局迁徙判断]]. He represents the safer official shelter Xun Yu intended, while Yuan Shao's takeover forces Xun Yu into a more direct judgment about which warlord camp can actually use talent and protect a future order.
