## Overview

冯嫽 / Feng Liao enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-463-caicaikan-zai-hanxuandi-yanzhong-shui-shi-zuidade-gongchen-lqblzdg7sdkzln4vxhtnnye92292|Hanji 463]] as the elderly Western Han woman who voluntarily returns to [[Wusun|乌孙]] after [[XieyouPrincessWesternHan|解忧公主]] comes back to Chang'an. The episode presents her as a trusted frontier diplomat whose reputation among Wusun and Western Regions people lets Han stabilize the young ruler Xingmi without relying only on force.

## Current Profile

The current evidence is narrow but distinctive. Hanji 463 says Wusun's large kunmi Yuanguimi and Chimi have died, and Xingmi succeeds while still too young and weak to command obedience. Feng Liao, already over seventy in the episode's telling, asks [[EmperorXuanOfHan|汉宣帝]] for permission to go back to Wusun and help settle the situation. Xuan agrees; Wusun and Western Regions people welcome her return; and, with her support, Xingmi suppresses rebellion and the Han-Wusun relationship remains friendly through the later succession to Cili Mi.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han woman whose influence belongs to the Wusun frontier-diplomacy field.
- Associated with Xieyou Princess's Wusun network but remains active after Xieyou's return to Han.
- Acts voluntarily despite old age, asking to return to Wusun when local succession is unstable.
- Trusted by Wusun and Western Regions people enough that her arrival has political effect.
- Helps the young Xingmi pacify rebellion and preserve Han-Wusun friendship.

## Qualifications

This page is bounded to Hanji 463. It does not reconstruct Feng Liao's full biography, earlier service with Xieyou Princess, exact titles, original mission documents, or the full Wusun genealogy around Xingmi and Cili Mi. The episode's age statement and stabilizing effect are kept source-scoped.
