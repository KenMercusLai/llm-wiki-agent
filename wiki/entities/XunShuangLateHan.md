# 荀爽 / Xun Shuang (late Han)

荀爽 / Xun Shuang enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-951-tianjiang-hengcai-jiujing-shi-haoshi-haishi-huaishi-lovwgng2wrtfno-s9ttpybbhksvh|Hanji 951]] as the sikong who helps soften the confrontation after [[YangBiao|杨彪]] openly opposes [[DongZhuo|董卓]]'s move from [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] to [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]]. His role is not developed as biography; he is part of the court's remaining damage-control layer when direct remonstrance risks lethal escalation.

In this source, Xun Shuang stands beside [[HuangWanLateHan|黄琬]] as a figure of [[DefensiveSpeechUnderTyranny|defensive speech under tyranny]]. The point is not that the court can check Dong Zhuo, but that a few senior officials can still temporarily reduce immediate danger even while the underlying [[CoerciveCapitalRelocation|coercive capital relocation]] proceeds.
