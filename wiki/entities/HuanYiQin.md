# 桓齮 / Huan Yi (Qin)

桓齮 / Huan Yi (Qin) enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-111-1-ruoda-de-zhaoguo-jing-wang-yu-yige-jinv-zhi-shou-lhrqb8-swlkuyoydgjyn8ldnlkzc]] as one of the Qin commanders sent against [[ZhaoState|赵国]] in 236 BCE, while Zhao is campaigning against [[YanState|燕国]].

The episode names him alongside [[WangJianQin|王翦]] and [[YangDuanhe|杨端和]]. In the source's campaign account, Huan Yi takes [[YeWarringStates|邺城]] and Anyang, making him part of the same Qin opportunistic strike that turns Yan-Zhao conflict into Qin advantage.
