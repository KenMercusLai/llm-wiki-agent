# Emmett Shear

## Overview
Emmett Shear co-founded [[Kiko]], [[JustinTV|Justin.tv]] and [[Twitch]], briefly led [[OpenAI]] during its 2023 board crisis, and later described [[Softmax]]'s AI-alignment research.

## Current Profile
His first-person startup account connects early failure to cash discipline, gaming-led product focus and founder context-sharing; Justin Kan's recollections corroborate parts of the trajectory but neither interview is a financial audit. The OpenAI interval and Softmax research are separate governance and research contexts.

## Key Characteristics
- Kiko's product mismatch became a cash and learning bridge to a second venture.
- Justin.tv's lifecasting attention turned into a creator platform and then a gaming-led Twitch focus.
- Runway control and investor communication made growth possible despite financing skepticism.
- An interim OpenAI appointment placed him inside a disputed board crisis.
- At Softmax and Twitch he describes alignment via collective understanding and founder mode via context propagation.

## Evidence
- **Early constraint:** [[socialradarspod-emmettshear-v2]] recalls the first 2005 [[YCombinator]] cohort, [[Kiko]] losing its rationale after Google Calendar, sale on eBay to Tucows for about $258,000, roughly $35,000 kept by each of Shear and [[JustinKan]] and $15,000 each lent to Justin.tv. [[tsr-s5-justinkan-v2-audio-tsr-s5-justinkan-v2-audio]] gives Justin's recollection of the rushed YC application, his $258,100 sale figure and the post-Kiko pitch to [[PaulGraham]]; these illuminate [[FounderCashFlowConstraint]] and [[FounderProductFit]].
- **Product shift:** [[socialradarspod-emmettshear-v2]] and [[tsr-s5-justinkan-v2-audio-tsr-s5-justinkan-v2-audio]] describe weak retention after lifecasting's viral start, requests for viewers' own streams, [[KyleVogt]]'s video infrastructure, [[MichaelSeibel]]'s joining and gaming usage that Shear favored. The focused [[Twitch]] product was acquired by [[Amazon]] in 2014; Seibel later recruited Shear back to YC, where he described interviews and office hours as exhausting but useful: founders compressed high-stakes judgment about fundraising, product and psychology into short conversations.
- **Operating risk:** [[socialradarspod-emmettshear-v2]] reports about eight weeks' runway in 2008, monthly P&L reviews, cuts and revenue experiments before Twitch was largely funded from Justin.tv cash flow. Shear reports roughly 30% month-on-month Twitch growth, negative dollar-weighted churn and around 40 VC rejections before Bessemer: his [[StartupRunwayDiscipline]] and [[InvestorRiskNarrative]] are reported experience, not audited metrics.
- **Board crisis:** [[tsr-s4-samaltman-v4-tsr-s4-samaltman-v4]] recounts [[SamAltman]]'s version of Shear's appointment during the [[OpenAIBoardCrisis]], Altman's announced [[Microsoft]] project and employee pressure for his return. [[tsr-s2-wrapup-v1]] is a short retrospective noting that the previously recorded interview became topical after Shear's brief appointment, along with the hosts' memorable quiche Lorraine anecdote; it is not independent crisis evidence.
- **Organization and research:** [[tsr-ycoffsite-emmettshear-v1-audioonly-tsr-ycoffsite-emmettshear-v1-audioonly]] reports Shear's account of founding [[Softmax]] with [[AdamGoldstein]], later joined by [[DavidBlumen]], to test agent self/other/“we” recognition in simulations and learning environments ([[AICollectiveAlignment]], [[LearningEnvironmentCenteredAITraining]]). His [[FounderMode]] lesson moves from direct coding/customer work to weekly keynotes, repeated company context ([[FounderContextPropagation]]) and organization/information-flow changes under [[ConwaysLawOrganizationalDesign]].

## Qualifications
- Shear and Justin supply complementary participant memories, not independently reconciled finances; even the eBay sale is rounded differently. Shear's 30% growth, churn and VC figures are his account.
- Altman's account of board motives and the Shear appointment is one side of a contested governance event. The season wrap-up only confirms later public salience.
- Softmax's alignment frame and parenting analogy state a research ambition, not demonstrated aligned-agent outcomes. Founder context-sharing does not mean “one brain, many hands” or rejecting delegation.

## What Changed
- Differentiates startup operating evidence from later governance and alignment ambitions, and distinguishes interviews from retrospective recap.
