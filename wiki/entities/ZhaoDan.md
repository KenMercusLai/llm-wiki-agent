# 赵丹 / 赵孝成王 / King Xiaocheng of Zhao

## Overview
赵丹在父赵何死后继赵王，早年由赵威后摄政；亲政后受上党十七城，长平时改用赵括并陷入外交孤立。战败后他又尝试求和、守邯郸与联楚魏，显示错误抉择和危机求存须同时记述。

## Current Profile
这些节目由战事、策士与王室互动拼出其政治处境，不足以把长平的全部伤亡归于赵丹一人。秦国压力、韩献上党、将领与使者的判断同样构成因果链；孝成王后期仍能任廉颇抗燕，不能把长平败局写成赵国彻底丧失军力。

## Key Characteristics
- 幼年即位时母后摄政，齐援与长安君为质暂解秦国进攻。
- 亲政受上党十七城时低估韩秦冲突转嫁的风险。
- 长平谈和与阵前换将使赵国的外交、后勤与军事处境叠加恶化。
- 战后和约、六城割让、贵族庇护及邯郸外援相互牵制。
- 战后仍通过联军解围、奖赏信陵君与廉颇抗燕维系国家能力。

## Evidence
- **继位与摄政：** 赵何去世、魏齐投平原君时赵丹继赵位，范雎以秦势追索魏齐使新朝承压。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-90-jiemi-xianqin-zui-wanmei-de-fuchou-liu7gbl-bffknkw6dx5udqcq4tcm]] 265 BCE 秦攻赵、赵威后临朝；齐要长安君作质，触龙从母亲关爱与儿子未来安全入手劝服威后，送质后齐援抵达、秦退。这是摄政者的决定，不应归功于幼主亲自游说。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-91-chulong-ruhe-qiaokai-shougua-zhaotaihou-de-men-1-lmgd7kdl1nrfwcrtkqttumtlpqok]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-91-chulong-ruhe-qiaokai-shougua-zhaotaihou-de-men-2-liy5yulg2ysop-jwfigvdkub1pk7]]
- **上党与长平的责任链：** 母后死后赵丹亲政，冯亭拟以韩上党十七城归赵；赵豹警告秦会将攻韩压力转至赵，平原君主张受地，赵丹取后者并赏冯亭、官属。节目另引《史记》梦兆：赵丹梦穿杂色衣、乘飞龙而中途坠入金玉之中；仅作文学式预示，不用它证明政策因果。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-1-ljshdryvf9qgabtn-siontthzsok]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-2-lrlulw0b6v0fb2xpr2i5ifem1h4n]] 长平初战受挫，赵丹遣郑朱直接求和而不先巩固与楚齐合纵，秦公然接待使者却未议成，使赵受孤立。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-1-loxyybm3njrtnudydafwzu9jp9jz]] 他嫌廉颇坚守不战，受范雎传言影响任赵括，尽管蔺相如和赵母警告；赵括渡丹水进攻被白起断粮合围，饥饿四十六日后阵亡、降卒遭杀，只余二百四十名少年返报。这既有君主误判，也有白起战术与粮援失效。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-1-loxyybm3njrtnudydafwzu9jp9jz]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-2-lvqctrazn8ddkcnp-lhspzzmzmns]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-3-ljwz8ebk0iyfleivkefvvoonrqb9]]
- **和议的代价：** 一篇据《战国策》记赵丹亲赴秦求和曾被扣，秦释之留赵郝议六城割让；不是所有史源一致的出行事实。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-1-lq4wvkd8qx3q7zji6fnnkxi3qcl1]] 虞卿反对依约割地以免秦休养，提出把六城转给齐国、示赵齐结盟以迫秦议和；楼缓则担心失信招诸国夹攻。节目同时怀疑元气未复的齐国是否真能按虞卿设想援赵；赵丹在两种风险间踌躇，秦后来仍进攻。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-2-lutsvwvnwzsezoorhe-vu7yjthyx]] 秦又扣平原君索魏齐首级，赵丹围其宅寻找魏齐以救亲属；虞卿弃相随魏齐逃，魏齐自尽后平原君归赵，但王陵仍攻赵。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-3-lsrmq1-rjqnsja8g07bhaqvhxhpi]]
- **围城求援与恢复：** 邯郸围困时赵丹派平原君赴楚，毛遂迫楚考烈王许合纵；楚军受魏地通道限制、晋鄙按兵邺城，联络不等于援兵到达。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-1-lvqbmaabsvbdz2zibgmseidx5phe]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-2-ltlobdujy-hdipimfdupvwod2ech]] 新垣衍欲尊秦王为西帝求撤兵，鲁仲连反对地位屈服；信陵君窃符领魏军解围、郑安平降赵后，赵丹用贵客之礼待信陵君并考虑五城及汤沐邑，信陵君拒过重封赏。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-2-ltlobdujy-hdipimfdupvwod2ech]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-3-lj-cr4vg4e1qdfjs6yez85-3zuiv]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-2-jiemi-xinlingjun-ping-shenme-shi-si-gongzi-zhi-shou-lodqntthbil9o8ezppt66lywzd-f]] 荀子与临武君在其前辩兵与民心，是朝廷讨论的背景而非赵丹亲撰兵法；燕在秦昭襄王死后试攻赵，他任廉颇获鄗代之胜。[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-3-xunzi-bingfa-li-cangzhe-renxing-de-mimi-lgfcp4hkruyhhrjguqhjbhllvwp43]] [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-2-liuguo-jingran-tongshi-wei-ta-diaosang-ln6-5v-b-ybqmf5ucepusdo3lf68]]

## Qualifications
- 长平人数和梦兆均来自节目对古书的转述；“贪上党”是主播的责任评价，不能消去赵豹、冯亭、赵括及秦军行动。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-93-zai-mengli-cangzhe-toutian-gaiming-2-lrlulw0b6v0fb2xpr2i5ifem1h4n]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-3-ljwz8ebk0iyfleivkefvvoonrqb9]]
- 《战国策》赵丹赴秦、六城谈判与《通鉴》线索应标明各自文本层，不把外交提案当已有效的和平。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-1-lq4wvkd8qx3q7zji6fnnkxi3qcl1]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-2-lutsvwvnwzsezoorhe-vu7yjthyx]]

- 魏齐获庇护是[[AristocraticPoliticalAsylum]]遭范雎[[PersonalRevengeThroughStatePower]]施压；[[HouYing]]属信陵君后来救赵的环节。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-3-lsrmq1-rjqnsja8g07bhaqvhxhpi]] 幼年摄政可与[[QiWangJian]]、[[JunWangHou]]的同期格局比较，但不是同一政令。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-91-chulong-ruhe-qiaokai-shougua-zhaotaihou-de-men-2-liy5yulg2ysop-jwfigvdkub1pk7]]
- 长平的[[CounterintelligenceRumorWedge]]促使换将，[[BattlefieldEncirclementLogistics]]导致赵军断粮；齐援未至可与[[ShieldStateRescueFailure]]并读。降卒处置的[[SurrenderedPrisonerSecurityDilemma]]、[[WarCostTempoDiscipline]]属于秦军后续选择，非赵丹命令。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-1-loxyybm3njrtnudydafwzu9jp9jz]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-2-lvqctrazn8ddkcnp-lhspzzmzmns]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-3-ljwz8ebk0iyfleivkefvvoonrqb9]]
- 《[[Zhanguoce]]》赴秦说与[[StatusAnxietyPersuasion]]、[[StrategicConcessionDiplomacy]]仅支持战后周旋，六城[[LandForPeaceExhaustionTrap]]未保证和平。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-1-lq4wvkd8qx3q7zji6fnnkxi3qcl1]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-2-lutsvwvnwzsezoorhe-vu7yjthyx]] 对信陵君的[[PowerEtiquetteReading]]属于封赏意向而非尽数交割。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-2-jiemi-xinlingjun-ping-shenme-shi-si-gongzi-zhi-shou-lodqntthbil9o8ezppt66lywzd-f]]
- 楚援受魏境[[CoalitionTransitDependency]]限制；鲁仲连用[[CivilizationBarbarismFrame]]、[[HistoricalPrecedentWeaponization]]和[[InterestReframingPersuasion]]反尊秦，不等于赵丹制定此术。[[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-2-ltlobdujy-hdipimfdupvwod2ech]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-3-lj-cr4vg4e1qdfjs6yez85-3zuiv]] 荀子辩论的[[XunzianMilitaryLegitimacy]]、[[WarringStatesMilitaryTransformation]]是论说非国策实施。[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-99-3-xunzi-bingfa-li-cangzhe-renxing-de-mimi-lgfcp4hkruyhhrjguqhjbhllvwp43]] 燕国趁灾的[[PostDisasterPredationMisread]]受廉颇胜绩反驳。[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-100-2-liuguo-jingran-tongshi-wei-ta-diaosang-ln6-5v-b-ybqmf5ucepusdo3lf68]]

## What Changed
- 把接受上党、战中换将、战后求存按决策阶段拆开，而非“昏君导致灭国”的单因果。
- 保留战后拒绝称帝方案、外援与廉颇抗燕的恢复面向。
