# 荆轲 / Jing Ke

荆轲 / Jing Ke enters the wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-2-yanguo-cisha-qinshihuang-weihe-hui-xuan-jingke-lsewjrmgup1yanf8ipn-i0ykqwjx]] as the person [[TianGuang|田光]] recommends to [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]] for the anti-Qin assassination plan. The episode stresses that Jing Ke is not introduced as the obvious best professional swordsman. He is a [[WeyState|卫国]]-origin wandering shi, reader, and侠士 whose mixed social identity makes him unusual and less predictable.

The source spends time on apparent contradictions in his image. Jing Ke seeks visibility around sword and chess disputes but withdraws when challenged by Ge Nie and Lu Goujian; later, in [[YanState|燕国]], he drinks in the market with [[GaoJianli|高渐离]] and a dog butcher, laughing, crying, singing, and acting "as if nobody else were there." Tian Guang nevertheless reads him as deep, bookish, and connected to worthy elders rather than as a mere market eccentric.

Jing Ke's selection therefore sits at the intersection of [[MoralizedShiIdentity|moralized shi identity]], [[PoliticalAssassinationEthics|political assassination ethics]], and [[HonorBindingBySelfSacrifice|honor binding by self-sacrifice]]. He accepts Tian Guang's referral without bargaining in the episode's account, but that acceptance comes after a friend has recommended him into a death mission and then killed himself to prove secrecy and moral seriousness.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-3-jingke-ciqin-weihe-jiujiu-bu-xingdong-zai-deng-shui-liihe-kgwigu3bggxmyofgvotged]] turns Jing Ke from selected assassin into mission planner. He tells [[YanTaiziDan|燕太子丹]] that he cannot approach [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] without [[FanWuji|樊无忌]]'s head and the [[DuKangMap|督亢地图]], then privately secures Fan Wuji's suicide. The episode also makes his delay operational rather than simple hesitation: even after [[XuFuren|徐夫人]]'s dagger and [[QinWuyang|秦武阳]] are ready, Jing Ke waits for a distant friend he appears to trust more.

The same episode gives Jing Ke's departure a failure signal. Taizi Dan's suspicion angers him into leaving before that friend arrives, so the mission begins through [[MistrustTriggeredMissionLaunch|mistrust-triggered mission launch]] rather than fully trusted readiness. The [[YiShuiFarewell|易水送别]] then fixes him in memory as a figure of tragic resolve: he leaves in mourning colors and song, without turning back.
