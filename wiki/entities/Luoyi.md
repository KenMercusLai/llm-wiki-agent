# 洛邑 / Luoyi

## Overview
本页按主题区分有出处的事件、解释及其局限。 洛邑是周京师；会盟不等于朝王，苏秦还乡及秦武王举鼎都利用其王室象征。

## Current Profile
洛邑是周京师；会盟不等于朝王，苏秦还乡及秦武王举鼎都利用其王室象征。 刘邦在洛阳定都及南宫论功是汉初政治场景，并非周代洛邑治理的延续。 楼敬和张良对洛阳地形、腹地及战争后新王朝的判断支持迁都长安。 擒韩信后的洛阳宣示说明迁都后此地仍可用于政治表达。

## Evidence
- **Zhou ritual and diplomatic center:** In 344 BCE the lords “会” at 洛邑, the Zhou 京师, rather than “朝” the king; the host uses this [[AudienceVsMeetingDiplomacy|lexical distinction]] to avoid claiming renewed royal control. His suggestion that [[Fengze|逢泽]] lay west of Luoyi to fit Qin's route is geographic inference. After the six-state [[HezongAlliance|合纵]], [[Shiji|《史记》]] describes [[ZhouXianwang|周显王]] clearing the road for [[SuQin|苏秦]] at his Luoyang homecoming; the procession reversed his family's earlier contempt for poverty ([[QianjuHougongReversal|前倨后恭]]). [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-18-zhongguo-lishishang-di-yi-li-rentoushui-lgqnequk0rxejqgihwdqxqyqnsvq]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-33-lishi-diyi-shuangwen-nanzhujiao-suqin-lvebjyhvzctjgaevwwoddpvg9nr0]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-32-shishang-shouwei-lianheguo-mishuzhang-suqin-lgfkk45yq1c0yblequ-d1t7mol3]]
  After [[GanMao|甘茂]] took [[YiyangWarringStates|宜阳]] and opened the Sanchuan route, a Shiji supplement has [[QinWuwang|秦武王]] visit the Zhou king, see the Nine Dings and die in a reckless lifting contest. In 284 BCE [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]], [[WeiZhaowang|魏昭王]] and [[HanXiwang|韩釐王]] again met at the Zhou capital region amid Qi's collapse; meeting there did not restore Zhou decision power. Later the city was [[ZhouNanwang|周赧王]]'s death setting after failed anti-Qin mobilization and Qin's punishment of [[WesternZhouPolity|西周国]], marking the end of the Zhou-annals frame. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-58-zhaowulingwang-ruhe-dazao-qiangguo-lq9mgpib0whh0w-jkv0kxcafbnnz]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-72-shenme-yang-de-ren-cai-shi-zhongzhen-zhishi-logxegnqexgc7z-lodw6tr6-8f28]] [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-98-lishishang-shouge-laolai-dengchang-le-luxsxepdedsxd-zprezfg3u3bsnu]]
- **Early Han temporary capital:** In a distinct Western Han context, [[LiuBang|刘邦]] initially set his capital at Luoyang and at the South Palace attributed victory over [[XiangYu|项羽]] to employing [[ZhangLiang|张良]], [[XiaoHe|萧何]] and [[HanXin|韩信]] according to their specialties ([[SpecialistDelegationLeadership|delegated leadership]]). This was a founding-regime political stage, not continuity of Zhou royal administration. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-169-pandian-liubang-dengjiqian-buweirenzhi-de-gushi-2-lqq8qhramfzii5o5icesd-uagwkc]]
- **Relocation rationale:** [[LouJing|楼敬]] argued that Zhou's Luoyang had worked under accumulated moral authority and settled ritual order, unlike Liu Bang's newly war-won empire; Zhang Liang added that limited hinterland and exposure on several sides made Luoyang less defensible. Liu Bang therefore moved to [[ChangAnHanCapital|长安]] rather than treating a prestigious center as sufficient strategic protection. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-170-liubang-weisha-cong-luoyang-banjia-dao-changan-2-lpqtexvxjrbrpjgbw5hrgmhl9apk]]
- **Subsequent political stage:** Despite that move, Liu Bang returned to Luoyang after arresting Han Xin through the [[Yunmengze|云梦泽]] / [[ChenCountyChuHan|陈县]] summons plan. He announced the arrest on a charge of unauthorized military action, spared Han Xin and reduced him to [[Huaiyin|淮阴]] marquis: the city remained useful for political declaration without remaining the capital. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-172-liubang-yangyou-yunmengze-zhiqin-hanxin-lrpkye8y6ibqdqs9pz7artk-6chv]]

## Qualifications
Luoyi and later Luoyang refer to the region in distinct dynastic contexts; “会” is not “朝.” Fengze geography and the capital-choice reasoning are attributed interpretations.

## What Changed
- 汉初定都及迁长安的战略辩论，补充了周王都礼仪中心的早期图景而不混淆两朝地理。

## Relationships
- [[GuanzhongRegion]] - 楼敬与张良比较洛阳的暴露地势和关中防御条件，刘邦因此由洛阳迁都关中长安。
- [[QinState]] - 秦武王攻占宜阳后进入洛阳见周鼎；后期秦对周室的压力也使洛邑成为周赧王末年的舞台。
- [[StrategicCapitalRelocation]] - 洛阳是汉初刘邦短暂定都但最终舍弃的选项，迁长安依据新朝的防御与腹地考量。
- [[WarringStatesPeriod]] - 诸侯在周京师会盟而不必朝王，洛邑的礼仪地位与战国实际权力分离。
- [[WesternHanDynasty]] - 刘邦在洛阳南宫论用人并短暂定都，迁长安后仍在此宣布韩信被捕；此非周廷行政的延续。
- [[ZhaitaiGaozhu]] - 周赧王晚年借债反秦失败的典故，与他在洛邑的周室终局相连；城市并非借债策略的发起者。
- [[ZhouNanwang]] - 洛邑既是诸侯284 BCE会于周京师的地点，也成为周赧王反秦失败后的去世地。
- [[ZhouRoyalHouse]] - 洛邑为周京师，苏秦返乡受周显王礼遇、秦武王见九鼎均借助王室象征，不能视为周重新统率列国。
