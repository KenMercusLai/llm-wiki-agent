## Overview

赵飞燕 / Zhao Feiyan enters this wiki through the paired Hanji 573-574 backfill as the former empress and later empress dowager whose alleged palace-case exposure becomes politically dangerous for [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-573-zhaofeiyan-jiemei-shi-hongyanhuoshui-haishi-tizuigaoyang-lkacfndvi8t1opep67xmquyzr_ww|Hanji 573]] challenges the simple "red-beauty disaster" explanation for [[HanChengdi|汉成帝]]'s heir failure, while [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-574-gudai-de-manfen-zuowen-shi-shenme-yangzi-lotqprwr3rk5lke0rjn94riljuvh|Hanji 574]] shows how [[GengYuWesternHan|耿育]]'s memorial lets Aidi stop further investigation without appearing to suppress truth for private gratitude.

## Current Profile

Hanji 573 presents Zhao Feiyan and [[ZhaoHedeWesternHan|赵合德]] as accused favorites whose guilt story deserves skepticism rather than automatic acceptance. The episode stresses that Chengdi had children who died young before the Zhao sisters explain the pattern, that the investigation is testimony-heavy, and that Wang Zhengjun and the Wang family had strong reasons to monitor heir production. Zhao Feiyan is therefore treated as a plausible target of [[GenderedConsortScapegoating|红颜祸水式替罪叙事]], not as a cleared innocent or settled culprit.

Hanji 574 presents Zhao Feiyan as a politically sensitive beneficiary-protector figure for Aidi. When Aidi was selected as crown prince, Zhao Feiyan is said to have helped him; [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]] was grateful to her and maintained a friendly relationship with her. That background makes a continued prosecution uncomfortable for Aidi, even though stopping the case risks angering [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] and the Wang family.

Geng Yu's memorial changes the frame around Zhao Feiyan's case. Instead of treating the issue as palace jealousy and crimes around a favored woman, the memorial argues that exposing the matter would injure Chengdi's reputation and spread a humiliating story across the realm and later generations.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han palace figure whose case is source-scoped here through Aidi's early reign.
- Paired with Zhao Hede as an accused favorite in Chengdi's heir-loss narrative.
- Possible object of gendered scapegoating rather than a settled single-cause explanation for Chengdi's no-heir outcome.
- Helped Aidi's earlier designation as crown prince according to the episode.
- Maintained a gratitude-linked relationship with Fu Taihou.
- Becomes the center of a politically costly investigation that Aidi wants to close.

## Qualifications

This page does not reconstruct Zhao Feiyan's full life, earlier rise, Chengdi-era palace politics, or legal case record. Hanji 573 challenges the evidentiary and narrative simplicity of the accusation but does not prove innocence. Hanji 574 supplies the Aidi-era closure frame and the memorial's political reading of the case.
