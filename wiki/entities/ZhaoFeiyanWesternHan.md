## Overview

赵飞燕 / Zhao Feiyan enters this wiki through [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-574-gudai-de-manfen-zuowen-shi-shenme-yangzi-lotqprwr3rk5lke0rjn94riljuvh|Hanji 574]] as the former empress and later empress dowager whose alleged palace-case exposure becomes politically dangerous for [[HanAidi|汉哀帝]]. The episode focuses less on her biography than on how [[GengYuWesternHan|耿育]]'s memorial helps Aidi stop further investigation without appearing to suppress truth for private gratitude.

## Current Profile

The source presents Zhao Feiyan as a politically sensitive beneficiary-protector figure for Aidi. When Aidi was selected as crown prince, Zhao Feiyan is said to have helped him; [[FuTaihouWesternHan|傅太后]] was grateful to her and maintained a friendly relationship with her. That background makes a continued prosecution uncomfortable for Aidi, even though stopping the case risks angering [[WangZhengjun|王政君]] and the Wang family.

Geng Yu's memorial changes the frame around Zhao Feiyan's case. Instead of treating the issue as palace jealousy and crimes around a favored woman, the memorial argues that exposing the matter would injure Chengdi's reputation and spread a humiliating story across the realm and later generations.

## Key Characteristics

- Western Han palace figure whose case is source-scoped here through Aidi's early reign.
- Helped Aidi's earlier designation as crown prince according to the episode.
- Maintained a gratitude-linked relationship with Fu Taihou.
- Becomes the center of a politically costly investigation that Aidi wants to close.
- Her case shows how palace scandal can threaten dynastic reputation, filial duty, and dowager-faction relations at once.
