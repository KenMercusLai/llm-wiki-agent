## Definition
Channel focus experiments are bounded tests of distribution and operating models before a founder commits scarce cash, staff, inventory, or attention.

## Current Synthesis
The Advice Line cases distinguish venue partnerships, wholesale, local stores, social media, trusted intermediaries, and professional endorsements by the buying behavior each can reveal. Advisors recommend staged commitments rather than treating a channel as a permanent identity; the reported caller updates are not controlled evidence that any suggested channel prevailed.

## Key Claims
- Reversible tests should precede a costly channel or inventory-model switch.
- A channel must fit the buyer's need and trust surface, not just maximize impressions.
- Observed purchases, reorders, referrals, customer calls, and attribution delays matter more than founder assumptions or platform dashboards alone.
- A strong product or narrow customer segment can determine which channel deserves the first experiment.
- Channel scale must be evaluated against cash, production, delegation, founder energy, and family-business ambition.

## Evidence
- Staging the commitment: [[advice-line-with-tim-ferriss-august-2025-84f10963-70ff-444d-87df-cfedab442943]] reports [[TimFerriss]] and [[GuyRaz]] advising [[Gob]] to learn and build reserves through venues, festivals or partnerships before costly DTC sleep acquisition; [[EBCo]] to trial wholesale for 6 to 12 months with a dedicated person, samples and trade shows while retaining stores after its Taylor Swift publicity; and [[KBecker]] to try limited made-to-order drops before abandoning inventory. Guy contrasts “made to order” with a potentially less reassuring “pre-order.”
- Category-specific trust: [[advice-line-with-chris-riccobono-of-untuckit-c087247f-1f85-4c15-b4aa-4ffea0368c4c]] has [[ChrisRiccobono]] and Guy steer [[AeroShorts]] toward a volleyball/college tribe rather than “shorts for everyone,” [[SnugSafety]] toward radio, local media, doctors and churches for senior reassurance, and [[HockeyNinja]] toward credible athlete use and equipment managers for safety gear. [[GreatnessWins]] treats golf-shop wholesale as both sales and discovery; these channels cannot simply be exchanged.
- Learning from lagged behavior: [[advice-line-with-curt-richardson-of-otterbox-e94bf774-de11-4434-843b-a551309a6773]] records [[CurtRichardson]] urging [[Everloop]] and [[VinceGiudice]] to test a handful of TikTok, Meta, Pinterest, retail, registry and show channels before scaling spend. Baby registries create a three-to-six-month discovery-to-purchase lag; calls and attribution checks matter. Guy proposes testing whether the 20% cash-back buyback resonates more than the recycled-materials message, not assuming it does.
- Product and sales focus: [[advice-line-with-carlton-calvin-of-razor-c0de1a84-3d35-45f3-921c-6144693ad86e]] advises [[EulogyGame|Eulogy]] and [[SeanBarassa]] to show game-night, convention and small-store demand before licensing (the source reports just under 1,000 copies after its 2024 Kickstarter); [[HoneyBespokeStationery]] and [[ShireenTippett]] can compare school-connected stores, markets, representatives and founder-led design content with Etsy dependence. For [[LittleWaterDistillery]], focusing [[48BlocksEspressoMartini]]'s espresso/chocolate products and delegating sales may matter more than spreading effort across SKUs.
- Capacity and ambition: [[advice-line-with-ronnen-harary-of-spin-master-paw-patrol-3894a69a-a4de-4bd8-b5f5-d3bc195994d5]] presents [[IslandBeeCompany]] and [[FelixCollin]] choosing between corporate gifting, weddings, hotels and local relationships or retail, distributors, trade shows and consumer storytelling for [[Hive5]]. [[GuyRaz]] warns viral demand might overwhelm family production, while [[RonnenHarary]] warns fear itself should not foreclose a larger consumer-products ambition.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
These are advisor recommendations and self-reported cases, not comparative channel trials. The EB wholesale interval and Everloop registry lag are case-specific; the Island Bee advisors disagree over how much growth risk to take. A test can recommend retrenchment, delegation, or more production capacity rather than a new advertising channel.
