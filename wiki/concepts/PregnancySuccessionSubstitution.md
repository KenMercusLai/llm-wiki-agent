# Pregnancy Succession Substitution / 孕身继承替换

Pregnancy succession substitution / 孕身继承替换 is the mechanism [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-109-1-lishishang-zui-zhuming-de-huangdi-bei-lv-le-liawtywj4c9n0zpii-rxl7cguzyy]] draws from [[Chunshenjun|黄歇 / 春申君]], [[LiYuanWarringStates|李源]], [[LiYuanSister|李源妹妹]], and [[MiWan|楚考烈王]]. A woman is already pregnant by one man, then is introduced into a childless ruler's palace so that the child can be recognized as the ruler's heir.

The mechanism depends on three conditions. First, [[SuccessionNonDesignationRisk|未定继承人风险]] creates enough fear that the ruler's trusted minister sees future danger in another branch succeeding. Second, pregnancy is hidden or timing is plausibly deniable. Third, the palace's legal recognition of mother and child matters more politically than biological fact once the child is accepted as crown prince.

This is related to [[MaternalStatusSuccessionLeverage|子以母贵式继承杠杆]] but not identical. Maternal-status leverage elevates an already known son by changing his maternal affiliation or adoption status. Pregnancy substitution tries to create an heir through concealed paternity and later recognition, making legitimacy depend on secrecy as well as maternal placement.

The concept also explains why the episode compares the Chu scandal with the [[QinShiHuang|嬴政 / 秦始皇]] paternity rumor. The host uses the Chu case not to prove the Qin rumor, but to show why similar scandal stories require [[HistoricalDetectiveReasoning|evidence testing]] before they are accepted as biography.

## Key Claims
- A childless ruler's court can become vulnerable to paternity manipulation if no legible successor has been accepted.
- Hidden pregnancy turns intimate access into succession politics.
- Legal recognition and palace status can outrun biological uncertainty once a child is installed as heir.
- The mechanism creates danger for every participant because the secret can later become blackmail, purge motive, or legitimacy crisis.
- A structurally similar scandal motif should not be imported into another lineage story without independent evidence.
