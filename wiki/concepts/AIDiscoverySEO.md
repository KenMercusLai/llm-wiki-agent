# AI Discovery SEO

## Definition
AI discovery SEO concerns how brands, institutions or products become findable, accurately described and potentially cited in AI-mediated answers; it overlaps with but does not replace conventional search and closed app-store discovery.

## Current Synthesis
Owned factual pages, independent mentions and AI-answer audits may improve discoverability, but visibility does not establish recommendation, conversion or causal attribution. Sponsored placements and polluted source pages make the trust boundary important.

## Key Claims
- Answer engines can favor dense, verifiable public facts over generic promotional copy.
- Third-party references and measurement tools can expose what models cite but cannot guarantee sales.
- Discovery tactics vary sharply between open web, college advising, consumer products and app stores.
- Answer integrity becomes harder to defend when paid placement or manipulative content shares the same compact interface.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- The survey, marketing spend and vendor performance are source-dated observations, not independently validated causal lift.
- Being crawled, mentioned or cited differs from recommendation, customer conversion and retention; keyword stuffing and artificial lists can worsen answer quality.

## What Changed
- The page now distinguishes factual owned content, external citation supply, closed-store ASO and paid answer placement.
