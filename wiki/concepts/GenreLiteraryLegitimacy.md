# Genre Literary Legitimacy / 类型文学正名

## Definition
Genre literary legitimacy asks what popular, speculative or stigmatized narrative forms can accomplish before judging their value by a prestige label.

## Current Synthesis
The strongest explicit defenses here concern Korean guaitan and Chinese wuxia. Wilde and Tokarczuk show particular strange-story techniques, while 《金瓶梅》 is a separate reputation/scandal case, not proof that all five works occupied the same genre hierarchy.

## Key Claims
- Grotesque stories can render domestic fear and exploitation without a realist disguise or a reassuring revenge ending.
- Wuxia conventions can organize cultural allusion, history and political conflict as literary craft while remaining entertaining.
- The weird or supernatural can communicate self-recognition, loss and tenderness through concrete narrative devices.
- Reputation built around scandal can obscure social networks and structure even in a canonical work.
- Literary assessment must inspect a work's actual form rather than infer seriousness from genre or prestige alone.

## Evidence
- Direct genre defense: the reading of Bora Chung's 《诅咒兔》 argues for guaitan as literature through 《头》's ignored female fear, the title story's revenge without catharsis, 《伤疤》's dependent village order and 《不受家》's fox, wealth and inherited bodily harm. The hosts resist reducing these to a single capitalism-or-patriarchy slogan. [[117-zuzhoutu-guaitan-wenxue-ye-yao-shangzhuo-chifan-766574341]]
- Wuxia craft: the Jin Yong discussion reads 北冥神功, 凌波微步 and other martial names through classical language; 《越女剑》 reshapes an older swordswoman tradition, while 《天龙八部》 makes Qiao Feng's identity conflict tragic and 《笑傲江湖》 receives a factional-power reading. The hosts contrast the mobility of Song Jianghu settings with the more controlled Ming founding order, as interpretation rather than direct proof of historical conditions. [[112-xianliao-jinyong-diyidan-mingjiao-jingran-jintian-hai-cunzai-754593095]]
- Strange form: the Wilde episode reads 《阿瑟·萨维尔勋爵的罪行》's inverted moral duty, 《W.H.先生的画像》's seductive forged evidence and 《坎特维尔的幽灵》's comic English ghost and practical American family. Its science-fiction legitimacy comparison is brief and does not establish a field-wide verdict. [[10-wang-erde-qiyi-gushiji-yong-zui-hui-sahuang-de-zuichun-qinwen-tianshi-545332195]]
- Everyday weirdness: [[OlgaTokarczuk|Tokarczuk]]'s 《怪诞故事集》 turns the frightening future self in 〈旅客〉, AI household intimacy and shutdown in 〈拜访〉, faulty familiar objects in 〈接缝〉, maternal labor in 〈罐头〉 and chosen transformation in 〈变形中心〉 into questions of aging, dependency and farewell. This is already a prestige-author case, not an excluded writer's recognition story. [[130-tuokaerqiuke-guaidan-gushiji-xunzhao-he-gaobie-803543569]]
- Scandal and structure: [[Banlatte|半拿铁]]'s cleaned-up retelling of 《金瓶梅》 tracks Ximen Qing's business, household, lending and official connections, Wang Po's priced intermediation and Pan Jinlian's constrained position, shifting attention from erotic reputation to urban everyday fiction and social texture. The 《水浒传》 entry route and later [[HongLouMeng|《红楼梦》]] reception are the show's literary framing. [[fanwai-15-banlatte-jiang-jinpingmei-la-erqie-haishi-luse-de-991508433]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
These are podcast readings, not comparative reception research. Enjoyment alone does not validate every genre work, and being a canonical scandal case or a Nobel author is unlike a genre-specific exclusion struggle. Speculative literary events and historical settings must not be used as direct empirical evidence.

## What Changed
- Distinguished explicit guaitan/wuxia defense from Wilde's short comparison, Tokarczuk's prestige-author example and 《金瓶梅》's reception problem.
