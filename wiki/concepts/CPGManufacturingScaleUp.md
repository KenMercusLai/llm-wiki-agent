# CPG Manufacturing Scale-Up

## Definition
CPG manufacturing scale-up is the move from founder-made batches to repeatable, safe, economically viable output and packaging sufficient to serve recurring retail or consumer demand.

## Current Synthesis
A co-manufacturer, owned plant and qualified supplier solve different bottlenecks; each requires capital, process knowledge and quality control. Infant formula has a much stricter continuity and compliance constraint than snacks. A durable cooler offers a useful but explicitly non-CPG supplier-dependency analogy.

## Key Claims
- Home and shared-kitchen output can reveal demand while failing volume, consistency and founder-labor tests.
- Contract manufacture addresses only the specific steps a partner can perform; finishing and packaging can remain binding constraints.
- Owning capacity may protect process, allergens and retailer formats but transfers financing and safety risk onto the company.
- Regulatory compliance and long replenishment lead times make formula growth a continuity problem for existing families.
- Supplier concentration can strand demand even for well-designed goods; redundant capacity and documented molds reduce that exposure.

## Evidence
- **From batches to process:** [[KrishnaKaliannan]] made [[CatalinaCrunch]] from apartment-oven and overnight commercial-kitchen runs before industrial cereal learning; [[JustinGold]] built [[JustinsNutButter]] with rented kitchens and grinders before national customers demanded audited output. Early sales alone did not establish repeatable production. [[catalina-crunch-krishna-kaliannan-from-homemade-keto-cocoa-puffs-to-breakfast-aisle-breakthrough-5aa86015-e7c2-448e-a84e-d1b34d2fcac2]] [[justins-nut-butter-justin-gold-he-was-waiting-tables-then-he-reinvented-peanut-butter-b456c4b4-0243-4e46-9460-d0de7cf5d98b]]
- **Partner gaps:** Catalina's cereal co-manufacturer could make squares but not reliably coat them or fill resealable stand-up pouches. Krishna built an Indianapolis finishing operation after packaging partners failed; formulation, texture and equipment co-evolved, while low-ticket bags could cost roughly their selling price to ship. [[catalina-crunch-krishna-kaliannan-from-homemade-keto-cocoa-puffs-to-breakfast-aisle-breakthrough-5aa86015-e7c2-448e-a84e-d1b34d2fcac2]]
- **Owned process and format:** [[NimaFotovat]] and [[SalmaFotovat]] brought [[TasteOfNature]] and co-packing knowledge to their new [[RiversideNaturalFoods]]/[[MadeGood]] venture, financing a 20,000-square-foot allergen-safe factory with family funds, secured borrowing and an episode-reported $2m pre-revenue loan. [[Costco]] then required different pouches, variety packs and cases; a 2024 brush-bristle contamination prompted a reported 2.5m-case recall, so ownership was no guarantee against failures. [[madegood-salma-and-nima-fotovat-lost-their-first-business-they-grew-their-next-one-into-a-snack-giant-cf5715bf-e9e0-45e0-a8a9-1256a7a4ed0d]]
- **Regulated continuity:** [[LauraModi]]'s [[Bobbie]] faced an initial FDA-related recall—a compliance setback, not an approval. Dependence on [[Perrigo]], five-to-six-month formula lead times and the need to protect subscribers led it to pause acquisition and later acquire [[NaturesOne]] capacity; safety and supply must be evaluated together. [[bobbie-laura-modi-how-a-baby-formula-startup-took-market-share-from-two-industry-giants-4488842b-74d3-40ed-8776-5831c5934e69]]
- **Durables analogy:** In 2008 the death of [[YETI]] partner [[IvanBrown]] exposed single-supplier dependence in the [[RotomoldedCoolerCategory|rotomolded cooler]] category. Digitizing [[YETITundra]] designs and adding U.S. rotomolders took an episode-reported eighteen months; coolers are not packaged consumables. [[yeti-ron-and-ryan-seiders-how-two-brothers-turned-a-400-cooler-into-a-2-billion-brand-d7f8e0e8-51a5-4444-8c1d-f5c43c353285]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- Vertical integration is conditional, not a default remedy: it adds working-capital, staffing and food-safety exposure. MadeGood's recall and Bobbie's early recall complicate success narratives. The YETI source title says Ron, while its body identifies cofounder Roy Seiders; do not resolve that metadata inconsistency by inventing a person.

## What Changed
- Organized the cases by repeatability, partner capabilities, owned-process tradeoffs, regulated continuity and supplier concentration.
