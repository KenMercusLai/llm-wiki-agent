# CPG Distribution

## Definition
Consumer packaged goods distribution is the combined problem of getting a physical product into a relevant channel, making it understandable at the point of trial, replenishing it economically and earning repeat orders.

## Current Synthesis
A store door is not sell-through. Channel fit depends on education, shelf and pack design, retailer needs, shipping or cold-chain economics, working capital and category ownership. Advice Line suggestions remain proposals; founder-retrospective numbers remain reported rather than independently audited.

## Key Claims
- Founder-led local stocking and demonstrations can break a retailer–distributor catch-22, but reorder velocity determines whether placement lasts.
- The right entry channel teaches an unfamiliar product at the moment of use and generates observable customer evidence before broad rollout.
- Buyer requirements, pack format, unit economics and trusted intermediaries differ across natural grocery, low-price beauty, professional referral, hospitality and frozen retail.
- Direct shipping, cold-chain and seasonal inventory costs can determine whether a brand must shift channel even when online demand exists.
- Expansion should test repeat orders and financing/control risks rather than treating any new shelf, private label or publicity as category proof.
- Displays, sampling and founder or representative education make a product credible on shelf, but must translate into profitable replenishment.

## Evidence
- **Placement versus movement:** [[JustinGold]] built [[JustinsNutButter]] and moved from [[GreatHarvestBread]] to local [[WholeFoodsMarket]] while [[UNFI]] wanted store demand and Whole Foods wanted UNFI supply; he delivered and demoed himself. Around 2006, about 25 stores and $150,000 sales still exposed slow jar repurchase. Moving squeeze packs from the energy-bar aisle to the nut-butter shelf turned them into trials; [[Starbucks]] later required audited manufacturing. [[justins-nut-butter-justin-gold-he-was-waiting-tables-then-he-reinvented-peanut-butter-b456c4b4-0243-4e46-9460-d0de7cf5d98b]]
- **Local education:** [[NimaFotovat]] and [[SalmaFotovat]] built [[MadeGood]] from an allergen-safe school snack into a broader family product, beginning with [[Loblaw]], testing U.S. natural grocery through Whole Foods and building Toronto sell-through with demos and merchandising before [[Costco]] required pouches, value packs and assortment changes. [[RedTruckOrchards]] was advised to sample cherry vinegar with clear uses, while [[Petaluma]] was advised not to diffuse its DTC subscription effort into retail too early. These are [[DistributionLedProductBuilding|channel-driven product and pack revisions]], not proof every shelf listing creates demand. [[madegood-salma-and-nima-fotovat-lost-their-first-business-they-grew-their-next-one-into-a-snack-giant-cf5715bf-e9e0-45e0-a8a9-1256a7a4ed0d]] [[advice-line-with-jeffrey-hollender-of-seventh-generation-f8ce2410-1176-47d9-be44-03f9fa945714]]
- **Buyer and channel fit:** [[ELFCosmetics]] overcame dollar-store rejection with PR/e-commerce, [[HEB]] spinner racks and [[Target]] end caps by showing low-price incremental sales; its [[LowPriceBrandPerception|one-dollar value positioning]] needed merchandising rather than an assumption that cheap equals unwanted, and faster DTC receipts differed from slower retailer payment. [[SusanGriffinBlack]] of [[EOProducts]] advised [[Yobi]] to use physician-founder credibility with Chicago dermatologists and pediatricians; [[CultureWineCompany]] reported 80% reorders in California and focused sommelier/restaurant education, while [[CaneDogCoffee]]'s hotel-heavy business faced Barbados travel/logistics limits. [[e-l-f-cosmetics-joey-shamah-the-dollar-store-formula-that-built-a-cosmetics-giant-bbc71e65-9e47-41cb-8dd4-c61fcfc2572e]] [[advice-line-with-susan-griffin-black-of-eo-products-58096820-613c-40ad-9743-f1d642447c91]]
- **Proof versus ownership:** [[SprinkleBites]] reported an early [[ThriveMarket]] reorder within 30 days and 88-pack movement for thirteen weeks, yet was cautioned that retailer private label could teach buyers to prefer a cheap copy; [[Plantamica]] was urged to test local shelves, reorder and unit economics before fundraising. These observations are limited [[CustomerPull|customer-pull]] signals, not established [[ProductLedWillingnessToPay|durable willingness to pay]]. [[UbeCo]] needed repeatable customer language before PR; [[JesseAndBens]] could show fry quality through hot air-fryer sampling, while [[JajuPierogi]] weighed bank/SBA finance against dilution. [[advice-line-with-shazi-visram-of-happy-family-organics-c354a554-e764-44c6-89f9-e2192e3e5a86]] [[advice-line-with-jeni-britton-of-jenis-splendid-ice-creams-2025-6dadc048-8cec-44ec-a3cf-ec314a00e41e]]
- **Transport and production:** [[KrishnaKaliannan]]'s [[CatalinaCrunch]] coating and resealable pouch required its own finishing capacity; the founder's [[DietaryConstraintProductInsight|low-sugar/keto product insight]] still needed a Whole Foods buyer's retail positioning, and a cheap cereal bag could cost nearly its price to ship. [[JohnCoogan]] reports [[Soylent]] bottles costing about $10 per box to ship versus roughly $1 for lighter [[Lucy]] nicotine gum; the [[FoodAndDrugAdministration|FDA]] pathway was slower for Lucy and, in his view, created a [[RegulatedConsumerProductMoat|regulatory entry barrier]]—not proof that lower shipping cost made regulation easy. [[ZhongXuegao]]'s frozen logistics impose temperature and order-value constraints that [[FreshMadeIceCreamRetail|store-made ice cream]] at [[YerenXiansheng]] approaches differently. [[catalina-crunch-krishna-kaliannan-from-homemade-keto-cocoa-puffs-to-breakfast-aisle-breakthrough-5aa86015-e7c2-448e-a84e-d1b34d2fcac2]] [[tsr-s5-johncoogan-audiofile-1-tsr-s5-johncoogan-audiofile-1]] [[132-xuegao-jianghu-fengtouquan-1-132-1]]
- **Experience and replenishment:** [[ChristyClement]]'s [[VashonIslandCoffeeDust]] sells through markets, Etsy, stores and wholesale, but gift recipients must adopt a repeatable beverage ritual, not merely receive a package. [[LaurenMenard]]'s [[Gob]] was advised to learn in venues and vending where its use moment is felt. [[GildedCoachTeas]] was advised to reactivate customers before buying reach, and baby-gear [[Everloop]]'s registry purchases produce a three-to-six-month attribution delay; those adjacent products do not prove food economics. [[advice-line-with-christina-tosi-of-milk-bar-fd7f2406-fda8-4c1d-936c-d20210215f96]] [[advice-line-with-tim-ferriss-august-2025-84f10963-70ff-444d-87df-cfedab442943]] [[advice-line-with-curt-richardson-of-otterbox-e94bf774-de11-4434-843b-a551309a6773]]
- **Physical trust and delegation:** [[TomRinks]]'s [[SunBum]] used surf shops, hotels, [[RichardBranson]]'s Necker Island account, representative [[MichaelLaSara]] and full sticker/display installations; that [[VisualBrandSystem|visual brand system]] made the shelf and venue itself a credibility surface before larger [[SCJohnson]] capacity. In adjacent footwear, [[BrianSmith]]'s [[UGG]] used [[SubcultureLedMarketing|surfer-led credibility]] before facing winter inventory-finance pressure; [[CarltonCalvin]] of [[RazorUSA]] advises [[HoneyBespokeStationery]] on school-linked stores and [[LittleWaterDistillery]] on [[SKUFocusUnderCashConstraint|focused SKUs]] and reps rather than founder-only selling. These are different categories with different buying rhythms. ## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- The cases cross food, cosmetics, sunscreen, nicotine, footwear and baby gear; their regulation, price and logistics differ. Advice does not establish subsequent outcomes. MadeGood's school-safe manufacturing promise was later tested by a broad recall. Reported reorders and scale figures are source accounts, not a current industry benchmark. DTC and retail are not a universal sequence.

## What Changed
- Collapsed arrival-order examples into trial, channel fit, economics, repeat proof and credibility mechanisms while keeping all sixteen ordered notes.
