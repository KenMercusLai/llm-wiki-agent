## Definition
Digital preservation is continuing stewardship that keeps records discoverable, intact, readable and responsibly usable across changes in media, software, ownership and access. Merely putting bytes on a disk or cloud service is not enough.

## Current Synthesis
[[LindaTodich]] of [[DigitalBedrock]] describes multiple copies, offsite storage, periodic checks, migration and documentation as a long-term obligation. Her [[WIPR]] case shows why technical context matters: 1950s–1980s radio recordings were digitized around 2007–08 onto LTO3 tapes, but the write method was forgotten; the team identified NT Backup and an old Windows environment before recovering material for the [[AmericanArchiveOfPublicBroadcasting]]. Cloud services hide rather than eliminate the physical medium and may scatter a family's records across accounts. The 2026 storage-supply episode adds Todich's concern that AI-driven hardware demand could raise archive costs and dependence on hyperscalers.

This maintenance also faces [[MemoryChipShortage]] and [[AIHardwareSupplyChainPressure]] in the dated account, while [[AnalogMediaPreservation]] and [[GamePreservation]] have distinct format and rights barriers. Preservation also has access and consent boundaries. Some publishers reportedly blocked [[WaybackMachine]] crawlers for fear that AI firms might use archives to obtain copyrighted news, even though the episode cites no direct instance of that route; blocking weakens records of changed or deleted public pages. [[TomasHoloneck]] distinguishes keeping personal traces from making [[AIGriefBots]] that speak for a deceased person: consent, dignity, family disagreement, deletion and unequal access all matter.

[[InternetArchive]]'s web-capture mission faces an [[AIProxyScrapingRisk]] allegation with no demonstrated example in the note; refused crawls increase [[InternetHistoryFragility]]. [[PhysicalVideoMediaRevival]] is another format-and-custody comparison, not evidence that all analog materials solve digital decay.

## Key Claims
- Multiple copies, checks, migration and documentation keep files findable and intact beyond a single storage event.
- Readability depends on compatible hardware, operating systems, backup formats and project knowledge as well as retained bits.
- Physical media scarcity and cloud account sprawl can constrain institutional archives and family inheritance alike.
- Public web archiving depends on publisher access and trust, not only crawler and disk capacity.
- Preserved personal data should not automatically become an interactive post-mortem simulation.

## Evidence
- Copies and checks: [[tech-20260303-0303-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260303-0303-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports Todich’s offsite copies, integrity checks and recurring stewardship rather than “store and ignore.”
- Recovery environment: [[tech-20260325-0325-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260325-0325-mp-tech-pod-128]] recounts WIPR’s LTO3 tapes, NT Backup identification and recovery on legacy Windows equipment instead of a simple file copy.
- Media and account dependency: [[tech-20260303-0303-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260303-0303-mp-tech-pod-128]] describes hard-drive procurement pressure on smaller archives and the cloud-account inheritance problem for families; [[tech-20260325-0325-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260325-0325-mp-tech-pod-128]] demonstrates how readable bytes still depend on software, drives and cables.
- Public access bargain: [[tech-20260421-0421-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260421-0421-mp-tech-pod-128]] relays [[AndrewDeck]]’s account of preemptive publisher blocks and [[MarkGraham]]’s warning about lost journalistic history without proving a Wayback proxy-scraping route.
- Post-mortem consent: [[tech-20260318-0318-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260318-0318-mp-tech-pod-128]] contrasts saved messages and videos with simulated personas and names [[GoogleInactiveAccountManager]] as a deletion-planning example.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The shortage and vendor statements are dated source reports, not a permanent capacity forecast. Preservation of analog media and games has overlapping stewardship needs but differing rights and format barriers. Publishers' copyright concern is genuine even if the alleged archive-to-AI scraping route is not shown; state-run archiving also raises record-control concerns. Interactive memorial tools may help consenting families or survivor testimony, but their effects and adoption are not quantified in these notes.

## What Changed
- Combined storage, technical readability and custody into one maintenance model rather than treating cloud storage as permanence.
- Added web access and post-mortem consent as distinct limits on “preserve everything.”
