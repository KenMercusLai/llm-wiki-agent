# Bureaucratic Risk Avoidance

## Definition
Bureaucratic risk avoidance is the preference for defensible procedure and low visible blame over a service or safety outcome when authority, expertise and accountability are split.

## Current Synthesis
The mechanism is not simple incompetence. In Indian digital procurement the purchaser may lack product authority; in *Yes Minister* the official is highly skilled at controlling it. Fictional rule escalation, nuclear denial and surgical case selection expose different costs of the same incentive, not evidence of equal prevalence.

## Key Claims
- A technically capable supplier cannot rescue a public service if the buyer cannot specify, judge or authorize a user-centred result.
- Skilled administrators can protect departmental continuity by controlling information, options and the minister's time.
- A rule designed for safety can become an obstacle when no official owns the exceptional decision.
- Institutional claims of safety can make anomalies harder to acknowledge and responsibility harder to allocate after harm.
- In high-risk medicine, restrictions and crude outcome measures can bias decisions away from difficult patients, but clinical safeguards still matter.

## Evidence
- **Buyer authority:** [[LeoMirani]] describes India's visa site reproducing paper-process logic despite the [[NationalInformaticsCentre]]'s technical ability; consultancy procurement without expert ownership does not resolve this. [[Aadhaar]] and [[UPI]] are counterexamples where decision authority and expertise were better aligned. [[strait-and-narrowing-the-iran-deal-crumbles-6a57608bf821a8210984481f]]
- **Process control:** In [[YesMinister|*Yes Minister*]], [[HumphreyAppleby]] contains [[JamesHacker]]'s open-government reform with red boxes, committees, wording and scheduling; this is [[BureaucraticWorkloadControl]], not lack of administrative skill. [[118-shi-dachen-xinla-fengci-yongbuguoshi-768665425]]
- **Accountable exception:** In [[FeimaPaiQiche|*Feima Pai Qiche*]], the 250 bus cannot open its doors before a stop, even as passengers' needs grow; driver, police and officials create coordination and permissions rather than take responsibility for opening them. This is satire about [[RuleIdolatry]], not an observed bus incident. [[23-feima-pai-qiche-anquan-shi-di-yi-wei-de-572699943]]
- **Denial and blame:** The account of [[ChernobylNuclearPowerPlant|Chernobyl]] connects official absolute-safety rhetoric and reluctance to believe a reactor exploded with secrecy and subsequent operator-versus-design blame; procedural and design conditions both matter. [[04-qieernuobeili-de-wuye-woyao-jin-jianyu-le-530594843]]
- **Clinical incentives:** [[StephenWestaby]]'s disputed rescue use of the restricted [[AB180BloodPump]] for Julie and his objection to crude published mortality statistics show distinct budget/device and selection pressures: a surgeon may avoid the sickest patients to protect reported results. [[86-dakai-yike-xin-na-meihao-de-zhang-wo-yijing-daguo-le-702323437]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- India has large successful digital infrastructures; neither all public servants nor all procurement is dysfunctional. *Yes Minister* and the bus are satire. Chernobyl also involved design, operator decisions, materials and production pressures: risk avoidance is not a sole cause, and Dyatlov's defense remains contested. Westaby's account does not justify generally waiving device validation or patient-safety rules.

## What Changed
- Separated buyer authority, skilled process control, exceptional-rule handling, safety denial and clinical measurement instead of treating them as one failure.
