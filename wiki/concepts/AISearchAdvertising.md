# AI Search Advertising

## Definition
AI search advertising sells paid visibility beside or within generated answers, product comparisons and commerce suggestions. It differs from brands seeking unpaid mentions through [[AnswerEngineOptimization]] and from AI used to *make* ad creatives. [[tech-20260304-0304-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260304-0304-mp-tech-pod-128]] [[tech-20251218-1218-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251218-1218-mp-tech-pod-128]]

## Current Synthesis
Answer engines must recruit users and advertisers while preserving answer integrity with fewer visible options than traditional result pages. Publishers' citation traffic, consumer fraud and incumbents' legacy ad revenue complicate the transition; the notes describe possible formats and dated cases, not a settled ad market. [[lanjian-hangtian-wancheng-zhongguo-shouci-ludi-huojian-huishou-yushu-keji-shizhi-chaoguo-3000-yi-1007302506]] [[tech-20260304-0304-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260304-0304-mp-tech-pod-128]] [[tech-20260223-0223-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260223-0223-mp-tech-pod-128]] [[tech-20260220-0220-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260220-0220-mp-tech-pod-128]] [[tech-20251218-1218-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251218-1218-mp-tech-pod-128]]

## Key Claims
- Sponsored answers have a trust and market-design problem: a few answer slots can give paid placement outsized influence. [[tech-20251218-1218-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251218-1218-mp-tech-pod-128]]
- Ad incumbents have advertiser bases and conversion data that AI entrants need to build or partner for. [[tech-20251218-1218-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251218-1218-mp-tech-pod-128]]
- Publisher links, organic citations and paid placements compete in a compressed answer interface; visibility alone may not restore referral traffic. [[tech-20260220-0220-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260220-0220-mp-tech-pod-128]]
- Paid search can confer unearned credibility on fraudulent destinations, requiring labeling and verification; a conventional-search scam is a warning, not proof about AI ads. [[tech-20260223-0223-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260223-0223-mp-tech-pod-128]]
- An incumbent can face a revenue trough when chatbot use rises before replacement ad formats work. [[lanjian-hangtian-wancheng-zhongguo-shouci-ludi-huojian-huishou-yushu-keji-shizhi-chaoguo-3000-yi-1007302506]]

## Evidence
- **Platform, user and advertiser.** [[GarrettJohnson]] at [[BostonUniversity]] explains in [[MarketplaceTech]] that [[Google]], [[Meta]] and [[Amazon]] have mature ad systems, while a new entrant such as [[OpenAI]]’s [[ChatGPT]], [[Gemini]] or [[Perplexity]] may start with text/product listings. Shopping comparison is a plausible early ad use case; [[Walmart]] and [[Shopify]] commerce relationships might supply purchase feedback, but an AI answer presents fewer merchants than a search page and user trust can be lost if sponsorship is obscure. [[AgenticCommerce]] adds payment and recommendation boundaries. [[tech-20251218-1218-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251218-1218-mp-tech-pod-128]]
- **Organic versus sponsored.** [[ErinGriffith]] reports plans to let brands pay beside OpenAI answers while explaining that specific machine-readable facts, not generic AI marketing filler, help [[GenerativeEngineOptimization|organic visibility]]. [[AIDiscoverySEO]] and [[AISearchAnalytics]] track mention, citation and performance but cannot equate visibility with sales. The stated ad plan is source-time reporting, not a claim of a launched universal format. [[tech-20260304-0304-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260304-0304-mp-tech-pod-128]]
- **Publisher source economics.** A Marketplace Tech episode on [[GoogleAIOverviews|Google AI Overviews]] says more prominent publisher links may still fail to compensate [[DailyMail]] and other publishers for lost open-web referral traffic. [[AIAnswerSourceAttribution]] and [[OpenWebTrafficDecline]] concern source credit as distinct from paid ad inventory. [[tech-20260220-0220-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260220-0220-mp-tech-pod-128]]
- **Trust transfer and incumbent transition.** A fake [[Davines]] retail site reached the host through a sponsored Google search result; [[SearchAdTrustGap]] and [[FakeRetailWebsiteImpersonation]] illustrate the danger when placement reads like verification, even though this was *not* an AI answer ad. A separate source reports [[Baidu]] second-quarter revenue down 4% year on year and online marketing down nearly 20% while AI search monetization was delayed; this is a reported snapshot, not proof AI was the sole cause. [[lanjian-hangtian-wancheng-zhongguo-shouci-ludi-huojian-huishou-yushu-keji-shizhi-chaoguo-3000-yi-1007302506]] [[tech-20260223-0223-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260223-0223-mp-tech-pod-128]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- Advertising can subsidize inference and relevant shopping queries, but commercialization need not mix a sponsor into the factual answer; labeling and verification matter. [[tech-20251218-1218-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20251218-1218-mp-tech-pod-128]]
- Publisher visibility, paid conversion and organic recommendation require separate measurement. A fake conventional search result cannot be used as an observed AI-search-ad fraud rate. [[tech-20260223-0223-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260223-0223-mp-tech-pod-128]] [[tech-20260220-0220-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260220-0220-mp-tech-pod-128]]

## What Changed
- Added the publisher-referral and sponsored-result fraud boundaries to the answer-ad market-design question.
- Kept reported Baidu pressure as a dated multi-cause transition, not a universal financial forecast.
