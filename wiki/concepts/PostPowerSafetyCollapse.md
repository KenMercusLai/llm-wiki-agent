# Post-Power Safety Collapse / 失势后的安全崩塌

Post-power safety collapse / 失势后的安全崩塌 is the pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-957-ta-ceng-xiongba-yifang-weihe-ruonuo-dao-zai-cesuo-zijin-lnkhah144822mc4aa9bnezklgvoo|Hanji 957]] draws from [[HanFuLateHan|韩馥]] after he loses [[JizhouLateHan|冀州]] to [[YuanShao|袁绍]]. The episode argues that Han Fu does not merely lose title and land; he loses the team, reciprocal value, judgment confidence, and protective environment that made daily life safe.

The source's concrete sequence is [[ZhuHanLateHan|朱瀚]]'s revenge attack, Han Fu's move to [[ZhangMiaoLateHan|张邈]] in [[Chenliu|陈留]], and his panic after seeing a whispered conversation between Yuan Shao's envoy and Zhang Miao. Whether or not a killing order actually exists, Han Fu's position has become unreadable to him. Ambiguous signals become fatal because he no longer has institutional leverage, trusted people, or a stable role.

The concept is adjacent to [[PowerExitTrap|权力退场困境]], but it is narrower and weaker. Power exit trap usually concerns high-merit or high-enemy actors whose success makes retirement dangerous. Post-power safety collapse names the exposed condition of a failed or displaced holder whose earlier office no longer protects him and whose remaining fear destroys usable judgment.

## Key Claims

- Losing office can remove practical safety even when no formal death sentence has been issued.
- Old subordinates and minor resentments can become dangerous once the former superior no longer controls the environment.
- Survival after displacement depends on retained relationships, value, and judgment; without them, ordinary ambiguity can become unbearable.
- This pattern is not praised as prudent fear. In Hanji 957, Han Fu is a negative case because panic and earlier personnel failure compound each other.
