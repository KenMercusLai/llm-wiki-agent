# AI Worker Literacy

## Definition
AI worker literacy is role-appropriate capacity to use, question, validate and govern AI at work, not a universal prompt certificate or guarantee of employment.

## Current Synthesis
Learning begins in school and continues through professional practice, yet tool skill alone does not fix unequal access, manager unreadiness, displacement anxiety or employer incentives. Useful literacy couples experimentation with domain fundamentals, privacy, evaluation and organizational trust.

## Key Claims
- Introductory exposure can lower fear but cannot by itself redesign jobs or guarantee protection against layoffs.
- Specialist fluency includes domain data, statistics, evaluation and governed deployment, not only prompting or API familiarity.
- Organizational adoption depends on managers, training and permission boundaries as well as motivated workers.
- Adoption choices vary by career stage; reluctance can reflect autonomy and incentives rather than inability.

## Evidence
- **From school to first use.** [[ep-9-chatgpt-and-education-systems]] has educator [[JosephStrader]] of [[GoCyberAcademy]] advocate K–12 computer science, cybersecurity and AI vocabulary alongside pencil-and-paper reasoning and teacher assistance, not only bans or text detectors; this is [[AILiteracyAgainstWorship|literacy against tool worship]]. [[ep-17-ais-impact-on-creativity-a-consumers-perspective]] reports [[MarkDataScienceWithSam|Mark]] cutting a seven-to-eight-minute speech preparation from about 90 to 20–30 minutes in his own case, while still checking facts, editing and rehearsing; he tests [[GoogleAppsScript]] as [[AIAssistedLightCoding]] and keeps confidential work inside approved licenses. [[tech-20260327-0327-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260327-0327-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports a [[USDepartmentOfLabor|U.S. Department of Labor]] text-message course lasting about one week at ten minutes a day; [[MariaCurie|Maria Curi]] calls it reassuring but insufficient against job displacement.
- **Professional formation.** [[ep-10-a-thought-provoking-chat-with-an-actuary-and-tedx-speaker]] has [[CharlesJohnson]] connect actuarial self-study, regulation and sign-off to AI use, with data scientists and actuaries complementing each other on underwriting rather than replacing domain accountability. [[ep-15-unveiling-data-scientists-role-in-the-generative-ai-era]] has [[MarinaDataScienceWithSam|Marina]] include [[PromptAsIntentTransmission|prompts]], APIs, web demos, compute budgets, generated-code review, synthetic-data privacy and [[GenerativeAIUseCaseTriage|bias/use-case triage]]. [[ep-16-data-decoded-navigating-the-ai-revolution]] has [[VishalDataScienceWithSam|Vishal]] retain SQL, Python, statistics, [[PredictiveModelValidation]], explanations and [[DataScienceStorytelling|business communication]]; his churn example connects logistic-regression scores to [[Salesforce]] actions.
- **Readiness is social.** [[tech-20260803-0803-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260803-0803-mp-tech-pod-128]] attributes to [[PriyaRathod]] of [[Indeed]] a behavioral—not age-based—definition of [[AINativeWorker|AI-native workers]], with worker skill uncertainty and [[ManagerialAIFluencyGap]] despite experimentation; the reported survey lacks detailed subgroup figures. [[tech-20260416-0416-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260416-0416-mp-tech-pod-128]] reports [[LaurenWeber]]'s older-worker example: a 68-year-old uses AI to learn Spanish personally but rejects another employer-led transition. The source reports U.S. 55-plus participation around 37% versus about 40% a decade before, citing pandemic and retirement finances too; relational tacit knowledge can be lost if experienced workers exit.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- A speech-preparation time saving in one interview is not a workforce productivity estimate. The older-worker participation change cannot be attributed solely to AI. Faster adoption by younger workers does not prove superior depth, and literacy training does not confer job security or bargaining power.
- AI fluency requires suitable data governance, role-specific permission and sufficient management support; forcing output targets can undermine trust.

## What Changed
- Reframed course, professional and worker cases as a continuum of foundations, specialist verification and organizational conditions.
