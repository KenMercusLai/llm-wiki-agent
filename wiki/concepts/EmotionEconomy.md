# Emotion Economy / 情绪经济

## Definition
The emotion economy is the sale of objects, experiences, identity or interpersonal response partly for their expected effect on mood and belonging, without implying medical efficacy.

## Current Synthesis
Comfort purchases, paid listening, household fragrance, aspirational brands and shared dessert scenes use different labor and supply systems. The user's felt benefit is real as an experience, while clinical claims and market-size inferences require separate evidence.

## Key Claims
- Emotional value can arise through touch, ritual, aesthetic self-image or shared occasions rather than a single product category.
- Paid listening involves labor and platform incentives that can support immediate contact yet reward dependency or blur therapeutic boundaries.
- Scent products turn private atmosphere and cultural memory into consumer offerings dependent on material sourcing and storytelling.
- Premium fashion and fragrance can sell an aspired self, but attention by foreign visitors is not established overseas sales.
- Desserts can bundle family time and celebration with taste, but emotion alone does not settle unit economics.

[[TactileComfortConsumption]] and [[RitualHopeConsumption]] express different mechanisms.
- [[ep236-lao-wu-kuai-qian-xinlihua-fufei-de-peiban-shi-zhende-ma-ltvuoeluoeu4-a3j3lamhc3buudx]] documents [[PaidListeningServices]] as paid matching, warmth, timing, silence and follow-up, with online hours, platform commissions and renewal tactics; [[AICompanionAttentionRisk]] creates a parallel always-available response problem. Self-harm threats or videos expose untrained listeners to crises; [[EmotionalServicePlatformIncentives]], [[ListeningTherapyBoundary]] and [[MentalHealthCrisisInterventionBoundary]] distinguish human labor from treatment.
- [[ep263-xianxiang-jueqi-faxian-zhongshi-xiangqi-de-meili-lq1lsqcprqyr7auo5gslwyymuiv]] follows [[LineIncenseDailyLife]] from formal incense appreciation into reading and home decompression, linking [[ChineseFragrance|Chinese fragrance]] and osmanthus memory, [[Agarwood|沉香]] supply, grafted [[Qinan|奇楠]], blending and [[Guanxia|观夏]] brand storytelling. A calmer evening is a consumer account, not a demonstrated sleep therapy.
- [[144-guohuo-chuhai-xinqushi-shanxia-yousong-guanxia-pane-feng-tou-quan-1-144-1]] attributes to the [[FengTouQuan|疯投圈]] hosts an [[AspirationalEmotionalPremium]] around [[ShanxiaYousong|山下有松]]'s cultural textures, [[Guanxia|观夏]]'s scent and [[PANE]]'s design; foreign tourist/store and Instagram attention, unlike a verified [[QualityLowPriceReasonedPremium|quality-price premium]], may precede distribution and is not audited sales.
- [[145-gaijia-zhongzi-de-canyin-yangpinpai-feng-tou-quan-1-145-1]] reports [[YerenXiansheng|野人先生]] and [[HaagenDazs|哈根达斯]] as family, celebration and ice-cream-shop comparisons, with [[MeetFresh|鲜芋仙]] in the restaurant-asset context. [[FreshMadeIceCreamRetail]] still needs local menus, store size, supply and repeat traffic to sustain margins.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- [[ep236-lao-wu-kuai-qian-xinlihua-fufei-de-peiban-shi-zhende-ma-ltvuoeluoeu4-a3j3lamhc3buudx]] does not equate companionship with psychotherapy; AI roleplay, manifestation and scent claims cannot replace crisis intervention. [[ep263-xianxiang-jueqi-faxian-zhongshi-xiangqi-de-meili-lq1lsqcprqyr7auo5gslwyymuiv]] and [[ep236-lao-wu-kuai-qian-xinlihua-fufei-de-peiban-shi-zhende-ma-ltvuoeluoeu4-a3j3lamhc3buudx]] share [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]], and [[144-guohuo-chuhai-xinqushi-shanxia-yousong-guanxia-pane-feng-tou-quan-1-144-1]] and [[145-gaijia-zhongzi-de-canyin-yangpinpai-feng-tou-quan-1-145-1]] share another; their observations are not four independent market surveys. Some [[145-gaijia-zhongzi-de-canyin-yangpinpai-feng-tou-quan-1-145-1]] restaurant ownership items are unrelated to emotional premium.

## What Changed
- Split mood relief, service labor, scent ritual, aspirational identity and family occasions; separated emotional benefit from clinical or commercial proof.
