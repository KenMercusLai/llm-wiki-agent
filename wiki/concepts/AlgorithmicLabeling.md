# Algorithmic Labeling

## Definition
Algorithmic labeling is the reduction of people or cultural material to categories that travel through feeds or govern platform visibility. This page distinguishes socially circulated identity shorthand from a platform's internal classification and prediction: the two can interact, but the sources do not establish that every interpersonal label was produced by an algorithm.

## Current Synthesis
Fast labels can make a social observation legible while concealing variation within a group. In interpersonal life, class and ability tags risk replacing direct knowledge of a person; in platform systems, inferred categories and eligibility rules affect what can be seen and which publics users are told they belong to. The corrective is contextual interpretation and greater awareness of sorting mechanisms, not the abolition of every useful category or measurement.

## Key Claims
- Feed-friendly class and identity shorthand can preempt patient judgment of particular people.
- A measured-ability label becomes a hierarchy of worth when a test score or school identity is converted into status and contempt.
- A contested identity label can instead open inquiry into the opportunity structure and conflicting demands behind it.
- Platform classifications govern inclusion and visibility as well as inferred user identity; this differs from merely using a social nickname.

## Evidence
### Claim 1 — social shorthand
- In the Japan-as-comparison discussion, the speakers name labels such as “县城婆罗门,” “万柳书院少爷,” and “小镇做题家” and worry that students may interpret classmates through such tags before meeting them as individuals. This is an episode-level concern about offline spillover, not a demonstrated universal effect. [[vol-102-aoguo-jiuye-bingheqi-de-riben-nianqingren-qu-nali-xunzhao-xingfugan-chuantai-qingdao-kuaima-lmpluij7rcfq49cxcyb65x3f7zfn]]

### Claim 2 — measurement becomes rank
- The history of IQ testing distinguishes Binet and Simon's school-support use from later institutional sorting and eugenic uses. Its present-day warning is that scores, degrees, school identities, and “智力婆罗门” posturing can confer an undeserved aura of scientific legitimacy on contempt. [[37-zhishang-ceshi-qingwen-ni-shi-zhili-poluomen-ma-608020679]]

### Claim 3 — unpacking the tag
- Jinzi's account complicates “小镇做题家”: study offered a route out of a town with limited visible alternatives, while school discipline, gendered beauty rules, and later urban-work expectations constrained the same person in different ways. The label is analytically useful only when these lived mechanisms remain visible. [[140-haikeyi-de-jin-nvshi-suoyi-ren-weishenme-yao-nuli-a-854173655]]

### Claim 4 — platform classification
- The algorithm-sociology episode describes inclusion rules and ranking that can hide work without deleting it, behavioral profiles built from clicks and skips, and inferred “people like you” publics that feed back into identity. It also describes creators adapting titles and tags for recognition. These are platform mechanisms, not direct evidence that all social shorthand arises from recommendation systems. [[159-suanfa-de-liufu-miankong-ta-shi-ruhe-cong-chuli-shuju-biancheng-dingyi-women-shi-shui-de-lh5ng5mbvkworfxlq9l-coscepc7]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- The Japan discussion is a comparative conversation, not a controlled study of youth labeling or a prediction for all societies. [[vol-102-aoguo-jiuye-bingheqi-de-riben-nianqingren-qu-nali-xunzhao-xingfugan-chuantai-qingdao-kuaima-lmpluij7rcfq49cxcyb65x3f7zfn]]
- IQ-style testing may help someone identify strengths and limits; the objection is to assigning dignity or social rank from a score, not every individualized use of a test. [[37-zhishang-ceshi-qingwen-ni-shi-zhili-poluomen-ma-608020679]]
- The algorithm episode resists a total-cocoon story: users who mix platforms and sources may encounter more varied material. Jinzi's account is a counterexample to flattening a social label, not evidence that algorithmic sorting is beneficial. [[159-suanfa-de-liufu-miankong-ta-shi-ruhe-cong-chuli-shuju-biancheng-dingyi-women-shi-shui-de-lh5ng5mbvkworfxlq9l-coscepc7]] [[140-haikeyi-de-jin-nvshi-suoyi-ren-weishenme-yao-nuli-a-854173655]]

## What Changed
- Separated interpersonal shorthand, psychometric ranking, and platform data classification rather than giving all three a single causal explanation.
- Recast “小镇做题家” as a prompt for contextual analysis, not an immutable identity.
- Made the limits of the youth-comparison and algorithmic-cocoon claims explicit.
