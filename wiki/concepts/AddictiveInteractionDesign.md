# Addictive Interaction Design

## Definition
Interface choices that shorten deliberation, disguise spending or use uncertain rewards to prolong engagement beyond reflective intention. Engagement alone is not a diagnosis of addiction, and settings differ.

## Current Synthesis
Gambling machines make mechanisms visible; live auctions and feeds borrow some but not all. Safety interventions, contested lawsuits and counter-friction reveal where empirical and legal questions remain.

## Key Claims
- Credits, near misses, quick cycles and exit friction can turn losses into a continuous machine-gambling session.
- Live commerce can make distinct purchases feel like rapid social play with uncertain rewards.
- Platforms’ safeguard experiments can affect exposure while leaving individual causation disputed.
- Youth-platform and AI-companion harm theories require separate causal and legal tests.
- Counter-design adds friction but can also reuse the engagement mechanics it opposes.

## Evidence
- [[44-yunqi-de-youer-dubo-chengyin-yinwei-women-haipa-ziyou-619995308]] reads [[TheLureOfLuck]] and [[MachineGamblingAddiction]] through credits rather than cash, [[NearMissDesign]], [[IntermittentReinforcement]], fast spins, casino environment, data-guided rewards, cash-out friction and the [[MachineZone]]; loot boxes and micropayments are analogies, not identical gambling products. The relevant question is whether a pause to cash out or feel cumulative cost is removed.
- [[buai-zhibo-daihuo-de-oumei-xiaofeizhe-weihe-zai-zhejia-pingtai-shang-daba-huaqian-1007104138]] describes [[Whatnot]]’s [[LiveAuctionCommerce]] with one-swipe bids, 5–15-second countdowns (about half under 10 seconds in the episode), sounds, winning animations, raffles, memberships and [[OnlineCardBreaking]] of sealed collectible cards by team. [[GamblingAdjacentCommerceDesign]] denotes the speed/randomness concern, not a finding that all its transactions are illegal gambling.
- [[tech-20260812-0812-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260812-0812-mp-tech-pod-128]] relays [[OliviaCarville]]’s report that a 2021 [[TikTok]] filter-bubble safeguard was withheld from about 10% of U.S. accounts, roughly 15 million, including [[ChaseNasca]]’s; his later death and 7,563 reviewed videos make [[PlatformSafetyABTesting]] and [[InternalSafetyResearchExposure]] consequential. The family suit encountered [[Section230]]; the reported withholding does **not** itself establish why he died or that the safeguard would have prevented it.
- [[tech-20260205-0205-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260205-0205-mp-tech-pod-128]] records plaintiffs’ alleged intentionally addictive design and [[EricGoldman]]’s challenge of [[PlatformLegalCausation]] in youth social-media litigation; [[Meta]] and [[YouTube]] deny the allegations while Snap and TikTok reportedly settled. [[tech-20260716-0716-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260716-0716-mp-tech-pod-128]] records [[GaiaBernstein]] on state AG suits over [[Facebook]]/[[Instagram]], a claimed $1.4 trillion tied to 2012–2024 young users, privacy/COPPA and a contested [[BigTobaccoPlatformAnalogy]]; her [[AICompanionAttentionRisk]] warning about memory, anthropomorphism and sycophancy is prospective, not proof of equivalent harms.
- [[tech-20260102-0102-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260102-0102-mp-tech-pod-128]] shows [[Clearspace]] breathing prompts and pauses, app blockers/lockboxes and a proposed heavy phone case as [[ScreenTimeFriction]]. [[TanyaSujohn]] notes that well-being apps’ dashboards, streaks and friend features can themselves imitate retention systems; [[DesignedAgencyInGames]] and [[FlowEnvironmentDesign]] remind us that chosen immersion is not necessarily trapped attention.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Product mechanisms, clinical dependence and liability are distinct. The TikTok safeguard exclusion is reported, and an individual suicide cannot be causally attributed from this account alone. The Whatnot gambling comparison and Meta youth-harm allegations remain contested; AI-companion effects are warnings. Reported monetary totals and litigation postures are source-time claims.

## What Changed
- Separates casino mechanism, auction purchase, reported safeguard withholding, disputed legal causation and counter-friction.
