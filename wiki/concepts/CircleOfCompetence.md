# Circle Of Competence

## Definition
A circle of competence is the boundary within which an investor can explain a business or instrument, test its assumptions, size a position and endure its risks without borrowed conviction.

## Current Synthesis
The boundary is neither a fixed list of industries nor an excuse to stop learning. It combines business research, permanent-loss awareness, portfolio purpose and behavioral fit. [[AIInvestmentResearch|AI research]] may speed preparation, but it does not supply judgment or make a strategy suitable for every investor.

## Key Claims
- Competence requires honest but expandable business understanding rather than a convenient “outside my circle” excuse.
- Knowing a company's customer behavior and durable economics differs from reading a price chart as a causal model.
- A good company or low valuation is not enough; expectations, catalysts, time horizon and the investor's temperament affect implementation.
- Capital concentration should track both understanding and purpose; unfamiliar assets can be small learning positions rather than core holdings.
- Investing-worldview fit includes rule culture, liquidity and tolerance for leverage; choosing not to pick stocks can be rational.

## Evidence
- [[e160-yige-jiazhi-touzizhe-de-20-nian-huigu-qiu-jifen-qiu-shenglv-qiu-shijian-lixen828sknlujulev9evt37mbuf]] records a value investor's self-audit: cash-flow durability, lifecycle, [[FinancialStatementAnalysis|close reading of reports]], [[BusinessMoat|changing moat]] and [[MarginOfSafety|not paying for optimistic outcomes]] underpin conviction. He uses roughly 10%, 5% and small research positions as personal sizing heuristics, not universal portfolio rules; AI sorts information but does not substitute for analyst judgment.
- [[ep80-yu-chali-mangge-de-kuashikong-duihua-dang-yanjing-shiming-shi-women-kanjian-shenme-lh4q7vpcfqoynssw23dbwcerkn7i]] uses an imagined [[CharlieMunger|Munger]]/[[WarrenBuffett|Buffett]] dialogue around [[SeesCandies|See's]] gifting trust, [[CocaCola|Coke]] habit and [[AmericanExpress|AmEx]] cardholder/merchant retention after the salad-oil scandal to contrast business-understanding with chart-only certainty. The historical interpretation is source-scoped; the case does not rule out all disciplined technical trading.
- [[139-paopao-mate-he-pinduoduo-zhide-touzi-me-feng-tou-quan-1-139-1]] has [[ICEInvestor]] distinguish [[PopMart]]'s growth and [[Labubu]] concentration from its valuation, and [[Pinduoduo]]'s low multiple from a guaranteed rerating. [[AICompressedInvestmentResearchAdvantage|AI-assisted research]] and catalysts cannot repair an unsuitable short- or long-term game when [[BehavioralInvestingBiases|behavioral biases]] undermine the investor.
- [[vol-105-ruhe-panduan-yige-touzi-zuhe-shifou-shihe-ziji-lmaowq8820pa0jyjw6z93b6hstpe]] ties [[PortfolioSuitability]] to housing/tuition versus long-horizon goals, [[AdaptivePortfolioDesign|diversification]], liquidity and comprehension of derivatives, snowball products, crypto and single stocks. Its 1%-decision waiting rule and 5%-monthly action cap are the host's examples, not validated universal thresholds.
- [[vol-110-touzi-jiushi-dui-shijieguan-de-toupiao-maichu-zichan-peizhi-di-yi-bu-wanjiepian-lk5jkgnzvzerymcixylsdqdtn7f8]] argues [[InvestmentWorldviewFit]]: a method based on property, value, quant, crypto or diversification may be coherent for its practitioner but not holdable for someone with different [[RiskPerception|risk perception]], horizon or leverage aversion. [[HumanCapitalInflationHedge|Earning capacity]] may matter more than market participation for some households.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- All examples are podcast judgments, not personalized investment advice or independent return tests. A competence claim must remain falsifiable by follow-up study and losses; comfort alone is no proof of expertise.

## What Changed
- Business-research boundaries now include portfolio purpose, behavior, instrument mechanics and worldview rather than an industry-only label.

## Related Concepts
