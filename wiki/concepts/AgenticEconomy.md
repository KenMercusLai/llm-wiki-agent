# Agentic Economy

## Definition
An agentic economy is the proposed network of agents, callable services and payment/identity infrastructure supporting delegated work and exchange. The source uses it as an emerging architecture and investment thesis, not an established market of autonomous employers and firms.

## Current Synthesis
The current evidence concerns prerequisites and limited demonstrations: tool contracts, safe runtimes, token supply and bounded payment. Differentiated agents hiring agents, zero-person companies and instant personalized inference remain speaker forecasts. Whether value accrues to models, harnesses, merchants or users is contested.

## Key Claims
- An execution economy needs agent-callable actions, sandboxed runtime, governed context and memory before repeated services can be delegated.
- Token price alone is a poor scale metric: capability per task, latency, peak capacity, routing and security constrain production economics.
- Payments need user mandates, merchant integration, limits, traceability and dispute handling for both human shopping and agents buying task resources.
- Agent-to-agent differentiated marketplaces are a plausible but unproven network effect, not demonstrated by agent-only social chatter.
- Model-as-OS and harness-as-OS arguments propose different capture points; neither proves a single inevitable winner.

## Evidence
- **Execution substrate:** [[agent-yuannian-di-500-tian-shenme-zai-xiaoshi-shenme-zai-dansheng-weishenme-women-bugai-zai-touzi-gui-siwei-de-ruanjian-lhwdxfpke3bmamjk4e6knk-5sn-b]] has [[TianjieJack]] and [[CangShifu]] argue that [[AgentFacingInterfaces|callable tools]], sandbox, memory, databases and payments precede a mature network, while GUI remains useful for trust. [[tan-mi-claude-code-gao-dong-agent-harness-dui-tan-lai-xin-lu-lkluk3i7c4gzw4jvxee7odsfgis3]] gives [[LaiXinlu]] of [[ShareAI]]'s three [[AgentHarness|harness]] layers—execution, context and governance—with cloud/personal-device networking and [[KComputer]] as proposed infrastructure. [[CodePilot]] and [[YouyouAgent]] are source examples/experiments, not evidence of commercial-scale agent trade.
- **Supply economics:** [[1-yi-token-julebu-jibaole-ai-de-ranliao-bugoule-duitan-yu-wenyuan-aliyun-bailian-jishu-fuzeren-ltn5k9jd9e04i5mfdkdo-ycoslsm]] has [[YuWenyuan]] of [[AliyunBailian]] describe [[MaaSInfrastructure|serving]] with peak scheduling, latency, sandbox, browser/search and observability. His neocloud thesis favors hiding GPU complexity behind AI-native model services rather than raw capacity resale; his 'need not self-build' case is a provider position that may fail under privacy, compliance or strategic-control constraints. [[dang-women-zai-taolun-harness-de-shihou-women-zai-taolun-shenme-shendu-duitan-minimax-hermes-agent-lvhm1cfno7mqmfv3g0aajmw4zdpd]] discusses [[HermesAgent]], [[AgentIdentityAndAuthentication|identity]] and subscription agents consuming budgets rather than merely hosted results such as [[Manus]]. [[TokenGrant]] is an early-stage token-budget proposal, not proof of profitability. Token use can rise while [[AIInferenceCostStructure|cost per successful result]] falls; neither number alone establishes customer value.
- **Bounded transactions:** [[keyi-gei-nide-agent-fa-yidian-linghuaqian-le-s10e22-9a652c19-ceb3-46c2-87b4-bca36e684311]] has [[PatrickWu]] and [[GaoNing]] of [[Clink]] describe [[AgentPaymentInfrastructure|Visa-mediated]] one-time payment credentials conditioned on product, price, category and user intent. Buying a human's product differs from a delegated budget for APIs or tokens; [[AgentSpendControls]] and [[AgentPermissionBoundaries]] include reauthorization, refunds, merchant fulfillment and dispute attribution. A demo is not a standing autonomous account.
- **Network hypothesis and weak proxy:** [[142-yusen-de-chuangtou-guancha-di-2-ji-harness-xia-yige-zijie-2026-da-jihui-he-stanley-druckenmiller-lg4sphlaunrjuulqraxs-1gc5ufz]] has [[DaiYusen]] imagine [[AgentMarketplace|specialized personal agents]] buying each other's taste, memory or interview-preparation capabilities. [[tech-20260213-tech-pod-128-tech-20260213-tech-pod-128]] describes [[MoteBook]]'s [[AISocialNetworks|agent-only social experiment]] alongside a reported [[Wiz]] exposure: chatter and uncertain authorship do not establish paid exchange or safe [[AgentSelfEvolution|agent differentiation]].
- **Capture-point tension:** [[136-quanqiu-da-moxing-jibao-di-9-ji-he-guang-miliao-coding-shi-agi-di-er-mu-guigu-yusanjia-zhenxiang-moxing-zheng-chengwei-xin-yidai-os-lh-cqyoss-dztmyb5kmbjapa6w9v]] frames frontier models as [[ModelAsOperatingSystem|an OS-like platform]] in an [[AGIThreeActs|AI second act]], while Dai Yusen puts accumulated memory, permissions and task routing in the harness. Both are forecasts: [[AIInvestmentMetrics|measuring value]] needs revenue, cost reduction or outcomes rather than [[TokenMaxxing|raw usage]].

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- Agent-to-agent hiring, instant personalized weights, fully autonomous organizations and future [[EverythingAgent|everything agents]] are proposals; no source establishes broad market clearing, lawful liability or a stable price mechanism.
- MoteBook's bot authorship and reported leak are cautionary, not proof of transactions. Platform providers' claims about 'no need to self-host' depend on data sovereignty, regulation and strategic control.

## What Changed
- Reorganized eight sources around execution, supply, mandates, network speculation and capture-point competition.
- Distinguished demonstrations from forecasts and usage metrics from profit or customer outcomes.
