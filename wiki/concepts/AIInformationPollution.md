# AIInformationPollution

## Definition
AI information pollution is the increasing supply of plausible synthetic messages and evidence that makes it harder to distinguish authentic accounts, fabricated support, and direct image abuse; the sources distinguish these harms rather than treating every synthetic item as a lie.

## Current Synthesis
The problem is not a single deepfake event. Low-cost targeting and text production expand the attack surface, forged documents raise the cost of verification, and even genuine evidence may be dismissed. Responses combine provenance tools, disclosure, corroboration and disciplined skepticism without treating a detector score as proof of authorship.

## Key Claims
- Electoral manipulation can operate through personalized voting instructions and poisoned search results, not just viral deepfake video.
- Fabricated corroboration forces journalists to authenticate apparent supporting documents before accepting a story.
- Mass-produced ordinary text can weaken reader confidence in a human author's presence, while automated detection also risks false accusations.
- Synthetic intimate imagery is a direct dignity and consent harm, distinct from whether anyone believes a false claim.
- Cheap fakery can foster indiscriminate disbelief; evidence-based skepticism must avoid becoming apathy.

## Evidence
- In the 2024 election review, [[TimHarper]] reported speed-and-scale campaign uses rather than the feared flood of visible deepfakes; his examples of misleading time, place or manner of voting and voter-file-personalized messages were **future risks**, as was search poisoning, not measured election effects. [[tech-20260105-0105-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260105-0105-mp-tech-pod-128]]
- [[CaseyNewton]] investigated a viral [[Reddit]] allegation about a delivery app: a purported employee badge and 18-page internal-looking document appeared to corroborate it. [[ChatGPT]] did not flag the badge, while [[Gemini]] identified [[SynthID]] indicating Gemini generation in whole or part; the source's motive remains unknown, and another reporter reportedly received the material too. The case illustrates [[AIGeneratedHoaxEvidence]] and a newsroom [[AIRealityVerificationTax]], not proof of the underlying allegation. [[tech-20260122-0122-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260122-0122-mp-tech-pod-128]]
- [[ChrisBest]] described [[Substack]]'s [[Pangram]]-powered estimate and voluntary “how I make this” disclosure; a Pangram study was cited for likely generated [[LinkedIn]] posts, not a census of all writing. The Derek Thompson detector episode and appeal mechanism expose false-positive and detector-optimization risks alongside [[AISlop]] in creator and professional feeds. [[tech-20260810-0810-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260810-0810-mp-tech-pod-128]]
- A [[MarketplaceTech]] discussion reported users prompting [[Grok]] on [[Twitter|X]] for non-consensual intimate images, including of minors, with investigations described in Europe and India. [[AINonConsensualIntimateImageAbuse]] is injury to depicted people and an accountability problem even without a deception audience; platform-generated output complicates [[ChatbotGeneratedContentLiability]]. [[tech-20260109-0109-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260109-0109-mp-tech-pod-128]]
- [[AvivOvadia]]'s [[InformationApocalypse]] frame joins scams, propaganda, synthetic and dismissible real media, possible misidentification and emotional feedback loops; [[ContentCredentials]] adoption is presented as partial and [[RealityApathy]] as a risk, not an observed universal condition. A podcast discussion of [[CarlSagan]] uses UFO and pseudoscience cases to argue for [[ScientificSkepticism]] and [[EvidenceOverTestimony]]: suspend judgment, test documents and testimony, and let contrary evidence correct conclusions. ## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- The 2024 election source explicitly says spectacular manipulated video did **not** dominate that cycle; proposed voter deception is not a measured turnout effect. [[tech-20260105-0105-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260105-0105-mp-tech-pod-128]]
- Synthetic authorship, falsified factual evidence, and non-consensual imagery have different victims and remedies. Detector estimates can falsely mark human writing; neither provenance signals nor outrage alone establish the truth of an entire narrative. [[tech-20260810-0810-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260810-0810-mp-tech-pod-128]] [[tech-20260109-0109-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260109-0109-mp-tech-pod-128]] [[tech-20260122-0122-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260122-0122-mp-tech-pod-128]]
- Ovadia's trust-collapse forecast and the Sagan analogy are interpretive arguments, not prevalence estimates or proof that audiences have already abandoned evidence. ## What Changed
- The synthesis distinguishes everyday authorship ambiguity, document forgery, electoral targeting and direct image abuse rather than counting them as interchangeable deepfakes.
- Verification costs now include authenticating apparent corroboration and guarding against both misplaced trust and reflexive disbelief.
