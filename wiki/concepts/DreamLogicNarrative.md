# Dream Logic Narrative

## Definition
Dream-logic narrative links episodes through association, bodily or linguistic transformation and emotional pressure more than through an ordinary causal plot.

## Current Synthesis
The Alice upper and lower readings make a continuous case: a White Rabbit's time pressure leads through scale changes and verbal games to a tea party with stopped time, arbitrary trial procedure and waking resistance. Pinocchio supplies a darker punishment-and-care version; Sarah Pinsker's *Dream Baby* lets dream experience alter waking bodies and communities without settling its cause. These are literary readings from one programme, not four independent theories of dreaming.

## Key Claims
- Improvised transitions can carry a story by curiosity and recurring symbols without a conventional plot arc.
- Scale, identity, language, stalled time and arbitrary authority make dream incoherence intelligible as recurring pressures rather than random scenes.
- A dreamlike sequence can move from danger, hunger and punishment toward family care instead of playful nonsense.
- Dream and waking reality can interpenetrate without the story resolving whether the experience is psychological, biological or external.

## Evidence
- Improvisation and entry: [[179-ailisi-mengyou-xianjing-shijie-duo-huangdan-wo-yeshi-ziji-de-zhuzai-shang-975483805]] describes Lewis Carroll's oral storytelling origin, Alice Liddell's historical context handled cautiously, and the White Rabbit's deadline drawing [[AliceWonderlandCharacter|Alice]] through the rabbit hole. Drink, cake, tears, doors, mouse, mushroom and Duchess scenes change body and identity by association; the Caterpillar asks who she is. Its tail/tale typography and verse parody make translation difficulty part of the form.
- Rules and agency: [[180-ailisi-mengyou-xianjing-shijie-duo-huangdan-wo-yeshi-ziji-de-zhuzai-xia-975539611]] continues the same *Alice in Wonderland* reading through the Cheshire Cat's detached grin, a six-o'clock tea party whose table accumulates mess, moving croquet implements and a trial that judges before evidence. These contradictions are [[NonsenseLogic|nonsense logic]] as narrative rules, not random plot errors. [[AliceWonderlandCharacter|Alice]] finally calls the Queen of Hearts' power mere cards and wakes. The episode compares rabbit-hole thresholds with *[[PeachBlossomSpring|Peach Blossom Spring]]*, *[[SpiritedAway|Spirited Away]]* and *[[TheMatrix|The Matrix]]*; those are otherworld-portal comparisons, not proof that every portal is a dream.
- Dark growth: [[122-pinuocao-chengzhang-de-cantong-jiqi-suo-chuangzuo-de-777464172]] reads [[CarloCollodi|Collodi]]'s *Adventures of Pinocchio* through abrupt injury, fake food, animal transformations, little coffins, apparent death and shark-belly darkness. [[Pinocchio]]'s relationship to Geppetto's food, coat and later dependence, and the [[BlueFairy|Blue Fairy]]'s shifting mother/testing roles, give the nightmare a family-care trajectory; psychological symbolism is the hosts' interpretation.
- Unresolved waking effect: [[166-youyou-changfeng-wenrou-de-huanxiang-zhi-ge-fuping-jiaolv-de-gouhe-948043059]] reads Sarah Pinsker's *Dream Baby* as a repeatedly dreamed child accompanied by lactation, online forums and children appearing on the California coast. The story declines to decide among longing, trauma, alien intrusion or another cause, unlike the cleaner logical architecture the hosts ascribe to Ted Chiang's *Story of Your Life*.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- Alice 179 and 180 are two halves of one book discussion; all four notes are from 蜜獾吃书. The reading does not validate dream psychology or a historical assertion about Carroll's disputed biography.
- Pinocchio's animal/mother symbolism is source-attributed literary interpretation. *Dream Baby* does not verify an alien or medical mechanism; the rabbit hole also belongs to a broader portal tradition.

## What Changed
- Joined both Alice halves as one scene-to-scene argument, and contrasted playful, punitive and unresolved dream forms.
