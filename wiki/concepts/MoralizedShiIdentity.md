# Moralized Shi Identity / 士的道德化身份

Moralized shi identity / 士的道德化身份 is the shift described in [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-97-3-lvbuwei-lishishang-fengtou-di-yi-ren-lswa9b864oc-s8vaxtdu15arydu2]] after [[Pingyuanjun|平原君]] looks down on [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]] for associating with [[MaoGong|毛公]] and [[XueGong|薛公]]. The episode says "士" began as a low aristocratic rank with noble blood and ritual obligations, but the collapse of older Zhou hierarchies made that inherited status less stable.

In Xinlingjun's answer, the deciding question becomes conduct rather than pedigree. A person mixing with gamblers or selling wine can still be a worthy if his character and judgment merit respect. That recoding helps explain why Xinlingjun's low-status friendships can strengthen rather than damage his reputation inside [[WarringStatesRetainerPatronage|战国养士]].

The concept also frames [[LuZhonglian|鲁仲连]] in the same episode. His refusal of fief and gold is presented as an aristocratic-moral ideal: a worthy person helps solve a crisis without converting the act into wages. That honor logic becomes the background against which [[MerchantAristocraticValueConflict|merchant-commercial calculation]] looks suspect and [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] becomes narratively disruptive.

[[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-114-2-yanguo-cisha-qinshihuang-weihe-hui-xuan-jingke-lsewjrmgup1yanf8ipn-i0ykqwjx]] adds [[JingKe|荆轲]] as a darker late-Warring-States example. Jing Ke's worth is not proved by official rank or perfect fighting reputation; [[TianGuang|田光]] sees a bookish, wandering, market-connected侠士 whose moral identity can be activated for [[WeakStateAssassinationStrategy|弱-state assassination]]. The source therefore extends "士" from low-status worthiness into a death-mission identity.

## Key Claims
- "士" shifts from a rank label toward a moral evaluation as old aristocratic structures weaken.
- Low visible status does not disqualify a person from being treated as a士 if the patron can recognize worth.
- Refusing payment can function as a proof of moral identity inside this value world.
- The concept helps explain why merchant profit-seeking is initially framed as aesthetically and morally inferior before Lu Buwei enters.
- Qinji 114-2 adds that a wandering, non-official shi identity can be mobilized for assassination when reputation, reading,侠义, and low-suspicion access overlap.
