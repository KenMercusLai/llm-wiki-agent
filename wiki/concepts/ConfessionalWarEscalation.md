# Confessional War Escalation

Confessional war escalation is the pattern where religious identity, dynastic legitimacy, and interstate rivalry make local security decisions feed wider conflict. [[696-elizabeth-i-vs-the-catholics-killing-the-queen-of-scots-part-6-glt2334035496]] grounds the concept in the movement from the [[BabingtonPlot]] to [[MaryQueenOfScots|Mary, Queen of Scots]]' execution and then toward the [[SpanishArmada]].

The episode does not reduce the Armada to a single-cause reaction to Mary. Instead, it treats Mary's death as removing one succession threat while intensifying the Catholic-Protestant and Anglo-Spanish struggle already surrounding [[England]], [[Spain]], [[ElizabethI|Elizabeth I]], and Philip II.
