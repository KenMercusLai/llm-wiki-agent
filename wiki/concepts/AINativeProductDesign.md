# AI Native Product Design

## Definition
AI-native design makes model interpretation, context and adaptive behavior part of the product's basic interaction, not merely an AI feature in a fixed flow. [[ZhangYueguang]] calls AI necessary but insufficient: [[Miaoya]] generated portraits through a bounded internet-product flow, whereas his proposed [[Docky]] workflow begins with the context and smallest reliable PPT unit. [[130-zhang-yueguang-chuangye-liangnian-shouci-fangtan-miaoya-bushi-ai-native-chanpin-liucheng-dao-shangxiawen-sheji-one-way-door-he-yinv-youxi]]

## Current Synthesis
The accounts describe three related but unproven design moves: open-ended intent with explicit quality boundaries; context that survives interactions; and proactive work that still returns control to people. These are product theses from founders, not a universal definition or measured superiority over conventional interfaces. [[130-zhang-yueguang-chuangye-liangnian-shouci-fangtan-miaoya-bushi-ai-native-chanpin-liucheng-dao-shangxiawen-sheji-one-way-door-he-yinv-youxi]] [[135-he-ziran-xuanze-chuangshiren-tristan-liao-elys-saibo-fenshen-linghun-context-de-huoqu-yu-liudong-he-ai-shejiao-wangluo-ltwegwvo7grn-v-rft0txlmqmcty]] [[zhe-keneng-caishi-ai-peiban-zhenzheng-gai-you-de-yangzi-duitan-shuaping-chanpin-eve-chuangshiren-tristan-lgvcb1tuur-1rf2qk8jv9chmwew]]

## Key Claims
- A capable model does not make a fixed flow AI-native; the design must account for open input, variable output, editability and context. [[130-zhang-yueguang-chuangye-liangnian-shouci-fangtan-miaoya-bushi-ai-native-chanpin-liucheng-dao-shangxiawen-sheji-one-way-door-he-yinv-youxi]]
- Persistent user context can support a useful proactive handoff, but requires privacy and demonstrated value in exchange for disclosure. [[135-he-ziran-xuanze-chuangshiren-tristan-liao-elys-saibo-fenshen-linghun-context-de-huoqu-yu-liudong-he-ai-shejiao-wangluo-ltwegwvo7grn-v-rft0txlmqmcty]]
- A companion can be designed around memory, timing and relationship progression rather than chat duration alone. [[zhe-keneng-caishi-ai-peiban-zhenzheng-gai-you-de-yangzi-duitan-shuaping-chanpin-eve-chuangshiren-tristan-lgvcb1tuur-1rf2qk8jv9chmwew]]
- Teams must explore model limits, product taste and engineering together when reliable reference products do not exist. [[musical-ly-ruhe-chengwei-tiktok-pm-yanzhong-de-zijie-chanpin-wenhua-he-quanqiuhua-zhilu-zijie-tiaodong-di-5-ji-ludflvaw7lid-tci9gagbbxobvtz]] [[130-zhang-yueguang-chuangye-liangnian-shouci-fangtan-miaoya-bushi-ai-native-chanpin-liucheng-dao-shangxiawen-sheji-one-way-door-he-yinv-youxi]]

## Evidence
- **Interaction and quality boundary.** Zhang contrasts [[Miaoya]]'s “真、像、美” portrait output with [[Docky]]: template slides constrain content, code-generated slides may be hard to edit, and image slides may be polished but uneditable. His [[OneWayDoorProduct]] test asks whether users would return to the old workflow; he proposes short, frequent, low-latency agent loops that expand ability, not simply longer autonomous runs. [[ContextEngineering]], [[AgenticWorkflow]] and [[HumanAgentCollaboration]] determine what context is sufficient and what the user can correct. [[130-zhang-yueguang-chuangye-liangnian-shouci-fangtan-miaoya-bushi-ai-native-chanpin-liucheng-dao-shangxiawen-sheji-one-way-door-he-yinv-youxi]]
- **Social context and handoff.** [[Tristan]] describes [[Elys]] as a network where [[CyberAvatars]] represent values, goals, tone, taste, public work and remembered interactions, pre-filter or comment, then pass connections to actual people. The proposed [[ContextFlywheel]] depends on useful matches motivating further disclosure; a ski-group meetup is an illustration, not network-effect proof. [[AISocialNetworks]] describes this distinct social shape. [[135-he-ziran-xuanze-chuangshiren-tristan-liao-elys-saibo-fenshen-linghun-context-de-huoqu-yu-liudong-he-ai-shejiao-wangluo-ltwegwvo7grn-v-rft0txlmqmcty]]
- **Companion continuity.** [[EVE]] is presented as more than a [[CharacterAI]] roleplay box: approximately 128 memory slots, asynchronous reflection and merging, planning and model routing, proactive real-world topics, 3D scenes, outfits and game unlocks support relationship state. Emotional post-training and negative feedback distinguish this design from a compliant assistant; subscription and game purchases still must cover inference costs. [[AICompanionActiveMemory]], [[EmotionalInteractionModels]] and [[AIFriendProducts]] describe its mechanisms and category. [[zhe-keneng-caishi-ai-peiban-zhenzheng-gai-you-de-yangzi-duitan-shuaping-chanpin-eve-chuangshiren-tristan-lgvcb1tuur-1rf2qk8jv9chmwew]]
- **Organization and exploration.** Zhang rejects a linear PM-spec/design/engineering handoff in favor of joint exploration of taste, latency, model boundaries and generated units; this informs [[AIOrganizationDesign]] and [[AgentFacingInterfaces]]. [[Vanessa]] contrasts [[ByteDanceFLOW]]'s AI exploration with the mature mobile method of finding a benchmark, executing hard and optimizing; [[NonConsensusInnovation]] needs field scanning and independent judgment when benchmarks are scarce. [[musical-ly-ruhe-chengwei-tiktok-pm-yanzhong-de-zijie-chanpin-wenhua-he-quanqiuhua-zhilu-zijie-tiaodong-di-5-ji-ludflvaw7lid-tci9gagbbxobvtz]] [[130-zhang-yueguang-chuangye-liangnian-shouci-fangtan-miaoya-bushi-ai-native-chanpin-liucheng-dao-shangxiawen-sheji-one-way-door-he-yinv-youxi]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- [[Elys]]'s high-dimensional context requires consent and practical benefit, not automatic access to intimate data. [[EVE]]'s design and founder-reported effects do not establish that companions improve users' real relationships. [[135-he-ziran-xuanze-chuangshiren-tristan-liao-elys-saibo-fenshen-linghun-context-de-huoqu-yu-liudong-he-ai-shejiao-wangluo-ltwegwvo7grn-v-rft0txlmqmcty]] [[zhe-keneng-caishi-ai-peiban-zhenzheng-gai-you-de-yangzi-duitan-shuaping-chanpin-eve-chuangshiren-tristan-lgvcb1tuur-1rf2qk8jv9chmwew]]
- [[ByteDanceFLOW]] is a contrast in an interview, not evidence that benchmark-driven execution never works. A fixed workflow can remain valuable when predictable output is the job. [[musical-ly-ruhe-chengwei-tiktok-pm-yanzhong-de-zijie-chanpin-wenhua-he-quanqiuhua-zhilu-zijie-tiaodong-di-5-ji-ludflvaw7lid-tci9gagbbxobvtz]] [[130-zhang-yueguang-chuangye-liangnian-shouci-fangtan-miaoya-bushi-ai-native-chanpin-liucheng-dao-shangxiawen-sheji-one-way-door-he-yinv-youxi]]

## What Changed
- The product criterion shifted from AI capability to context, editable output and user control.
- Social and companion examples extend the design question beyond fixed screens without proving the proposed network or relationship outcomes.
