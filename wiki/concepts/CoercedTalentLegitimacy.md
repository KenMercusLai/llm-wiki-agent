# Coerced Talent Legitimacy / 胁迫征士合法性

Coerced talent legitimacy / 胁迫征士合法性 is the pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-949-lishishang-de-dongzhuo-daodi-duo-canbao-lk6idogwmc54nk1sdfc5wonyvgbb|Hanji 949]] builds from [[DongZhuo|董卓]] after he receives xiangguo privileges. Dong Zhuo knows he needs respected figures, cleaner appointments, and a more legitimate-looking court, but his power rests on soldiers terrorizing [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] and killing or punishing opponents.

The source's recruitment branch names [[ZhouBiLateHan|周毖]], [[WuQiongLateHan|伍琼]], [[ZhengTaiLateHan|郑泰]], and [[HeYongLateHan|何颙]] as figures involved in cleaning corrupt officials and recommending worthies. It then tracks [[XunShuangLateHan|荀爽]]'s rapid elevation, [[ChenJiLateHan|陈纪]]'s appointment, and [[HanRongLateHan|韩融]]'s appointment as visible scholar-prestige gains for Dong Zhuo.

The concept's limit appears through [[ShentuPanLateHan|申屠蟠]]. Because the recruitment is backed by fear rather than trustworthy recognition, refusal becomes possible as a moral exposure of the system. Shentu Pan's noncompliance shows that coercion can place names in offices, but cannot make those offices prove willing elite support.

This differs from [[TalentAttractionThroughRespect|礼贤下士式人才吸附]]. Respect-based attraction depends on the recruited person's ability to refuse without being treated as an enemy. Coerced talent legitimacy keeps the external shape of recruitment while hollowing out the consent and dignity that make recruited talent politically persuasive.

## Key Claims

- Violent powerholders may seek scholar prestige precisely because force alone does not create stable legitimacy.
- Rapid promotion can function as political packaging when appointments are used to signal elite support faster than trust can form.
- Coercive recruitment can staff offices, but it weakens the evidentiary value of those offices because observers can read compliance as fear.
- Refusal by a respected figure can expose the gap between formal summons and genuine legitimacy.
- The pattern sits between [[CourtOfficeCapture|朝廷开府任官失序]] and [[TalentAttractionThroughRespect|礼贤下士式人才吸附]]: it uses court appointments, but fails the non-coercive talent-attraction test.
