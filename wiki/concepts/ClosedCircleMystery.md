# 暴风雪山庄 / Closed-Circle Mystery

## Definition
暴风雪山庄 / closed-circle mystery confines the likely suspects and the flow of information within a bounded setting. A storm is only one possible boundary; a flight, a blocked exit, a secretive institution or even a folk-tale island can play the part.

## Current Synthesis
In [[QuanYuanXianYiRen|《全员嫌疑人》]] and [[QuanYuanMingZhenTan|《全员名侦探》]], enclosure creates a finite, playable suspect game: the snowbound inn, dark underground gallery, transoceanic flight and gang headquarters require no common weather. [[WatsonPower|华生力]] makes bystanders temporary detectives, allowing mistaken theories to test clues before a reversal. [[MukashiMukashiAruTokoroNiShitaiGaArimashita|《很久很久以前，在某个地方有一具尸体》]] briefly places 桃太郎's鬼岛 within a similar frame, while its more detailed 一寸法师 and 猿蟹合战 examples depend chiefly on consistent magical rules, not necessarily a closed suspect pool. [[TheNameOfTheRose|《玫瑰的名字》]] uses a bounded monastery differently: the forbidden library and controlled access to books constrain evidence and interpretation. Its first episode's apparent [[BookOfRevelation|《启示录》]] pattern is qualified by the second episode's account of overreading; a closed setting does not guarantee a tidy symbolic design.

## Key Claims
- Physical and social boundaries can make a suspect pool finite without literal snow or a villa.
- In a light puzzle mystery, finite suspects and shared constraints let multiple provisional explanations compete; [[WatsonPower|华生力]] distributes deduction beyond a single detective.
- Folk-tale geography can supply enclosure, but rule-based magical puzzles do not automatically satisfy strict closed-circle criteria.
- A closed institution can restrict evidence and interpretive authority as well as movement; apparent symbolic order can be a false solution.

## Evidence
### Finite puzzle and distributed deduction
- [[158-quanyuan-mingzhentan-tuili-shupian-lai-yibao-929377122]] describes four bounded situations in the two 全员 books, seven stories and a wrapper mystery in 《全员嫌疑人》, and 小何's temporary-detective effect. The host calls the playful format [[PuzzleSnackMystery|推理薯片]] and accepts its deliberate implausibility, provided the clues earn the reveal.
### Folk-tale boundary and rule consistency
- [[99-henjiu-henjiu-yiqian-yaoguai-ye-tuili-a-730851220]] only briefly describes sequential monster deaths on 桃太郎's鬼岛 as closed-circle-like. Its fuller 一寸法师 example uses body size, an ogre's stomach and a magic mallet for an alibi/half-locked room; 猿蟹合战 uses role swaps and animal transformation for identity logic.
### Monastery and contested meaning
- [[53-meigui-de-mingzi-shang-zhen-yu-jia-zheng-yu-xie-quanshi-yu-guodu-quanshi-636490184]] places William and Adso in a 1327 monastery where library catalogues, architecture and gatekeeping limit what investigators can know; the initial deaths invite an apocalyptic reading.
- [[54-meigui-de-mingzi-xia-zhen-yu-jia-zheng-yu-xie-quanshi-yu-guodu-quanshi-638214699]] revises that reading: William's apparently coherent Revelation sequence does not explain every death. The enclosure exposes [[KnowledgeMonopoly|知识垄断]] and [[InterpretationAndOverinterpretation|过度诠释]], rather than ensuring fair-play neatness.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- The Eco novel's theological politics and semiotics are not interchangeable with a lightweight finite-suspect game; the first episode stops before the solution, so its proposed pattern is not a confirmed cause.
- The folk-tale episode summarizes 桃太郎 briefly; do not classify every tale it discusses as a strict closed-circle mystery.
- A playable puzzle is an artificial literary convention, not a model of ordinary police investigation.

## What Changed
- Integrated puzzle, folk-tale and monastery examples by boundary mechanism; explicitly corrected the first Rose episode's apparent Revelation solution with its sequel.
