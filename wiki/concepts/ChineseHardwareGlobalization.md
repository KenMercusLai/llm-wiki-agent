# Chinese Hardware Globalization

## Definition
Chinese hardware globalization is the route from domestic engineering and manufacturing capabilities to overseas demand, trust, localized operations and compliance; the route differs by product category.

## Current Synthesis
[[DJI]]'s consumer-drone and creator-camera brand, [[BYD]]'s [[Brazil]]/[[Japan]] vehicle adaptation, [[Hisense]] and [[Lenovo]]'s sports demonstrations, and the prospective [[BafangElectric|Bafang]] e-bike component shift show different paths. Domestic battery process strength is a foundation, not by itself proof of overseas sales. Category leadership, industrial protection and trust cannot be collapsed into one universal success formula.

## Key Claims
- Defining a technical consumer category can build pricing power and overseas brand recognition, but later security rules can close an early opening.
- Strategic vehicles need market-specific fuel, size, parts and production decisions rather than direct export of a domestic design.
- Technical sponsorship can demonstrate reliability to foreign buyers when the participating companies already have overseas businesses.
- Supplier depth and process yield are prerequisites for some hardware stacks but do not establish overseas adoption on their own.
- E-bike electrification presents a prospective component-stack opening, not an achieved Chinese global takeover.

## Evidence
- [[140-da-jiang-hai-neng-di-kong-fei-duo-jiu-feng-tou-quan-1-140-1]] credits DJI's drones and [[PortableCreatorCameras|Pocket-style cameras]] with category-definition and price power; hosts claim more than 80% overseas revenue, while describing U.S. certification/security restrictions and [[LowAltitudeRegulatoryRisk|civil-military risk]]. Their smaller-category-versus-EV protection contrast is an interpretation, not a law.
- [[fuzhuang-pinpai-a-f-xunzhao-zhongguo-hezuo-huoban-fufei-tiqian-kan-telangpu-tiewen-fuwu-shangxian-1004810677]] reports BYD's Chinese-Brazilian flex-fuel PHEV co-development and a target exceeding 50% local parts by 2027; its Japan-specific compact model reportedly had more than 700 early orders by August 3, 2026. These are launch and target measures, not evidence of lasting market share. [[GlobalProductLocalization]] and [[JapanMarketEntryTrust]] describe the two adaptation constraints.
- [[vol-264-ba-shijiebei-zuowei-fangfa-999416048]] describes Hisense's progression from naming exposure to VAR displays and Lenovo's FIFA global technology partnership for AI, visualization and media/team analysis at the 2026 [[FIFAWorldCup]]. [[GlobalSportsSponsorship]] can show products, not prove subsequent sales.
- [[e229-cong-shougong-zuofang-dao-quanqiu-diyi-zhongguo-dongli-dianchi-nixi-shi]] attributes Chinese battery capacity to 2009 [[TenCitiesThousandVehicles|十城千辆]] demand, the 2015 [[PowerBatteryWhitelist|白名单]], [[LocalBatteryIndustrialClusters|local clusters]] in [[Changzhou]] and [[Yibin]], cell yield, coating/moisture control and [[PowerBatteryIndustryChain|materials-to-pack integration]]. The [[Northvolt]] comparison concerns manufacturing execution; it does not verify [[CATL]] adoption abroad.
- [[no-199-zixingche-200-nian-973489465]] contrasts [[Shimano]]'s cold-forging, drivetrain and patents with assemblers, then suggests motors, batteries, sensors, software and service could shift value toward [[BafangElectric]] in [[EBikeValueChainShift|electric bikes]]. This is a prospective route; the note supports channel and after-sales trust as hurdles, but does not substantiate the old page's specific European trade-measure assertion.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- Source figures, especially DJI earnings/revenue mix and BYD orders, are attributed podcast or reported claims, not independently audited here. Policy exposure varies across drones, cars, batteries and bicycles.
- Domestic production scale cannot substitute for foreign certification, [[TrustAsBusinessAsset|consumer trust]] or service; sponsorship and early orders are not commercial outcomes.

## What Changed
- The brand-led DJI case now sits beside localized automotive launch, technology demonstrations, domestic battery capability and a conditional e-bike component opportunity.
