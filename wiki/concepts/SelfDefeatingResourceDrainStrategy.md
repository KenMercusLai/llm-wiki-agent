# Self-Defeating Resource-Drain Strategy / 疲敌反强敌

Self-defeating resource-drain strategy / 疲敌反强敌 is the pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-104-yinmou-shisuan-rang-qinguo-bantu-you-you-you-pengzhang-le-lgnyhbduqutegtksrjwcvhmn8-t6]] extracts from [[HanState|韩国]]'s attempt to exhaust [[QinState|秦国]] through the [[ZhengGuoCanal|郑国渠]]. Han's plan is rational in the short term: make Qin commit labor, money, and years to an enormous project instead of attacking east.

The backfire comes from the project's real usefulness. Because [[LuBuwei|吕不韦]] already wants Guanzhong irrigation and because [[ZhengGuoHydraulicEngineer|郑国]]'s engineering plan can actually increase Qin's grain base, the cost is not pure waste. Once the sunk cost is near completion, abandoning the project would hurt Qin more than finishing it, so the attempted delay becomes long-term Qin state capacity.

## Key Claims
- A drain strategy can fail when the imposed burden is also durable infrastructure.
- The weaker state's time-buying goal can conflict with the stronger state's long-term capacity-building goal.
- Exposure does not automatically end the plan if the material project has become valuable enough to finish.
- The pattern is close to [[YinmouYangmouConversion|阴谋阳谋转换]] because the hidden motive and open utility of the same act diverge.
