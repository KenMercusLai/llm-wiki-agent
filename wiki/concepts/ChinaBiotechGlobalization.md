## Definition
China biotech globalization is the attempt to capture more of an innovative drug's worldwide value through clinical development, registration and commercialization rather than selling most overseas rights through licensing.

## Current Synthesis
The registered episodes describe a ladder, not a binary choice: license-out or overseas NewCo, shared global development costs and profits, or a capital-intensive international organization. [[BeiGene|百济神州]] is the recurring advanced example, yet the speakers explicitly say China does not already have a fully fledged global pharmaceutical MNC under their strict definition.

## Key Claims
- Licensing provides near-term cash and external validation but can cap later upside and control.
- Global commercialization requires organizational capacity across clinical trials, registration, reimbursement, sales and lifecycle management, not merely a good molecule.
- Joint global development can bridge licensing and full self-commercialization while retaining risk and cost.
- Clinical outcomes and executed milestones must test deal headlines before globalization is called proven.
- Overseas localization of manufacturing, supply chains and R&D is more demanding than exporting from China.

## Evidence
- Strategy and value capture: [[vol-117-shengwu-yiyao-de-2025-chaodi-zhongguo-yanfa-jiaolv-he-xinwang-jiwei-lmhral0rmq6tohiqdwsgmfapnyn7]] contrasts [[BiotechLicenseOutArbitrage|license-out]] and [[ReverseNewCoBiotechModel]] with [[BeiGene]]'s product-backed global sales effort; it cautions that an upfront payment may trade away upside and affect IPO incentives. This is a source-dated 2025 assessment, not a general ban on licensing.
- Organizational ladder: [[130-shui-cai-shi-shengwu-yiyao-hangye-fazhan-de-hexin-qudongli-jiantan-chuangxinyao-chanyelian-de-jiaose-fengong-lnau0sacx1jm1t-2locdc5nszakh]] defines [[BioTechToBioPharmTransition]] by clinical development, approval, launch, reimbursement and sales, then [[GlobalPharmaCommercialization]] by worldwide registration, portfolio, indication expansion, M&A and BD. Smaller teams can use CRO/CDMO/CSO outsourcing, but trial design and enrollment through [[ClinicalDevelopmentCapability]] still shape value capture; BeiGene is among the closest Chinese cases, not a complete MNC.
- Shared risk: [[156-shengwu-yiyao-de-2026-dang-shichang-bu-zai-wei-bd-zaodong-zhongguo-yaoqi-de-xingchen-dahai-cai-ganggang-zhankai-lil-ugrzq8uvzviq3f8i-wm9ilup]] describes the [[InnoventBiologics]]–[[TakedaPharmaceutical]] PD-1/IL-2 bispecific partnership as global cost and profit sharing. 2025 BD headlines, including [[SanShengPharma]]–[[Pfizer]] and [[HengruiPharma]]–[[GSK]], are not the same as realized global profits; the episode calls 2026 a [[BiotechBDDataValidationPhase]] for clinical readouts and milestone execution.
- Clinical proof: [[156-shengwu-yiyao-de-2026-dang-shichang-bu-zai-wei-bd-zaodong-zhongguo-yaoqi-de-xingchen-dahai-cai-ganggang-zhankai-lil-ugrzq8uvzviq3f8i-wm9ilup]] uses [[Akeso]]–[[SummitTherapeutics]]' PD-1/VEGF result to distinguish promising progression-free survival from still-uncertain overall survival, and links larger deals to rebuilding clinical-data trust after enrollment/CRO concerns. This checks the asset thesis in [[vol-117-shengwu-yiyao-de-2025-chaodi-zhongguo-yanfa-jiaolv-he-xinwang-jiwei-lmhral0rmq6tohiqdwsgmfapnyn7]] against outcomes rather than transaction value alone.
- Local execution: [[165-nianbaoji-zhong-de-zhenshi-zhongguo-2026-lpredevu-gakn92dwutmulytmslo]] reports BeiGene's global clinical capability and argues that an overseas factory alone is insufficient: manufacturing, supplier clusters and R&D must become locally embedded under security-first supply chains. Its annual-report discussion also notes a [[ChinaInnovationTransparencyGap]] in communicating Chinese innovation abroad.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
These are related podcast perspectives and investment-industry interpretations, not independent proof of any company's eventual success. Capital intensity, payer acceptance, trial quality and local compliance can reverse an attractive deal's economics. The old page's specific CAR-T local-government manufacturing/service-fee claim cannot be corroborated in the registered 165 Source Note; it is not retained as evidence.

## What Changed
- Replaced a single self-commercialization contrast with a staged licensing, partnership, clinical-validation and localization model.
