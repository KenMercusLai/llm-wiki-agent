# Chronicle Source Gaps / 编年史料空缺

## Definition
Chronicle source gaps / 编年史料空缺 are absent, terse or disconnected notices in a historical annal that leave events, identities or causal sequences underdetermined.

## Current Synthesis
Blank years and compact war entries in [[SimaGuang|司马光]]’s [[ZizhiTongjian|《资治通鉴》]] require visible uncertainty. Other accounts can supplement a missing record, but their date, provenance and conjectural status remain separate. A present but misplaced notice belongs under [[ChronicleChronologyDrift|chronology drift]], not the same category as silence.

## Key Claims
- An empty annal year is evidence of the archive's limits, not proof of peace or compiler negligence.
- A parallel biography or history can fill narrative space only as a labeled supplement, not as an invisible continuation of the annal.
- Terse warfare entries demonstrate activity without reliably supplying motive, alliance boundaries or outcome.
- A compressed conquest notice can name participants and result yet omit the identity and causal link needed to explain a reversal.

## Evidence
- [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-07-liangqi-cisha-2-lhngst3xsnyjrmzh1cvheocvvsqm]] calls 395 BCE blank while describing [[NieZheng|聂政]] and [[XiaLei|侠累]] through variant [[Zhanguoce|《战国策》]] and annal accounts; [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-09-zhanguo-shidai-de-shijie-dazhan-losmum91vjpqfymywelj60o7-vbl]] groups the 380–379 BCE Qi/Yan/Han/[[ZhaoState|Zhao]]/[[WeiState|Wei]]/Chu fighting but says no clean single campaign or winner can be reconstructed. These caution against smoothing a jagged [[EarlyWarringStatesInterstateWar|war sequence]].
- [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-2-lmnqfjmydygqnfec6xhzpixralvi]] marks 370s [[QiWeiwang|Qi Wei Wang]] court chronology as possibly misplaced, a different issue from missing entries. [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-10-yi-jiazu-danwei-you-mayi-chi-daxiang-de-jingshen-1-lq3t7lwpmq1oyb8zmmmn2tywnu8d]] finds 374 BCE blank under [[ZhouLiewang|King Lie]] in the chronicle and explicitly borrows [[Shiji|《史记》]]'s [[TaishiDan|太史旦]] visit to [[QinState|Qin]] under [[QinXianGong|Duke Xian]]; the [[KinshipLegitimacyDiplomacy|shared-origin appeal]] is the host’s political reading, and a possible identification with [[Laozi|老子]] is speculation rather than a recovered annal fact.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-11-shijie-fengqi-yunyong-1-lt60b2gddiosf5kade35uopwohi5]] notes no chronicle entry for 367 BCE under [[ZhouXianwang|King Xian]] and a skipped 363 BCE despite a separate [[QinEastwardPressure|Qin eastward attack]] on Wei's [[Shaoliang|少梁]] and Zhao aid; [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-08-yidai-mingjiang-wuqi-cansi-3-ltca-ikuzo9ej7a0lfrgwv2-wulb]] notes 384 BCE blank under [[ZhouAnWang|King An]] and a terse 383 BCE Wei victory at Tutai without motive or aftermath. Repetition of gaps does not license a continuous war story.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-118-1-wangjian-lv-liushiwan-dajun-duizhan-xiangyan-haoxi-kaichang-le-lsf2puinwg0m3bgyi25znof7aznu]] observes that the chronicle compresses [[WangJianQin|王翦]] and [[MengWu|蒙武]] capturing [[ChuFuchu|楚王负刍]] with [[ChangpingJun|昌平君]]'s death. [[LiKaiyuan|李开元]]'s [[QinMi|《秦谜》]] reconstruction identifies him as Xiong Qi and links his rebellion to [[LiXinQin|李信]]'s failed Chu campaign; the episode presents this as a distinct interpretive layer, not information contained in the terse notice.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- The seven notes are one show's interpretation of transmitted histories, not seven independent archival witnesses. Even if an adjacent event is known, the omitted year's full course cannot be inferred from it; alternate source traditions may contradict each other.

## What Changed
- The early blank-year diagnosis now includes a late-Chu identity-and-causation gap and explicitly separates a speculative book reconstruction from the chronicle notice.

## Related Concepts
