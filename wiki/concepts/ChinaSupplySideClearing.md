# China Supply-Side Clearing

## Definition
China supply-side clearing is a proposed change in industrial capacity and competitive structure so that rising output can translate into sustainable prices, cash flow, and company earnings rather than ever-thinner margins.

## Current Synthesis
[[MouYiling|牟一凌]] contrasts electricity use and production growth since 2022–23 with weak PPI and corporate profits. The policy case for curbing “内卷式恶性竞争” is real in the cited discussions, but weak-firm exit, price recovery, and durable shareholder returns remain hypotheses to test, not completed results. [[ChinaDeflationDemandRepair|Demand and income repair]] is necessary alongside supply change.

## Key Claims
- Activity and shareholder value can diverge when production expands faster than pricing power.
- Tail-firm exit and less destructive competition are proposed supply-side remedies, not evidence that a clearing has occurred.
- The issue extends beyond firms when strategic-sector price wars interact with local incentives and overseas trade backlash.
- Market rerating can precede PPI, cash-flow and earnings confirmation; supply reform without household demand repair is incomplete.

## Evidence
- In [[vol-112-yici-feigongshi-de-2024-fansi-he-2025-zhanwang-duihua-lanxiaokang-x-mouyiling-lkasjtodqgmnbpyambqoxl-fcsyv]], Mou distinguishes national production capacity from listed-company earnings and describes electricity/PPI divergence since 2022–23: stronger volume with falling prices need not reward shareholders. This is the company-value question behind [[NewOrderAssetPricing]].
- [[vol-111-guanyu-2025-nian-de-si-ge-caixiang-lgaira5qw1fhic4qwihrimed6q9g]] identifies medical procurement, EVs, batteries and photovoltaics as late-2024 examples where local industrial support and price pressure meet overseas anxiety; its [[Northvolt]] comparison concerns strategic competition, not proof that Chinese firms have exited.
- [[vol-115-quanqiu-hongguan-he-ziben-shichang-2025-zhanwang-duanqi-wenti-bu-jiejue-jiu-meiyou-zhongqi-he-changqi-le-lrfl2e5nuf7-ubq-shybqytjhuhx]] separates the 2024 September policy turn and 2025 valuation stage from leading-profit signals and eventual PPI/earnings recovery. Its [[AHShare2025Barbell]] pairs cash-flow dividends with future [[NewQualityProductiveForces]] exposure rather than declaring an industrial turnaround.
- [[153-quanqiu-hongguan-he-ziben-shichang-2026-zhanwang-da-nian-zhihou-reng-shi-da-nian-lupeqjdszon-wp5zdq06w3ustw2d]] reports the 2025 asset rally beside property, employment and consumption weakness; its January 2026 outlook argues anti-involution and capacity clearing cannot by themselves end deflation without income, confidence and effective demand. [[ShortTermDemandBeforeLongTermReform]] and [[LaborShareConsumptionRebalancing]] explain the transmission gap.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- All four notes are related podcast perspectives and dated views, not independent measures of industry exit or policy effects. Neither demand stimulus alone nor supply clearing alone is presented as sufficient.
- [[PolicyDrivenMarketRally]] and [[AShareValuationIndicators]] can register expectation before fundamentals. [[ValueTrap]] and [[GoodCompanyGoodStock]] distinguish apparent cheapness and company quality from investable returns; no return promise follows from this framework.

## What Changed
- The earlier production-versus-profit diagnosis is now paired with a demand-side condition, rather than treating forced exit as a self-sufficient cure.
- The 2025–26 market readings distinguish sentiment and valuation recovery from PPI and earnings confirmation.
