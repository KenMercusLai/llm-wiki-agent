# Good Intentions Political Limits

## Definition
Good intentions political limits names the gap between ethical purpose and executable, accountable public action; it does not mean that kindness or restraint are political errors.

## Current Synthesis
The bounded accounts range from ancient court and military episodes to development intervention and corporate complicity. They point to institutions, incentives, executors and moral limits in different settings, not a universal historical law that benevolence fails.

## Key Claims
- Private kindness needs boundaries and public care needs durable institutions, rather than episodic gestures alone.
- Humane military aims need trained execution, legitimate command and realistic constraints.
- Advice and conquest must be judged partly by the character of those empowered to execute them and by civilian costs.
- Moralized reform and outside improvement can fail when capacity, local knowledge or feedback are missing.
- Realism cannot absolve opportunistic complicity or abolish the moral claims of ordinary people.

## Evidence
- Personal and institutional care: Hanji 919's opening argues that kindness without boundaries can become exploitation; its subsequent case of Emperor Ling's proposed garden shows attendants citing Mencian shared pleasure while ignoring displaced farmers, against Yang Ci's warning. Mencius's '惠而不知为政' response to Zi Chan personally ferrying people across the Zhen and Wei rivers instead demands reliable bridges and access. Tian Dan's gift of a fur robe to a freezing elder by the Zi River was read by Qi Xiang Wang as rival popular credit; a pearl artisan suggested the ruler publicly own the care, but later courtiers still turned Diao Bo's Chu mission into suspicion and ritual humiliation. - Command: the Hanji 936 host treats Zhang Wen's hesitation over Sun Jian's proposed execution of Dong Zhuo as understandable because Dong Zhuo had western prestige and no uncontestable capital charge; the risk was public defiance weakening discipline, not a universal duty to kill. In Hanji 972, Liu Yu ordered no burning and only Gongsun Zan killed, but his loose force could not execute that narrow siege objective; the episode contrasts Tian Chou's later law, rites, schools and Wuhuan relations as humane authority given local form. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-936-luanshi-xian-sha-shengmu-xin-zhehua-dui-ma-lml-kuyc7yys2ijh2kt46pwnugar]] [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-972-sanguo-liuyu-zhisi-taisha-taitianzhen-lvawrcandbh628rckukosgarh7v2]]
- Consequences and executors: Jia Xu advised Dong Zhuo's remnant commanders Li Jue and Guo Si to counterattack Chang'an rather than disband; the source recounts their plunder and mass deaths, while his projected intent, remorse and later attempts to help Emperor Xian remain the host's interpretation. Song Taizu's '先南后北' was a logistical/financial choice, yet Wang Quanbin's Shu campaign inflicted plunder and resistance; the founding move to Kaifeng used canals and southern grain but increased defensive demands, while military centralization reduced warlord threats at a long-run frontier cost in the hosts' reading. - Reform and field intervention: the discussion of [[WangMang|Wang Mang]] describes moral reputation and omens as political capital, then Wang fields, monopolies, taxes, repeated currency changes and frontier symbolism outrunning administration and diplomacy amid inflation, disaster and revolt. The reading of Nigel Barley's 1977–79 Dowayo fieldwork in Cameroon reports that German cotton promotion, vegetable gardens and water projects could reshape food prices, resources, belief or apparent success; translators, missionaries and the observer's own help complicate confident inference. - Conscience under strategic pressure: the Mencius reading defends '民为贵' and resistance to tyrants while treating Qi's failed Yan campaign as a case where righteous self-description did not ensure disciplined governance. The [[ThePrince|《君主论》]] episode separates how power operates from endorsement of cruelty and contextualizes Machiavelli's republican commitments and Cesare Borgia reading; it closes with the ethical rule '不到万不得已绝不作恶' (do not do evil unless absolutely necessary). The IBM–Dehomag episode alleges that customized punched-card administration assisted Nazi classification and persecution, contrasting Jacobus Lentz with Rene Carmille; its claim that moral rules protect the weak rebuts using political 'realism' to excuse collaboration. ## Counterevidence & Qualifications
These are heterogeneous podcast interpretations, not independent proof of a common causal law. The late-Han host's warnings do not prove a capital offense for Dong Zhuo or Jia Xu's inner motives. Song founding stories and the contested succession record require source criticism; the IBM–Nazi moral stakes are not equivalent to routine court tradeoffs. Mencian resistance, Zi Chan's care and Tian Chou's settlement show why effective goodness, not cynicism, is the goal.

## What Changed
- Consolidated personal boundaries, public works, military execution, reform feedback and principled restraint rather than thirteen successive source additions.
- Preserved the legal hesitation, civilian harm and moral counterexamples instead of equating realism with cruelty.
