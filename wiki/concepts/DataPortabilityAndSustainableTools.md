# Data Portability And Sustainable Tools

## Definition
Data portability and sustainable tools combine a credible maintenance model with the ability to retrieve and reuse personal data if an app, device or provider changes.

## Current Synthesis
Payment may support upkeep, but price alone does not guarantee continuity. Exportable notes, multiple backup layers and longitudinal health records all preserve future options; each has different privacy, storage and professional-judgment constraints.

## Key Claims
- A tool earns trust through usable export and maintenance as well as features; paid AI inference still needs visible user value.
- Portable transcripts and reading notes can be repurposed by agents without binding a knowledge workflow to one app.
- Local storage, cloud and discounted net-disk plans have different recurring costs and failure modes; a sole cheap copy is fragile.
- Longitudinal health records may support trend interpretation, but cross-provider compatibility or AI diagnosis is not demonstrated by saving files.

## Evidence
- **Product sustainability:** [[KejiLuandun]] contrasts ad-subsidized free software with per-query inference costs, using [[NiDeShufang]] as a small-tool example of product focus and portability; rumored [[Doubao]] subscription pricing does not itself prove users will pay or that a developer can maintain the service indefinitely. [[cong-qq-huiyuan-dao-doubao-baoyue-zhongguoren-weishenme-zong-juede-ruanjian-gai-mianfei-keji-luandun]]
- **Reusable knowledge:** The [[YingdiHaike]] hosts suggest choosing [[AISkills]] for repeated, verifiable work, exporting [[Podwise]] transcripts and [[WeChatReading]] notes into scheduled [[RoutineAgentAutomation]] rather than leaving them in an app. The source is a workflow proposal, not a measured productivity gain. [[ep127-cong-skills-dao-zidonghua-gongzuoliu-lun-agent-ruhe-jieguan-zhenshi-shengchanli-lntwhoxpi433ptke-nhohb-5lbpz]]
- **Copy and cost accounting:** In one host's example, about 100 TB of cloud storage cost nearly 10,000 RMB monthly, prompting reuse of a NAS, five 26 TB drives and a UPS over a three-year comparison; a 115 40 TB/eight-year plan was expressly one backup layer, not a trusted sole archive. Physical media and UPS add maintenance responsibility. [[zhe-bannian-women-you-maile-naxie-keji-haowu-1]]
- **Health continuity:** [[JiangXun]] argues that years of lab reports, blood pressure, sleep, oxygen and wearable records can surface trends such as a changing thyroid-value slope within the normal range. He restricts AI to context and questions: treatment and prescriptions remain doctors' responsibility, and the note does not establish cross-hospital interoperability. [[ba-shenti-shuju-cunqilai-keneng-shi-putongren-zui-huasuan-de-ai-touzi-1]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Three of these sources are the same show and are proposals or personal cases rather than cross-domain efficacy trials. Local copies may be lost; cloud services may close; health records contain sensitive information and trends do not diagnose disease. Subscription revenue does not prove uptime, while the 115 plan is not a durability guarantee. Agent reuse depends on permissions, formats and validation.

## What Changed
- Organized tool trust, agent-reusable knowledge, backup economics and health continuity as related but distinct portability cases.
- Separated data preservation from proven AI medical or commercial outcomes.
