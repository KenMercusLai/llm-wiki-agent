# Defeated Population Conversion / 降众资源化

Defeated population conversion / 降众资源化 is the statecraft pattern [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-968-jiemi-sanguo-zui-lihai-de-yangmou-lg4qfkdgw-stixrebhjdoshil6yj|Hanji 968]] draws from [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s treatment of the surrendered Qingzhou Yellow Turban forces. The defeated group is not treated only as captives, enemies, or a food burden. It becomes differentiated capacity: selected fighters become [[QingzhouSoldiers|青州兵]], while the wider dependent population is tied to farming, supplies, and early [[TuntianSystem|屯田制]] logic.

The concept is narrower than generic surrender. [[PoliticalSurrenderCascade|招降示范级联]] asks how one surrender encourages another; [[CasualtySparingSurrender|免杀式纳降]] asks how killing can be avoided or strategically limited. Defeated population conversion asks whether a ruler can reorganize surrendered people into a durable military and productive base.

In Hanji 968, the mechanism matters because it gives [[MaoJie|毛玠]]'s advice material traction. "奉天子" can create legitimacy and attract talent, but a legitimacy platform without food and soldiers is hollow. Population conversion links manpower, farming, supply, and public order so that Cao Cao's later political title strategy has an operating base.

## Key Claims
- A surrendered population can be a strategic asset if it is sorted into military, agricultural, and dependent-support roles.
- Conversion reduces the immediate danger of mobile armed refugees while increasing the receiving regime's manpower and food base.
- The pattern depends on administration: tools, land, supply allocation, discipline, and command structure must turn people into capacity.
- In Cao Cao's case, the episode makes population conversion the material partner to legitimacy strategy rather than a separate humanitarian gesture.
