# Financial Statement Analysis

## Definition
Financial statement analysis reads profit, financial position and cash movements together to test a company's operating account, rather than treating reported earnings or share price as a complete verdict.

## Current Synthesis
The sources combine an ordinary-investor primer, a fund manager's close-reading practice, an Adidas turnaround and one host's cross-company reading of Chinese annual reports. They offer different uses of filings—not an independently checked set of primary company statements.

## Key Claims
- The income statement's profit, balance sheet's assets and obligations, and cash-flow statement's actual receipts must be reconciled.
- Metrics acquire meaning from the business model: margin, capital expenditure, leverage and ROE cannot be compared mechanically across asset-light and capital-intensive firms.
- Receivables, inventory, cash conversion and auditor signals make reports more useful as a defensive screen than a price-prediction machine.
- Close reconstruction of reports tests a value thesis and its cash-flow durability; AI can organize evidence without replacing the analyst's judgment.
- Comparing many firms' reports can reveal sector-level stress and adaptation, while a turnaround's stock price can move ahead of its reported repair.

## Evidence
- Three-statement reconciliation: [[ep86-mianzi-dizi-rizi-caibao-zhi-jiang-zhe-san-jian-shi-lukmnlkkuxvrvv12frosd9teg7bj]] calls the income statement “面子”, balance sheet “底子” and cash flow “日子”: revenue and accounting profit must be checked against collections, financing and capital spending. [[ProfitAndCashFlowQuality]] captures the difference between earning and receiving cash.
- Business-model interpretation: [[ep86-mianzi-dizi-rizi-caibao-zhi-jiang-zhe-san-jian-shi-lukmnlkkuxvrvv12frosd9teg7bj]] contrasts [[Nvidia]]'s chip design and outsourced fabrication with [[SMIC]]'s fabs, equipment and depreciation; [[AssetLightVsHeavyAssetModels]] changes expectations for margins and investment. [[ReturnOnEquityAnalysis]] can mistake leverage for quality, while [[NonGAAPEarnings]] and different reporting regimes change what a quoted profit includes.
- Defensive checks: [[ep86-mianzi-dizi-rizi-caibao-zhi-jiang-zhe-san-jian-shi-lukmnlkkuxvrvv12frosd9teg7bj]] uses [[SichuanChanghong]] and [[ApexDigital]] to illustrate [[ReceivablesRisk]], [[BestBuy]] and [[Zhangzidao]] for inventory and verification problems, and [[AuditOpinionRisk]] for auditor-change or opinion warnings. These [[AccountingRedFlags]] are prompts for checking collections and valuation, not a finding of misconduct. [[vol-271-a-di-da-si-ruhe-chudi-fantan-1007333155]] instead follows [[Adidas]] after supply-chain delays and the [[Yeezy]] break, reading [[SportswearInventoryCycle]], sellable months, gross margin and free cash flow together with [[RetailInventoryVelocity]] and [[InventoryWriteDownRisk]]. These are episode interpretations, not an independent audit of the companies.
- Research craft: the manager interviewed in [[e160-yige-jiazhi-touzizhe-de-20-nian-huigu-qiu-jifen-qiu-shenglv-qiu-shijian-lixen828sknlujulev9evt37mbuf]] says hand-copying or rebuilding reports makes changes in cash flows and business rhythm harder to overlook; [[ValueInvesting]] requires a plausible [[BusinessMoat]], a realistic lifecycle and [[MarginOfSafety]] rather than low multiples alone. [[AIInvestmentResearch]] can sort and compare figures, but cannot decide whether a cheap firm is a [[ValueTrap]].
- Aggregate and timing uses: [[vol-127-nianbaoji-zhong-de-zhenshi-zhongguo-2025-lqjy2pnfy09zyewj0ljxjuc3gxqv]] has [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] use 2024 [[China]] company reports and management language to discuss weak credit-card, cement and medical-procurement signals, local-government pressure, and REIT rents and occupancy; [[AnnualReportMacroReading]], [[BalanceSheetMacroAnalysis]] and [[ChinaLocalDebtResolution]] are interpretive joins, not a representative census. [[vol-271-a-di-da-si-ruhe-chudi-fantan-1007333155]] cautions that market expectations for Adidas improved before every reported metric did: [[MarketExpectationGap]] is not proof of a completed operating reversal.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- EP86's named-company illustrations and Vol.271's Adidas numbers are podcast reports, not figures independently reconciled to original filings. A standard audit opinion is not a guarantee; accounting warning signs indicate questions, not automatically fraud.
- Vol.127's macro reading is one host's cross-firm selection, not an unbiased economic statistic; company mix, management incentives and sector timing matter. Adidas is one turnaround, not a general timing rule for stock prices.

## What Changed
- Integrated the three-statement primer, model-specific metrics, risk screening, close research, and cross-company/turnaround uses without making a chronology of episode additions.
