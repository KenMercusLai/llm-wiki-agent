## Definition
Currency risk is exposure to changes in exchange value or in the credibility and purchasing power of the unit in which income, assets and future expenses are measured.

## Current Synthesis
A household should start from where and how it expects to spend, not from the headline yield on a foreign asset. The evidence covers a bond converted back to RMB, a foreign-currency insurance promise, overseas brokerage returns and an extreme Argentine inflation case. Exchange-rate volatility, domestic price instability, capital controls and payment access interact but are not identical risks.

## Key Claims
- A foreign asset's local-currency return can shrink or reverse when translated into the household's spending currency.
- Future residence, education and expenses are a better starting point for base-currency choice than a short-term FX prediction.
- Foreign-currency insurance adds payout, contribution, dividend and liquidity mismatch when family needs remain domestic.
- A currency's credit and settlement network can lose trust even without an immediate bilateral FX transaction.
- Severe inflation makes exchange rates and price adjustments part of household, wage and retailer routines.

## Evidence
- Investing conversion: [[ep39-feng-man-lou-xiaji-quanqiu-shuaitui-manman-bijin-yanfang-sishou-bubu-weiying-manliao-xiabannian-meigu-meizhai-huilv-lgz718un4sjvgxqbydfikyxz0hcj]] warns that a dollar deposit or [[USTreasury|U.S. Treasury]] yield can be offset by RMB appreciation; duration, fiscal financing and [[FederalReserve|Fed]] rate-cut uncertainty remain separate bond risks. Scarce [[QDIIAllocation|QDII]] quota is not an attractive entry price. In that episode, [[PeoplesBankOfChina|the PBoC]] is described as preferring RMB stability to either outflow-inducing depreciation or export-harming appreciation; this is the speakers' 2024 policy reading, not a fixed intervention rule. [[ep89-haiwai-quanshang-da-dizhen-kuajing-touzi-xinshidai-li8ya-r5cpz3sifdjby73vh9-rxs]] adds that winning U.S. stocks can look different after RMB/USD conversion, drawdowns and access-route friction; its 2026 brokerage restrictions are a compliance issue, not a mechanism of currency depreciation.
- Base currency: [[176-niuyue-yinian-yige-beiguanzhuyizhe-de-huofa-725-shalong-shilu-lte-k8lw8e1txxw3nfxvo3wal8ah]] has [[DavidWeng|大卫翁]] propose [[BaseCurrencyThinking]] from life location and future payments. His New York live-salon discussion favors diversified options and deliberate delay over casual leveraged FX forecasting; it also mentions REITs, bonds, equities and insurance as possible destinations, not a currency trade prescription.
- Insurance mismatch: [[ep18-dou-shi-huang-quan-yu-yue-ke-baoxian-mai-dui-xin-an-le-lly-wx9zecfmov5cbmufmynlosw]] compares foreign-currency [[OverseasInsuranceRisk|Hong Kong insurance]] illustrations, non-guaranteed dividends, premiums, surrender values and benefits with a family's income, medical, education and retirement needs. Its “人在哪，钱就应该在哪” is a rule of thumb with exceptions for real overseas needs, not a prohibition.
- Unit of account: [[129-huobi-de-benzhi-yiji-huangjin-de-zhenzheng-jiazhi-chuantai-shifen-xiyin-lsjbfttqxf58uk-a4g8-srretkwb]] interprets [[MoneyAsFlow]] through credit, accepted payments and clearing; gold, RMB, dollars and Bitcoin can produce different nominal pictures of the same holdings as confidence changes. [[CurrencyAnchorTransition]] and [[MoneyIllusion]] extend risk beyond one trade's conversion rate.
- Daily instability: [[currency-chaos-in-argentina-summer-school]] depicts [[Argentina]] through [[LucasBabic]]'s shoebox dollars, [[SayaDate]] timing purchases, [[JuanPabloGospinoGomez]]'s nominal wage adjustments and [[NeoTango]]'s repeated shoe repricing. Official/blue and special rates in a [[MultipleExchangeRateRegime]] under [[CapitalControls]] create allocation and corruption concerns; [[MenuCosts]] impair comparison. [[SebastianGaliani]] distinguishes useful [[ExchangeRateFlexibility]] from damaging volatility and opposes treating formal dollarization as an automatic fix.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The three Yi Lao Yong Yi episodes belong to one show cluster and report time-specific market and regulatory judgments. Argentina's prolonged crisis is an extreme counterexample, not a default for stable-currency households; the same source describes the pain of austerity despite slowing inflation. Hedging or holding foreign assets can match genuine overseas liabilities. A Treasury's duration risk and an offshore broker's legality must not be mislabelled as FX risk.

## What Changed
- Distinguished purchasing-power and credibility risk from bilateral exchange conversion.
- Connected insurance and household spending to the same base-currency decision without prescribing an asset.
