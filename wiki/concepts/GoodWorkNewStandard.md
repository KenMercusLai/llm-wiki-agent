# Good Work New Standard / 好工作的新标准

## Definition
A “good job” is a changing combination of income, basic protection, dignity, development, autonomy, and sustainable fit with a person's life; it is not one universal occupational title. The [[ep244-jizhe-yanzhong-de-hao-gongzuo-shenme-yang-lk8iz9cllzx2jou5-sw9yt2ogpyb|reporters' discussion]] poses this as a question, not a new fixed benchmark.

## Current Synthesis
Income and security remain material, especially amid illness, layoffs and care obligations, but a salary alone cannot compensate for loss of bodily or professional agency. The linked conversations test this judgment from within employment, after leaving it, during leave, and under AI-assisted work. All four are episodes of [[TalkSanlian|Talk三联]], not independent labor-market measurements.

## Key Claims
- “Good” can shrink to ordinary rights when wage payment, leave, insurance and freedom from managerial abuse are not reliable baselines.
- Meaningful reporting requires public responsibility and embodied interviews, but ideals can coexist with hierarchy, emotional exhaustion and economic insecurity.
- Leaving a damaging job can restore autonomy without eliminating the need for income, daily structure, family negotiation and a viable route back.
- Paid leave is useful only if handoffs, communications and workplace permission let workers actually disconnect and recover.
- AI-assisted productivity is not an end in itself: management may translate fluency or token use into more workload rather than higher quality, reward or human judgment.

## Evidence
- Baselines and life stage: In EP244, [[WeiQianSanlian|魏茜]], [[LiXiaojie|李小杰]] and [[WuShubin|吴淑彬]] weigh pay against dignity, growth, pain and meaning. One account makes income central to future security under health and redundancy risk; “normal work” already means no wage arrears or abuse, basic pay, annual and sick leave and social insurance. [[YuanChanggeng|袁长庚]] instead asks how a tolerable relation to work can be rebuilt over a longer life. [[ep244-jizhe-yanzhong-de-hao-gongzuo-shenme-yang-lk8iz9cllzx2jou5-sw9yt2ogpyb]]
- Reporting's double edge: The same reporters describe social journalism's public value and its emotional cost when reporters are treated as intruders or traffic seekers. Portals once prized content judgment, whereas algorithmic platforms changed media workers' position; an AI-written story cannot substitute for the felt context, interviews and byline responsibility of field reporting. [[ep244-jizhe-yanzhong-de-hao-gongzuo-shenme-yang-lk8iz9cllzx2jou5-sw9yt2ogpyb]]
- Exit is not an automatic alternative: EP257 distinguishes chosen freelancing and recovery from unemployment and failed re-entry. [[MiaFreelancer|Mia]] gained control over overseas-brand projects after ecommerce marketing, while taking on client acquisition, payment and self-presentation risk; one job seeker reportedly disguised a two-year illness-treatment gap as entrepreneurship. Parents' survival concerns and workers' desire to be seen are not simply opposing moral camps. [[ep257-bu-shangban-yi-chang-taoli-yu-xunzhao-de-fuza-maoxian-li-wflyd48dmxvgersphxfp-gnng]]
- Rest inside work: EP265's “请3休13” example highlights the difference between a calendar slot and usable leave. [[LiJingSanlian|李菁]], [[YanFeiSociologist|颜飞]] and [[LeiTao|雷涛]] describe always-on messaging, shame and insufficient handoff; they argue a longer break may allow attention and rhythm to loosen after several days. The Canada travel segment illustrates sensory and local-life recovery, not proof that any destination cures burnout. [[ep265-dang-qing3xiu13-chongshang-resou-women-ruhe-gei-rensheng-fang-yige-changjia-lsloczlnkmcrrfpn9coh8rrqo-wb]]
- Human scale under AI: EP275 reports first-pass English translation at [[SanlianLifeWeekly|三联生活周刊]] with human checking, and discusses self-funded [[ChatGPT]], [[Claude]] and [[Codex]] use as examples of tool adoption rather than job-quality measures. [[ChenMingxia|陈明霞]] disputes token consumption as a productivity KPI without better quality, revenue or work; fluent AI prose can conceal weak interviewing. Faster tool use can raise assigned tasks without corresponding reward, so judgment, reading, conversation and human connection remain part of evaluating a job. [[ep275-token-tongzhang-shidai-shui-hai-neng-bu-ke-tidai-renzai-zhongliu-tebie-cehua-01-lgnagvell4jyvv7eejrcgno2xjsr]]

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- The speakers do not agree that identity should leave paid work altogether: wages buy real security, and freelancing may trade supervision for market exposure. A failed project need not define a person's worth, but material constraints cannot be solved by boundary-setting alone. [[ep257-bu-shangban-yi-chang-taoli-yu-xunzhao-de-fuza-maoxian-li-wflyd48dmxvgersphxfp-gnng]]
- Short holidays, digital tools and AI have different effects across organizations. The episodes' rest-duration and productivity arguments are reported perspectives, not controlled comparisons; AI adoption also depends on how managers redesign and reward work. [[ep265-dang-qing3xiu13-chongshang-resou-women-ruhe-gei-rensheng-fang-yige-changjia-lsloczlnkmcrrfpn9coh8rrqo-wb]] [[ep275-token-tongzhang-shidai-shui-hai-neng-bu-ke-tidai-renzai-zhongliu-tebie-cehua-01-lgnagvell4jyvv7eejrcgno2xjsr]]

## What Changed
- The benchmark now tests employment, exit, vacation and AI-mediated productivity together rather than treating salary or resignation as the answer.
