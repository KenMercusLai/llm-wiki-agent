# AIJournalismTrust

## Definition
AI journalism trust is confidence that a reported account has traceable sources, an accountable author and editorial verification even when AI assists with finding, drafting or checking material.

## Current Synthesis
Trust depends on both newsroom production and the external evidence supply. Transcription and lead detection can expand reporting; mostly generated articles, synthetic source packages and inaccessible archives may instead weaken perceived effort, attribution or the ability to test claims. A human byline alone is insufficient without reporting practice.

## Key Claims
- Assistance with gathering and reviewing evidence differs from AI drafting the published account.
- Reporter presence, interviews and byline responsibility contribute knowledge and accountability that polished prose cannot replace.
- Synthetic credentials, documents and media raise verification costs even when a reporter writes every word.
- Archive access and historical source checking are necessary for correcting or reconstructing claims.
- Reader trust rests on disclosed process and an identifiable viewpoint, but detection signals are fallible.

[[WillaRemus]] found reviewed AI-drafted stories basic and asked whether additional [[LocalJournalism]] coverage outweighs staff craft and reader concerns; support for lead discovery is not endorsement of undisclosed automated prose. In a Tang-poetry-route reporting episode, journalists found AI useful for arranging routes and materials but unsafe for invented classical quotations, titles, scholars or chronology: site visits, documents and experts must check assertions about [[TangPoetryRoad]]. Former reporters in [[ContentEngineering]] describe sourcing, follow-up and uncertainty as transferable model-evaluation skills; the episode also treats [[AIInteractionInternationalization]] as context-specific cultural re-creation rather than literal translation. Neither model-evaluation nor localization is proof a trained model has performed field reporting. ## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- The Cleveland account offers a real local-news scarcity argument for AI-assisted basic coverage, while journalists dispute whether writing and reporting are separable. Neither position proves all AI-supported stories lose trust. [[tech-20260311-0311-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260311-0311-mp-tech-pod-128]] [[ep244-jizhe-yanzhong-de-hao-gongzuo-shenme-yang-lk8iz9cllzx2jou5-sw9yt2ogpyb]]
- Substack detector scores can falsely accuse human writers; a SynthID indication on a badge does not alone determine every allegation in a story. [[tech-20260810-0810-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260810-0810-mp-tech-pod-128]] [[tech-20260122-0122-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260122-0122-mp-tech-pod-128]]
- Archive-blocking reports identify a feared training route, not a documented instance of that route being exploited. [[tech-20260421-0421-mp-tech-pod-128-tech-20260421-0421-mp-tech-pod-128]]

## What Changed
- Trust is framed jointly through authorship, fieldwork, source authentication and preservation of the public record.
- Creator media and model content-engineering illustrate adjacent practices but do not dissolve editorial responsibility.
