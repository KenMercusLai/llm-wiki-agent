## Definition
Designed agency is the player's sense that actions matter within a system of goals, constraints, feedback, timing and consequences. Neither unlimited generated options nor frequent button presses alone guarantee meaningful control.

## Current Synthesis
[[Xiaoning]] argues that a battle-royale game's shrinking zone can make decisions more meaningful precisely by narrowing them; natural-language freedom and one-prompt game generation still need stable, tested play systems. [[Roi]] describes [[Mujian]]'s [[AISimulationContent]] as “系统收敛，但体验开放”: creators set roles, rules and changing state so a participant receives consequential feedback rather than only more text. These are design proposals and early platform accounts, not established retention outcomes.

[[ZhangYueguang]] traces this bounded relationship-play idea partly to [[Yuanyin]], while [[AIFriendProducts]] alone do not supply a game objective. [[ZhangYueguang]] sees companion play, authored IP and relationship progression as a narrower near-term envelope for [[AIOtomeGames]] than wholly open AI games. A contrasting account of [[MachineGamblingAddiction]] shows why agency must include control over stakes and exit: slot-machine credits, near misses and variable rewards invite repeated micro-actions even when meaningful outcome control is scarce. Structured freedom can enable play or entrap the player depending on incentives and limits.

[[AIInteractiveContentPlatforms]] face a separate creator-and-consumer return problem: if [[CreationAsConsumption]] is fun for the maker, that alone does not prove others will return to play.

## Key Claims
- Constraints and clear stakes can improve decision quality and perceived agency rather than merely reduce choice.
- Player input must change understandable system state and feedback; generated continuation alone is not a satisfying game loop.
- Authored characters and progression can give current AI flexibility a tractable role without claiming full game generation is solved.
- Apparent agency can be manipulative if the interface optimizes repetition while hiding money, risk or exit.
- A playable AI experience still requires testing, tuning and repeatable production beyond a model's text capability.

## Evidence
- Constraining decisions: [[2026-ai-youxi-quanjing-saomiao-si-ceng-tujing-san-da-wuqu-yi-ge-gongshi-quekou-duitan-405-youju-xiaoning-lgk71gytqtsvkc-wipz0hkzkemne]] rejects unlimited choice and uses shrinking-zone stakes as a counterexample to the assumption that more options yield better play.
- Consequential feedback: [[ta-xiang-zao-yi-ge-ai-shidai-de-chaoji-youlechang-duitan-roi-mujian-chuangshiren-ceo-ltfh-on-4qqz-ardqury4g0jejec]] reports Roi’s rules, multi-agent roles and state changes in Mujian, while token costs and creator distribution remain unsettled.
- Bounded authored relationships: [[130-zhang-yueguang-chuangye-liangnian-shouci-fangtan-miaoya-bushi-ai-native-chanpin-liucheng-dao-shangxiawen-sheji-one-way-door-he-yinv-youxi]] reports Zhang’s Yuanyin experience and proposes IP, companionship and character progression as a narrower envelope for AI dialogue.
- Manipulative apparent agency: [[44-yunqi-de-youer-dubo-chengyin-yinwei-women-haipa-ziyou-619995308]] describes gambling credits, near misses, intermittent reinforcement and the machine zone as repeated action without control over outcome or costs.
- Production beyond prompting: [[2026-ai-youxi-quanjing-saomiao-si-ceng-tujing-san-da-wuqu-yi-ge-gongshi-quekou-duitan-405-youju-xiaoning-lgk71gytqtsvkc-wipz0hkzkemne]] discusses multimodal feedback, testing and weak retention in early AI simulations; [[ta-xiang-zao-yi-ge-ai-shidai-de-chaoji-youlechang-duitan-roi-mujian-chuangshiren-ceo-ltfh-on-4qqz-ardqury4g0jejec]] treats Mujian’s operating costs and distribution as open tests, not evidence that general AI game generation is solved.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
The interviews are practitioner interpretations rather than controlled user studies. Token volume need not mean immersion; generated freedom could become more effective as systems mature. The gambling episode treats game loot boxes as an analogy whose legal and cash-out rules differ. Deliberate constraints do not make a game ethically safe without genuine control over costs and exit.

## What Changed
- Distinguished consequential action from both unrestricted prompts and retention-oriented micro-actions.
- Framed companion games as a bounded proposal, not evidence of general AI-game maturity.
