# Defensive Speech Under Tyranny / 暴政下的防御性言说

Defensive speech under tyranny is the [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-956-yongren-he-zhizhe-jiujing-chaju-zai-nali-lphee8cj-7gcmg-cynpiphjvauuf|Hanji 956]] pattern where officials avoid direct contradiction under [[DongZhuo|董卓]] by redirecting speech into public legitimacy, timing, or the tyrant's own declared values.

[[HuangfuSongLateHan|皇甫嵩]] answers Dong Zhuo's fear question by making fear depend on whether Dong Zhuo assists the court with virtue or abuses punishment. [[CaiYong|蔡邕]] responds to Dong Zhuo's honorific ambition by recognizing the stated merit but delaying the title until the Guandong rebellion is pacified.

## Key Claims

- Under coercive power, blunt refusal may be lethal, so effective speech often works through condition, timing, and the ruler's self-image.
- The method is defensive, not proof of institutional health; it preserves the speaker and may slow harm without controlling the ruler.
- It differs from open remonstrance because the immediate goal is survival and narrowing the tyrant's move rather than direct moral confrontation.
