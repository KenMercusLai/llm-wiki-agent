## Definition

Power-structure targeting / 权力结构定向求援 is the strategy of solving a blocked institutional problem by identifying the actor whose role, access, relationship, or legitimacy can actually change the decision, rather than spending effort on broad but low-leverage social contact or expressive protest.

## Current Synthesis

[[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-876-zhichang-zong-pengbi-shuoming-ni-meiyou-zhaodui-guanxi-lrjob-a07t29hdr0iogrmcsjr7qp|Hanji 876]] creates this concept through [[JiaBiaoLateHan|贾彪]]'s rescue strategy during the first Party Prohibition prison crisis. After [[ChenFanLateHan|陈蕃]] is removed and officials become afraid to plead for [[LiYingLateHan|李膺]], [[FanPangLateHan|范滂]], and other prisoners, Jia Biao does not escalate through public performance. He goes to [[LuoyangLateHan|洛阳]] and targets [[DouWuLateHan|窦武]], who has better access to [[EmperorHuanOfHan|汉桓帝]] because of family status and office position.

The source's workplace opening says that low-quality relationships are costly, but the durable concept is not anti-social withdrawal. It is relationship discrimination under pressure: first map who can move the outcome, then approach through a channel that fits that person's leverage and risk. Jia Biao's work is effective because it converts the party-prisoner problem from isolated moral concern into a memorial that the emperor cannot ignore as easily.

## Key Claims

- Not all relationships are equally useful in a crisis; leverage depends on position, access, credibility, and decision proximity.
- Public protest can be morally expressive while still being weaker than a targeted channel when the decision-maker is insulated.
- A blocked case often requires changing who carries the argument, not only sharpening the argument itself.
- Effective intercession may be low-visibility: the connector can matter even when another actor signs the memorial.
- Power-structure targeting remains ethically qualified because it can solve rescue problems but can also become access manipulation in other contexts.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications

This concept does not claim that only elite relationships matter or that public action is useless. It names a source-bounded pattern where open pleading has become dangerous and a better-positioned intercessor can still move the case. The same mechanism can become morally ambiguous when used for private brokerage, nepotism, or accusation access rather than rescue.

## What Changed

- Hanji 876 creates the concept from Jia Biao's targeted approach to Dou Wu in the first Party Prohibition rescue path.
