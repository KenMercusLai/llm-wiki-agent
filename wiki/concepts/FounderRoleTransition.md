## Definition
Founder role transition is the change from personally carrying most tasks to a bounded contribution—CEO, product or creative lead, board member, adviser, specialist, or former owner—as the organization, capital needs and founder's own capacity change. It is not always a one-way CEO handoff.

## Current Synthesis
The cases distinguish delegation of tasks, hiring operating leadership, stepping out of the CEO role, sale with continued employment, and full exit. The best fit depends on which work the founder does uniquely well, which operating capabilities the next stage needs, and who controls financing and succession. A handoff can be reversible; a title can remain while real authority shifts. These are founders' interview accounts, not a comparative measure of successor performance.

## Key Claims
- Early founder breadth supplies product and channel learning but eventually makes the founder a capacity constraint.
- A founder can retain taste, customer understanding or technical judgment while transferring repeatable operations to specialists.
- Capital-intensive growth and ownership terms can change the founder's role independently of personal preference.
- CEO succession requires an actual decision boundary; founder shadow-control can defeat a nominal handoff.
- The transition may run in reverse when a technical founder must become CEO or a founder returns after an operator-led period.
- Burnout, risk appetite and stage-fit can justify an exit into a different institution or a narrower role.

## Evidence
- Delegating operational breadth: [[justins-nut-butter-justin-gold-he-was-waiting-tables-then-he-reinvented-peanut-butter-b456c4b4-0243-4e46-9460-d0de7cf5d98b]] recounts [[JustinGold]] formulating, manufacturing, delivering and demoing [[JustinsNutButter]], then working through [[LanceGentry]], [[VMG]] and CEO [[PeterBurns]]; after the reported $280 million 2016 [[Hormel]] acquisition, he stayed about five years, left in 2021 and later returned following [[ForwardConsumerPartners]]' controlling investment. [[advice-line-with-carlton-calvin-of-razor-c0de1a84-3d35-45f3-921c-6144693ad86e]] has [[CarltonCalvin]] regret personally selling [[RazorUSA]] products to save sales-rep margin: delegating [[CPGDistribution]] to sales reps changes which channel work the founder owns, without proving a CEO handoff. [[catalina-crunch-krishna-kaliannan-from-homemade-keto-cocoa-puffs-to-breakfast-aisle-breakthrough-5aa86015-e7c2-448e-a84e-d1b34d2fcac2]] has [[KrishnaKaliannan]] shift from home formula and overnight packaging and shipping toward nutrition, protein, fiber and sugar after [[CatalinaCrunch]] appointed [[DougBehrens]] CEO in 2024; its competing $100 million brand and $200 million sales language is not treated as a verified scale metric.
- Specialized founder contribution: [[sun-bum-tom-rinks-the-secrets-of-a-master-brand-builder-2023-77ab41eb-99a8-4dd2-ae39-d066818afc21]] has [[TomRinks]] recruit [[AdamFrancis]] as [[SunBum]] CEO because margins, production and finance were unlike his brand/design strengths; [[SCJohnson]]'s reported 2019 purchase is linked to international sunscreen compliance and scale. [[advice-line-with-christina-tosi-of-milk-bar-fd7f2406-fda8-4c1d-936c-d20210215f96]] has [[ChristinaTosi]] explain that [[MilkBar]] benefits more from her culinary and creative work than from her remaining CEO. [[starr-restaurants-stephen-starr-how-a-non-foodie-built-thriving-restaurants-on-gut-instinct-6c0eb387-4722-4284-aded-23bbd0a9e6f4]] has [[StephenStarr]] call himself [[STARRRestaurants]]' “executive producer,” assembling chefs, designers and operators around concept, atmosphere and site; the [[TheContinental]]/Cafe Republic contrast also limits the transferability of taste alone.
- Scale, financing and control: [[e-l-f-cosmetics-joey-shamah-the-dollar-store-formula-that-built-a-cosmetics-giant-bbc71e65-9e47-41cb-8dd4-c61fcfc2572e]] describes [[JoeyShamah]]'s hands-on response to a 2006 viral order spike, reportedly shipping 192,000 orders through China. [[TSGConsumerPartners]]' reported 49% minority investment at about $70 million valuation retained family control; [[TPG]]'s later majority purchase at roughly $265 million installed [[TarangAmin]] as CEO, and Joey and his father left in December 2015 before the 2016 public listing. [[serena-lily-serena-dugan-and-lily-kanter-they-built-a-20m-brand-then-one-investor-almost-destroyed-it-31a4ddcd-1cc1-48d0-9369-fd85e8714e66]] reports [[LilyKanter]]'s CEO search and end-2015 exit after [[SerenaAndLily|Serena & Lily]]'s inventory financing, investor litigation and 2x participating preference; [[SerenaDugan]] moved from creative lead to external part-time work about six months later, before both exited fully. The founders' account of investor conduct and returns is not an independently adjudicated judgment. [[ugg-brian-smith-how-an-epiphany-surfers-and-500-launched-an-iconic-sheepskin-footwear-company-56230048-59d6-43c2-b386-7d06ea89f939]] has [[BrianSmith]] move from door-to-door [[UGG]] sales into commissioned selling and consulting after a reported sub-$15 million 1995 sale to [[Deckers]]: rising seasonal orders outran inventory financing.
- Genuine succession and its limits: [[build-a-bear-maxine-clark-a-former-shoe-executive-launches-a-stuffed-animal-empire-8e5da7d7-4e2b-4978-b625-f72cb5d57d88]] has [[MaxineClark]] step down as [[BuildABear]] CEO in June 2013 and learn to let [[SharonPriceJohn]] make decisions she would not have chosen, rather than remain a shadow CEO. [[yeti-ron-and-ryan-seiders-how-two-brothers-turned-a-400-cooler-into-a-2-billion-brand-d7f8e0e8-51a5-4444-8c1d-f5c43c353285]] says [[RoySeiders]] left the [[YETI]] CEO seat and [[RyanSeiders]] the president seat in 2015, remaining involved after [[CortecGroup]]'s reported 2012 two-thirds stake for about $70 million and the [[YETIRambler]] expansion. The episode title says “Ron,” but body identifies Roy. [[advice-line-with-curt-richardson-of-otterbox-e94bf774-de11-4434-843b-a551309a6773]] has [[CurtRichardson]] say that by 2019 [[OtterBox]] had outgrown his startup skill set; several CEOs and a return to mobile-accessory focus followed, with profitability and peak-sales claims limited to his account.
- Reversal and personal fit: [[shopify-tobias-lutke-how-a-snowboarder-built-a-150-billion-business-2019-d0b07b6a-125e-4896-babd-678d19957306]] has programmer [[TobiasLutke]] take the [[Shopify]] CEO role after [[ScottLake]] left around 2008, deliberately moderating growth while learning HR and management—transition toward, not away from, CEO. [[tsr-s3-ryanpetersen-v6-tsr-s3-ryanpetersen-v6]] has [[RyanPetersen]] recruit [[DaveClark]] from [[Amazon]] for repeatable [[Flexport]] operations, then return at the board's request to reset budget and operating discipline; this is Petersen's perspective on the handoff. [[tsr-s5-tomblomfield-v2-audio-tsr-s5-tomblomfield-v2-audio]] has [[TomBlomfield]] step back from [[Monzo]] after regulated-bank financing and burnout, with [[TSAnil]] becoming CEO; after recovery he moved to [[YCombinator]] as a partner with institutional legal and finance support. [[50-cents-a-pool-the-pricing-model-behind-a-saas-exit]] has [[RonHash]] sell [[Skimmer]] after reporting more than $1 million ARR and 1,500 customers because he preferred building a product to building the next stage's HR, values and management infrastructure; [[QuickFax]] illustrates his later narrower product work.

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
Not every founder should leave CEO: Lütke's progression and Petersen's return run against a linear professional-manager narrative. Financial pressure may force a transition rather than demonstrate superior leadership. Company revenue, valuations, deal terms and post-handoff performance above are guest-reported, not independently verified. [[FounderSuccession]] concerns authority transfer specifically; [[StageAppropriateHiring]] covers role fit before any formal handoff.

## What Changed
- Distinguished task delegation, operating handoff, equity sale, return and exit rather than treating all as the same succession.
- Retained cross-industry case actors, reported figures, timing and financing constraints while attributing interview claims.
- Set explicit limits on whether a handoff improved company performance.
