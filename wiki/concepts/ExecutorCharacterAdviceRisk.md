# Executor Character Advice Risk

Executor character advice risk is the danger that advice can be tactically sound in abstraction but destructive when executed by violent, undisciplined, or politically unfit actors. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-967-ta-canyu-mousha-dongzhuo-jingneng-taoguo-sishen-weilie-lv24biyswj9ljdld5htynhauhph3|Hanji 967]] gives the pattern through [[JiaXu|Jia Xu]]: his advice to [[LiJueLateHan|Li Jue]], [[GuoSiLateHan|Guo Si]], and other [[DongZhuo|Dong Zhuo]] remnant commanders not to disband but to counterattack [[ChangAnHanCapital|Chang'an]] solves their immediate survival problem, then unleashes plunder and court violence.

The host treats Jia Xu's later refusal of reward and lower-profile conduct as evidence that he recognized the moral cost of the advice. Whether that interior remorse is accepted or not, the structural point remains: an adviser is responsible for estimating not only whether a plan can work, but who will execute it and what they are likely to do with success.

## Pattern

- A plan is proposed to prevent immediate collapse or death.
- The plan depends on executors whose incentives and character are poorly constrained.
- Success gives those executors access to coercive power.
- The resulting harm exceeds the adviser's stated intention.
- Later repair is possible but cannot erase the earlier release of destructive capacity.
