# Coalition Self-Preservation Failure / 联盟自保失灵

## Definition
Coalition self-preservation failure occurs when participants facing a common threat decline to bear the immediate military, logistical or political costs of joint action, even where another member's defeat may later expose them all.

## Current Synthesis
The 318 BCE anti-[[QinState|Qin]] five-state force reached [[HanguPass|函谷关]] under [[ChuHuaiwang|楚怀王]] of [[ChuState|Chu]], yet members waited for someone else to fight; the episode says Qi's [[Mengchangjun|孟尝君]] moved slowly, Qin cut Chu's grain route and withdrawal cascaded. [[HanState|Han]] and [[WeiState|Wei]] then suffered Qin pursuit at [[XiuyuBattle|修鱼]] and [[ZhuozeBattle|浊泽]]. In the late-Han [[AntiDongZhuoCoalition|coalition against]] [[DongZhuo|Dong Zhuo]], [[YuanShao|袁绍]] exemplified [[PrestigeBasedCoalitionLeadership|prestige-based leadership]] without binding command or unified supply; [[CaoCao|曹操]]'s isolated [[BianshuiBattleLateHan|Bianshui]] defeat and rejected pass-control plan preceded the [[SuanzaoCamp|酸枣]] camp's grain-driven dispersal. Other cases distinguish withholding grain at [[ChangpingBattle|Changping]], halting an already-mobilized rescue of [[Handan|Handan]] at 邺, conditional participation over political refugees, and waiting until [[XiangYu|项羽]] broke Qin at 巨鹿. Each is a different version of misaligned commitments, not proof of one universal motive.

## Key Claims
- A named leader and shared enemy do not make hierarchy, food supply and risk-sharing binding; the first supply shock can produce cascading retreat.
- Alliance failure can leave front-line participants exposed to retaliation after the wider coalition withdraws.
- Refusing food, interpreting a neighbor's danger as a gain, or stopping a relief army short of battle can fail a buffer state without a formal alliance collapse.
- Coercive activation can deliver aid at moral and institutional cost; appeasement and private-gain diplomacy offer distinct, not identical, alternatives to battle.
- Political refugee and succession disputes can block joint operations, while late entry after another actor's decisive victory is not the same as a wholly failed campaign.

- [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-950-budong-quanli-beihou-de-renxing-bi-bai-wuyi-lu8-u78yqvvwwarsz8dlevvzchxr]] reports [[ZhengTaiLateHan|郑泰]]'s warning about self-appointed commanders, divided food routes and Yuan Shao's prestige leadership. [[zizhi-tongjian-hanji-953-luanshi-zhizhong-yaobuyao-jiangjiu-renyi-lrzfimgqatjxyw9zx9ry2kiwxkon]] follows Cao Cao's Xu Rong/Bianshui defeat, his proposed Chenggao/敖仓/孟津 [[StrategicPassControl|pass-control]] containment, coalition inaction and 酸枣粮尽.
### Relief withheld or forced
- [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-94-shashen-baiqi-changping-zhi-zhan-zhong-de-caobao-mingjiang-2-lvqctrazn8ddkcnp-lhspzzmzmns]] says Qi refused Zhao's grain appeal during [[ChangpingBattle|长平]]; the note names [[QiWangJian|齐王建]] and [[JunWangHou|君王后]] in that refusal, while [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-95-changping-zhi-zhan-hou-qinguo-weishenme-meiyou-mie-le-zhaoguo-2-lutsvwvnwzsezoorhe-vu7yjthyx]] shows [[YuQing|虞卿]]'s uncertain Zhao-Qi land-for-support proposal and [[WeiAnxiWang|魏安釐王]]'s court initially reading Zhao's peril as advantage until [[KongBin|孔斌]] warned otherwise.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-2-ltlobdujy-hdipimfdupvwod2ech]] says Wei sent [[JinBi|晋鄙]] with a reported 100,000 troops, then stopped at [[YeWarringStates|邺]] after [[QinZhaoxiangwang|秦昭襄王]]'s threat, leaving Chu's agreed relief dependent on Wei transit ([[CoalitionTransitDependency]]). [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-3-lj-cr4vg4e1qdfjs6yez85-3zuiv]] presents [[LuZhonglian|鲁仲连]]'s rebuttal of [[XinyuanYan|新垣衍]]'s [[SubmissionThroughStatusElevation|proposal to honor Qin as emperor]]; [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-96-maosui-zijian-6-lteqe-0ykjtv01cmxv1fhx6z0vdi]] follows [[Xinlingjun|信陵君]]'s [[TigerTallyCommandSystem|stolen tiger tally]] on [[HouYing|侯嬴]]'s advice, killing of Jin Bi and relief of 邯郸.
### Bargaining, feuds and delayed joining
- [[zizhi-tongjian-zhouji-50-zhangyi-shi-zenyang-ba-chuhuaiwang-huyou-que-de-loxg3ylhmskvdm-axtco5y-acege]] has [[ZhangYiStrategist|张仪]] promise 600 li of [[Shangyu|商於]] land to separate Chu from Qi before reducing the offer to six li: an [[UnverifiedTerritorialPromiseTrap|unverified promise]], not reluctance to attack first. [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-130-3-weihe-li-mutong-wei-chuwang-limian-dayou-wenzhang-lmcuvrxer-cou6gmlppqwhb3n4vz]] says [[TianRong|田荣]] demanded Chu and Zhao execute [[TianJia|田假]], [[TianJiao|田角]] and [[TianJianQiGeneral|田间]] before joining [[XiangLiang|项梁]] against [[ZhangHanQin|章邯]], which both states refused.
- [[zizhi-tongjian-qinji-134-ni-buzhidao-de-pofu-chenzhou-banben-ltnry1fva2hzplw4ft8nrfpoiolw]] describes [[ChenYu|陈馀]]'s hesitation, other lords watching while [[WangLiQin|王离]] besieged Zhao from their camps and their submission after Xiang Yu broke the siege at [[JuluBattle|巨鹿]].

## Counterevidence & Qualifications
- All twelve historical notes belong to one program's narrative, not twelve independent historical confirmations. Attributed numerical totals, inferred intentions and moral evaluations remain the host's interpretation.
- Julu ended in victory and later participation, not comprehensive coalition defeat. 信陵君's successful rescue required stolen authority and 晋鄙's death; neither makes the original institutions work well.
- Lu Zhonglian's “civilization/barbarism” rhetoric and historical precedents are contested by the host; it is an argument against submission, not neutral proof. 张仪's deception and 田荣's refugee feud are distinct from simply fearing first contact. This 战国楚怀王 is not the later 芈心.
